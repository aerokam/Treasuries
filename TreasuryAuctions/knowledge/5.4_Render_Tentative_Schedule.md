# 5.4 Render Tentative Schedule

**Specifies:** [5.4 Render Tentative Schedule](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TREASURYAUCTIONS)
**Implemented by:** `TreasuryAuctions/src/app.js` (`renderTentative`, `renderTentBody`, `tentWeekKey`), `TreasuryAuctions/index.html`
**Input:** the [Tentative Auction Schedule](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tentative-auction-schedule) from [Load auction data (5.1)](./5.1_Load_Auction_Data.md); [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) from the user
**Output:** [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables) to the user

Draw the Tentative Schedule tab from the [Tentative auction schedule (S9)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s9) rows.

<a id="tentative-schedule"></a>

- Show the rows of S9 whose floating rate flag is "No", in auction date order, with the columns Auction Date, Term, Type, Reopen, TIPS, Announcement Date and Settlement Date. Show the three dates as mm/dd/yyyy. The Type is the `SecurityType` S9 states.
- Show a TIPS flag of "Yes" in bold green.
- Alternate the row shading at each change of auction date.
- Apply the [typed column filters](./5.2_Render_Historical_Auctions.md#typed-filters) and the [sort](./5.2_Render_Historical_Auctions.md#sorting) of [Render Historical Auctions (5.2)](./5.2_Render_Historical_Auctions.md).
- Leave the row count of the tab bar blank.
- When S9 did not load or has no rows, the tab reads "No tentative schedule data available."
