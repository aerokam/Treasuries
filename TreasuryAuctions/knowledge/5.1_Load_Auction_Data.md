# 5.1 Load auction data

**Specifies:** [5.1 Load auction data](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TREASURYAUCTIONS)
**Implemented by:** `TreasuryAuctions/src/app.js` (`loadData`, `upcomingUrl`, `parseCSV`)
**Input:** [Auction results (S5)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s5) and [Tentative auction schedule (S9)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s9), from R2; the [Upcoming Auctions](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#upcoming-auctions) of [FiscalData (E3)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e3)
**Output:** [Auction Results](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#auction-results) to [Render Historical Auctions (5.2)](./5.2_Render_Historical_Auctions.md) and [Render Upcoming Auctions (5.3)](./5.3_Render_Upcoming_Auctions.md); [Upcoming Auctions](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#upcoming-auctions) to [Render Upcoming Auctions (5.3)](./5.3_Render_Upcoming_Auctions.md); the [Tentative Auction Schedule](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tentative-auction-schedule) to [Render Upcoming Auctions (5.3)](./5.3_Render_Upcoming_Auctions.md) and [Render Tentative Schedule (5.4)](./5.4_Render_Tentative_Schedule.md)

Fetch the three sources and pass each to the processes that draw it.

<a id="load"></a>
When the page opens and when the user clicks Refresh:

- Fetch three sources in parallel, each with the browser cache bypassed:
  - [Auction results (S5)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s5), from its public R2 URL.
  - The [Upcoming Auctions](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#upcoming-auctions), from the `upcoming_auctions_v2` endpoint of [FiscalData (E3)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e3): the fields `cusip`, `announcemt_date`, `comp_auction_close_date`, `issue_date`, `security_term`, `security_type` and `reopening`, for `comp_auction_close_date` on or after the current UTC date, sorted by `announcemt_date` and then `comp_auction_close_date`.
  - [Tentative auction schedule (S9)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s9), from its public R2 URL.
- Read each `AuctionCalendarDate` node of S9 as one schedule row:
  - Keep `AuctionDate`, `SecurityTermWeekYear`, `SecurityType`, `AnnouncementDate` and `SettlementDate` as stated.
  - Set Reopen, TIPS and the floating rate flag to "Yes" where `ReOpeningIndicator`, `TIPS` or `FloatingRate` is `Y`, and to "No" otherwise.
  - Drop a node with a blank `AuctionDate`.
  - Sort the rows by auction date, ascending. A parse error leaves the schedule empty.
- Read S5 under its header row, so the columns of the Historical Auctions tab are the columns of S5.
- Pass the S5 rows to [Render Historical Auctions (5.2)](./5.2_Render_Historical_Auctions.md), then the S5 rows and the upcoming auctions to [Render Upcoming Auctions (5.3)](./5.3_Render_Upcoming_Auctions.md). Pass the schedule rows to [Render Upcoming Auctions (5.3)](./5.3_Render_Upcoming_Auctions.md) and [Render Tentative Schedule (5.4)](./5.4_Render_Tentative_Schedule.md).
- When S5 does not load, show the status "Failed to load auction data: " and the reason, and pass nothing further.
- When the upcoming auctions do not load, pass none to [Render Upcoming Auctions (5.3)](./5.3_Render_Upcoming_Auctions.md). When S9 does not load, pass no schedule rows.
- Show the status "Updated: " and the local time when the load completes.
