# CPI Explorer (App Overview)

**CPI Explorer** charts U.S. Consumer Price Index data from three series: the monthly [CPI-U NSA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#cpi-nsa), the monthly [CPI-U SA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#cpi-sa) and the daily [Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi). The user selects any combination of the series to overlay on one chart, one of five display modes, and a date range. A logarithmic vertical axis is available for the index level.

---

## Data flow diagram

CPI Explorer is process 7 on [Level 1](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL1). It reads [CPI history (S8)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s8) and [Ref CPI (S3)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s3) from R2 when the page opens. [Fetch CPI history (1.12)](../../knowledge/1.12_Fetch_CPI_History.md) writes S8 and [Fetch daily Ref CPI (1.13)](../../knowledge/1.13_Fetch_Daily_Ref_CPI.md) writes S3.
