# 2.5 Render time series

**Constrained by:** [Visual Standards](./Visual_Standards.md)
**Implemented by:** `YieldsMonitor/src/app.js#updateCharts`, `#createChartInstance`, `#updateDynamicTicks`, `#syncChartContainers`, `#applyDefaultBounds`, `#captureChartViews`, `#restoreChartViews`; `YieldsMonitor/src/custom-series.js#customBounds`; `shared/src/chart-time-axis.js#getXTimeUnit`; `YieldsMonitor/src/trading-time.js`
**Input:** [Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield-series), from [Assemble range data (2.1)](./2.1_Assemble_Range_Data.md); [Live Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#live-quotes), from [Read live quotes (2.2)](./2.2_Read_Live_Quotes.md); the current Yield and [Day Change](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#day-change), from [Calculate day change (2.3)](./2.3_Calculate_Day_Change.md); [SA Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield-series), from [Adjust for seasonality (2.4)](./2.4_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md); [Bond Holiday](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), from [Bond holidays (S16)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s16)
**Output:** [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables), to the user

Read the [Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield-series), the [Live Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#live-quotes), the current [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) and [Day Change](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#day-change), the [SA Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield-series) and the [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), and write the [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables) of the Time Series tab: one chart per checked symbol, and the sidebar readings next to every symbol.

## Layout

Charts are grouped into a TIPS row and a Treasury row, each sorted by maturity. A chart appears only for a checked symbol; unchecking one removes its chart.

## Sidebar symbol list

Every symbol shows its reading, checked or not: data is read for all symbols on every update, and only the charts depend on the selection. Each group of rows has a header row naming its columns, Term, Yield, Change and Updated:

- Yield: the current Yield of [Calculate day change (2.3)](./2.3_Calculate_Day_Change.md) for that symbol.
- Day Change: the Day Change of [Calculate day change (2.3)](./2.3_Calculate_Day_Change.md), green when zero or positive and red when negative, with a tooltip stating the reference it was calculated against.
- Updated: the time of the symbol's latest plotted point, `HH:mm` when that point is today and `MM/DD HH:mm` otherwise. It differs by symbol because CNBC publishes a symbol's bar only for a minute in which it quotes, so symbols checked at the same moment show different times.

## Time range selection

The sidebar offers preset range buttons — 2D, 10D, 1Y, 2Y, 3Y, 10Y, ALL — and a Custom option:

- A Custom range reveals a start-date and an end-date picker and an Apply button.
- Applying reads [Assemble Custom range data (2.1.4)](./2.1_Assemble_Range_Data.md#assemble-custom-range-data) for the picked dates. Each chart reads the second Yield Series of 2.1.4, which has one-minute bars wherever they exist.
- The chart's default horizontal bounds run from the first point of its series to one minute past the last, so the plot has no empty edge at either end of the picked window. The latest point's marker may extend 8 px past the plot area's right edge, so it is drawn whole.

## Refresh Data

Refresh Data keeps each chart's zoom, where a chart still at its default bounds is reset as usual because the new default bounds already cover the new data.

- A zoomed chart keeps its horizontal window. If the window's right edge was at or beyond the chart's latest point, the window moves forward by the extent of the new data, keeping its width, so the latest point stays visible. Any other window is kept as it was.
- With Sync Zoom & Pan on, every chart slides by the same amount, the largest new-data extent among the charts.
- A chart whose vertical axis was zoomed manually keeps that zoom; any other chart's vertical axis is refit to the visible points.
- Changing the range or the picked dates resets the zoom, since the new window shows different dates.
- Checking or unchecking a symbol, and Hide non-trading hours, keep every chart's zoom, vertical axis included; a chart added while Sync Zoom & Pan is on is given the window of the other charts. A zoom changes only by a zoom or pan of the user, or by Reset All Zoom.

## X-axis tick density and labels

- Every range: the unit updates on every zoom or pan, at a threshold measured from the visible span rather than the preset range itself, so the labels always match the resolution zoomed into — minutes below 6 hours visible, hourly from 6 hours up to 3 days, daily up to 90 days, monthly from 90 days up to about 18 months, yearly beyond that.
  - Below 3 days visible: ticks labeled `MM/DD HH:mm` (e.g. `04/28 14:30`).
  - From 3 days up to 90 days visible: ticks labeled `MMM D` (e.g. `May 1`).
  - From 90 days visible onward, at every coarser unit including the yearly one: ticks labeled `MMM YYYY` (e.g. `May 2025`): the yearly unit widens the gap between ticks.
  - In the `MMM D` and `MMM YYYY` labels, a tick whose label repeats the label of the previous tick is left blank.
  - Tick spacing follows the visible span in steps: below 90 days visible, ticks fall at the smallest of the round intervals (1, 2, 5, 10, 15 and 30 minutes; 1, 2, 3, 6 and 12 hours; 1, 2, 5, 10, 15 and 30 days) that leaves at most as many ticks as the chart is wide enough to label, between 5 and 10, aligned to round ET times. A zoom therefore changes the spacing a step at a time, never from hourly to every few minutes in one click.
  - With Hide non-trading hours on, the visible span is the drawn width, each trading session counting as one day, and minute and hour ticks fall inside trading hours only.

## Tooltip time

CNBC delivers the 1Y, 2Y, 3Y, 10Y and ALL ranges as daily bars that carry a date and no time of day, and cnbc.com shows only the date for them. The tooltip on those ranges states the date alone, `MM/DD/YY`. The 2D, 10D and Custom ranges state date and time in ET.

## Intraday visual standards (2D, 10D and Custom ranges only)

- After-hours (before 08:00 and from 17:00 ET) is shaded on every trading day.
- A full Saturday or Sunday is shaded the same way.
- If the data of a trading day spans 08:00 or 17:00 ET, a dashed vertical line marks whichever boundary it spans.
- The plotted line itself is drawn in a lighter shade of its color across any segment falling in after-hours or a weekend, to keep the primary trading session visually dominant.
  - A segment whose two points both fall within trading hours (08:00 to 17:05 ET on a weekday) is not after-hours data and keeps the full color, so a line at daily resolution is never lightened.

None of this shading, boundary marking, or reduced-opacity segment applies outside 2D, 10D and Custom: every other range shows only the plain line.

## Hide non-trading hours (2D, 10D and Custom ranges only)

When the Hide non-trading hours checkbox (see [Visual Standards](./Visual_Standards.md#hide-non-trading-hours)) is on:

- Trading hours are 08:00 to 17:05 ET on a weekday that is not a [Bond Holiday](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday); the 17:05 end is the final bar CNBC provides for the day.
- Read the [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday) before the first chart is drawn, whether or not the Seasonally Adjusted checkbox is on.
- The horizontal axis shows trading hours only, laid end to end: the hours before 08:00, from 17:05 on, weekends and Bond Holidays take no width, and a point outside trading hours is not drawn.
- A narrow gray vertical bar separates one trading day from the next, including Friday from Monday and the day before a Bond Holiday from the day after it. The line between two points on either side of it crosses the bar.
- The after-hours and weekend shading and the dashed 08:00 and 17:00 lines described above are not drawn.
- The vertical axis fits the points drawn.
- Tick labels, tooltips and the Sync Zoom & Pan window keep their real ET times; only the horizontal spacing differs.
- A weekend or a Bond Holiday takes no width, so a daily tick for a Saturday, a Sunday or a Bond Holiday is not drawn: it would sit on the close of the preceding trading day, over the neighboring labels.
- On the Custom range, a day that has only a daily point (see [Assemble range data (2.1)](./2.1_Assemble_Range_Data.md)) keeps it: its time of day is that of the stored daily point, inside trading hours, so it is not a non-trading point.
- A pan moves the horizontal window by the width dragged on the drawn axis, so a weekend or a Bond Holiday that enters or leaves the window does not change the number of trading sessions it shows.
- Checking or unchecking it keeps each chart's zoom: the window is in real ET times, so it holds on either axis.

## The line to the current time for a stale symbol

When the quote time of the [Live Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#live-quotes) of [Read live quotes (2.2)](./2.2_Read_Live_Quotes.md) for a symbol is more than thirty minutes later than the last plotted point of its chart, because the feed fell back to an archived day or stopped updating:

- Draw a dashed horizontal line from the last point to the current time, at the Yield of the last point. No intraday data exists for that stretch, so the line shows elapsed time and not a Yield path.
- Extend the default horizontal bounds of the chart to the current time, so the dashed line is visible without a pan or zoom.

This applies to 2D and 10D only.

## SA overlay

For each of the five TIPS symbols: the raw (Quoted) Yield line is drawn first, the SA Yield line second, in a single distinguishing color rather than a dashed style, labeled "SA Yield" against "Yield" in the tooltip. Unchecking either "Quoted" or "SA" in the sidebar toggle (see [Adjust for seasonality (2.4)](./2.4_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md)) empties that chart's corresponding line rather than removing the chart; the Y-axis auto-rescale fits to whichever line or lines remain shown.
