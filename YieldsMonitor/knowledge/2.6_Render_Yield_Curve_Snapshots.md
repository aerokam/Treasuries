# 2.6 Render yield curve snapshots

**Specifies:** [2.6 Render yield curve snapshots](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_YIELDSMONITOR)
**Constrained by:** [Visual Standards](./Visual_Standards.md)
**Implemented by:** `YieldsMonitor/src/app.js#updateYieldCurves`, `#valueOnDate`
**Input:** [Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield-series), from [Assemble range data (2.1)](./2.1_Assemble_Range_Data.md); [SA Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield-series), from [Adjust for seasonality (2.4)](./2.4_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md)
**Output:** [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables), to the user

Read the [Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield-series) and the [SA Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield-series) of every symbol, and write the [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables) of the Yield Curves tab: a TIPS curve and a Nominal curve, each with the [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) of every maturity on the start date of the active range and on its end date.

## Dependencies

- [Visual Standards](./Visual_Standards.md) — the pan and zoom rules this chart follows
- [DATA_DICTIONARY.md](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md) — [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield)
- [Assemble range data (2.1)](./2.1_Assemble_Range_Data.md), [Adjust for seasonality (2.4)](./2.4_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) — supply every figure drawn here
- [Render breakeven inflation (2.7)](./2.7_Render_Breakeven_Inflation.md) — uses the start and end dates and their labels of this process

<a id="snapshot-dates"></a>
## Snapshot dates

- Select one anchor symbol, used also by [Render breakeven inflation (2.7)](./2.7_Render_Breakeven_Inflation.md), so that all three curves compare the same two dates.
  - The anchor is the first of 10-Year TIPS, 1-Year TIPS, 30-Year TIPS and 2-Year TIPS that has a Yield Series with a point for the active range.
  - 5-Year TIPS is not an anchor, because its feed has sparse and irregular bars ([Assemble range data (2.1)](./2.1_Assemble_Range_Data.md)).
  - If no anchor symbol has a point, the curves have no start and end dates and their labels are "—".
- The start date is the date of the first point of the anchor Yield Series and the end date is the date of its last point. The Yield Series ends at the window of the active range, so for the Custom range a picked start date with no point moves to the next date that has one, and a picked end date moves to the previous date that has one.

## Per maturity

For each maturity, on the TIPS curve and on the Nominal curve:

- Read the Yield on the start date: that date's first point.
- Read the Yield on the end date: that date's last point.
- If a maturity has no point on the date, leave a gap. No value from another date is used.
  - The chart draws a line across the gap, in a lighter shade of the same color and dash as the rest of the line.

<a id="labeling"></a>
## Labeling

The start dataset is labeled with the Eastern Time timestamp of the first point of the anchor Yield Series, and the end dataset with that of its last point, each followed by "ET". A first point stamped 00:00 is labeled 17:00. The labels are the same on the TIPS curve, the Nominal curve and the curve of [Render breakeven inflation (2.7)](./2.7_Render_Breakeven_Inflation.md).

## SA overlay

When the Seasonally Adjusted checkbox is on ([Adjust for seasonality (2.4)](./2.4_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md)), the TIPS curve gains a start dataset and an end dataset, labeled with "(SA)", at all five TIPS maturities. They are the first and the last point of the [SA Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield-series) of [Adjust for seasonality (2.4)](./2.4_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) on the same two dates as the Yield datasets.

- A maturity or date with no SA Yield is left a gap, drawn as a gap of the Yield datasets is. The SA Yield of an end date of today exists, because the TIPS of the current date comes from the Live Quotes and not from the rollover log.
- Unchecking the Quoted checkbox empties the Yield datasets of the TIPS curve and leaves its SA datasets. The Nominal curve has no SA datasets and does not depend on either checkbox.
- The legend lists the SA datasets while the Seasonally Adjusted checkbox is on, and the Yield datasets of the TIPS curve while the Quoted checkbox is on.
- The TIPS curve keeps its SA datasets, empty while the checkbox is off, so a change of checkbox updates its data and does not rebuild the chart.
