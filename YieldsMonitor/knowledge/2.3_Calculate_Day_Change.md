# 2.3 Calculate day change

**Specifies:** [2.3 Calculate day change](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_YIELDSMONITOR)
**Implemented by:** `YieldsMonitor/src/app.js#updateCharts` (the closeP walk)
**Input:** [Market Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#market-yields) and [Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield-series), from [Assemble range data (2.1)](./2.1_Assemble_Range_Data.md); [Live Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#live-quotes), from [Read live quotes (2.2)](./2.2_Read_Live_Quotes.md); [Bond Holiday](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), from [Bond holidays (S16)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s16)
**Output:** the current [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) and the [Day Change](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#day-change) per symbol, to [Render time series (2.5)](./2.5_Render_Time_Series.md)

<a id="calculate-day-change"></a>
Read the [Live Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#live-quotes), the [Market Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#market-yields) read by [Assemble range data (2.1)](./2.1_Assemble_Range_Data.md) and the [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), and write the current [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) and the [Day Change](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#day-change) of each symbol:

- Select the Market Yields of the symbol: the `5D` `TimeRange` read by 2.1 if there is one, otherwise the `1D` `TimeRange` read by 2.1, otherwise the [Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield-series) of the symbol.
- Write the current Yield: the Yield of the [Live Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#live-quotes) of the symbol, otherwise the Yield of the last point of the selected Market Yields.
  - If neither exists, the symbol has no current Yield and no Day Change.
- Find the prior close:
  - Drop the points dated a Saturday, a Sunday or a [Bond Holiday](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday). A Bond Holiday is dropped only once the Bond Holidays are loaded.
  - Find the latest date among the remaining points. The prior close is the last point dated earlier than that date and timestamped 17:05 Eastern time or before.
- Write the Day Change: the current Yield less the Yield of the prior close.
  - If no point qualifies as the prior close, the Day Change is the current Yield less the previous day's closing Yield of the [Live Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#live-quotes).
  - If that is also missing, the symbol has no Day Change.
  - The sidebar states which reference applies: the time and Yield of the prior close, or the previous day's closing Yield of the Live Quotes.

## Why the prior close is not the previous day's closing Yield

The Day Change is stated against the 17:05 Eastern-time session close ([Close Price Investigation](./Close_Price_Investigation.md)). The previous day's closing Yield of the Live Quotes is on the basis of the daily close of the chart-bar feed, the mid-afternoon (about 3 PM Eastern-time) benchmark, so the two references fall at different points of the trading day. The previous day's closing Yield is used only when no point qualifies.

## Why the `5D` `TimeRange` is preferred

The `1D` `TimeRange` begins two days before the request (measured 2026-10-16), so over a weekend it can lack the session of the previous trading day, and the prior close would be missing. The `5D` `TimeRange` begins ten days before.
