# 2.1 Assemble range data

**Specifies:** [2.1 Assemble range data](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_YM_ASSEMBLE)
**Implemented by:** `YieldsMonitor/src/app.js#fetchOne`, `#fetchLive`, `#fetchArchiveFallback`, `#fetchHistory`, `#fetchAllHistory`, `#fetchCustomChartSeries`; `YieldsMonitor/src/custom-series.js#layerCustomSeries`, `#archiveDatesToRead`
**Evidence:** [Close Price Investigation](./Close_Price_Investigation.md)  
**Input:** the active [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) (range, symbols, Custom start/end date), from the user; [Market Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#market-yields), from [CNBC (E5)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e5)'s chart-bar feed; [Yield history (S6)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s6); [Intraday archive (S18)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s18)
**Output:** [Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield-series) per symbol, to [Calculate day change (2.3)](./2.3_Calculate_Day_Change.md), [Adjust for seasonality (2.4)](./2.4_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md), [Render time series (2.5)](./2.5_Render_Time_Series.md), [Render yield curve snapshots (2.6)](./2.6_Render_Yield_Curve_Snapshots.md) and [Render breakeven inflation (2.7)](./2.7_Render_Breakeven_Inflation.md)

Read the active [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections), the [Market Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#market-yields) of [CNBC (E5)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e5), [Yield history (S6)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s6) and the [Intraday archive (S18)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s18), and write one [Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield-series) for each checked symbol:

- The range selects the sub-process: 2D and 10D, [Assemble 2D/10D range data (2.1.1)](#assemble-2d-10d-range-data); 1Y, 2Y and 3Y, [Assemble 1Y/2Y/3Y range data (2.1.2)](#assemble-1y-2y-3y-range-data); 10Y and ALL, [Assemble 10Y/ALL range data (2.1.3)](#assemble-10y-all-range-data); Custom, [Assemble Custom range data (2.1.4)](#assemble-custom-range-data).
- The sub-process depends on the range and is the same for every symbol, [TIPS](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips) and nominal Treasury alike.
- A read of [Yield history (S6)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s6) that fails contributes no points.
- The `TimeRange` values of the chart-bar feed read here return, measured on 2026-10-16 for US10Y and US10YTIPS:
  - `1D`: one-minute bars from two days before.
  - `5D`: five-minute bars from ten days before.
  - `1M`, `3M` and `6M`: daily bars from one, two and three years before.

<a id="assemble-2d-10d-range-data"></a>
## 2.1.1 Assemble 2D/10D range data

Read the [Market Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#market-yields) of each checked symbol and, when they are missing, the [Intraday archive (S18)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s18), and write the [Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield-series):

- Read the `1D` `TimeRange` of the chart-bar feed for 2D and the `5D` `TimeRange` for 10D.
  - A result read earlier in the session is reused unless the user asked for a fresh read.
  - A result with no bars is not kept, so the next read of the feed is made again.
- For 2D, also read the `5D` `TimeRange` under its own key, for [Calculate day change (2.3)](./2.3_Calculate_Day_Change.md).
- Keep the bars dated on or after the cutoff, midnight Eastern time of the date 2 days before the current date for 2D and 10 days before for 10D, and drop the bars dated a Saturday or a Sunday.
- If bars remain, they are the Yield Series.
- If no bar remains, the feed returned nothing for the symbol and `TimeRange`:
  - Read the [Intraday archive (S18)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s18) for the current date and each of the fifteen dates before it, from the latest, until a date whose snapshot has bars in the same `TimeRange`.
  - Write the bars of that date as the Yield Series. A date whose bars end before the close is used as it is.
  - If no date has bars, the Yield Series is empty and the chart shows no data for the range.

<a id="assemble-1y-2y-3y-range-data"></a>
## 2.1.2 Assemble 1Y/2Y/3Y range data

Read the daily [Market Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#market-yields) and the `5D` `TimeRange` of each checked symbol and, when the daily bars do not reach the cutoff, [Yield history (S6)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s6), and write the [Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield-series):

- Read the `6M` `TimeRange` of the chart-bar feed. One read per symbol serves 1Y, 2Y and 3Y.
  - The cutoff is midnight Eastern time of the date 1, 2 or 3 years before the current date.
  - The result is kept per symbol and reused unless it is missing, its first bar is dated after the cutoff, or the user asked for a fresh read.
  - If a read is made and the `6M` `TimeRange` returns no bars, read `3M`, and if that returns no bars, read `1M`. Their first bars are dated about two years and one year before, so they reach back less far than `6M`.
  - A new result is kept if no result is kept, or if its first bar is dated no later than the first bar of the kept result.
- Keep the bars dated on or after the cutoff and drop the bars dated a Saturday or a Sunday.
- Read the `5D` `TimeRange`, kept per symbol and reused unless the user asked for a fresh read, and append every bar dated later than the last kept bar, on or after the cutoff and not dated a Saturday or a Sunday.
- If no daily bars were read, or the first daily bar is dated after the cutoff, read [Yield history (S6)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s6) and write before the series its points dated on or after the cutoff and earlier than the first daily bar. These points have the weekly or quarterly resolution of S6.

<a id="assemble-10y-all-range-data"></a>
## 2.1.3 Assemble 10Y/ALL range data

Read [Yield history (S6)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s6) and the `5D` `TimeRange` of each checked symbol, and write the [Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield-series):

- Keep the points of [Yield history (S6)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s6) dated on or after the cutoff, midnight Eastern time of the date 10 years before the current date for 10Y and 50 years before for ALL.
- Read the `5D` `TimeRange` of the chart-bar feed, kept per symbol and reused unless the user asked for a fresh read, and append every bar dated later than the last kept point, on or after the cutoff and not dated a Saturday or a Sunday.

<a id="assemble-custom-range-data"></a>
## 2.1.4 Assemble Custom range data

Read [Yield history (S6)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s6), the [Market Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#market-yields) and the [Intraday archive (S18)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s18) for the Custom start and end date of the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections), and write two [Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield-series) for each checked symbol:

- The window is midnight Eastern time of the start date up to, and excluding, midnight Eastern time of the day after the end date.
- Keep the points of [Yield history (S6)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s6) inside the window and drop the points dated a Saturday or a Sunday.
- If the end of the window is later than five days before the current time, read the `5D` `TimeRange`, kept per symbol and reused unless the user asked for a fresh read, and append every bar dated later than the last kept point, inside the window and not dated a Saturday or a Sunday.
  - The start of the window applies to these bars as well, so a symbol whose S6 points include none inside the window receives no bar from before it. The 5-Year TIPS is such a symbol.
- The series so far has one value per day at the resolution that [Render yield curve snapshots (2.6)](./2.6_Render_Yield_Curve_Snapshots.md) and [Render breakeven inflation (2.7)](./2.7_Render_Breakeven_Inflation.md) read. Write it as the first Yield Series.
- Write the second Yield Series, read by [Render time series (2.5)](./2.5_Render_Time_Series.md), by layering these sources for each Eastern-time day:
  - One-minute bars: the `1D` `TimeRange` of the chart-bar feed, when the end of the window is later than five days before the current time, and the `1D` feed of the [Intraday archive (S18)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s18) snapshots of the ten latest weekdays of the window before the first date of the `1D` bars and before the current date, each with the snapshot of the following date. The snapshot of the following date holds the bars after the 17:05 Eastern-time snapshot.
  - Five-minute bars: the `5D` `TimeRange` of the chart-bar feed.
  - Daily points: the first Yield Series.
  - A day with one-minute bars takes only those. A day with five-minute bars and no one-minute bars takes only those. Any other day takes its daily points.
  - Bars dated a Saturday or a Sunday and bars outside the window are dropped. Of two bars with the same timestamp, the one read last is kept.
  - The limit of ten weekdays bounds the snapshot reads, which are up to about 0.5 MB per symbol and date (515 KB for US10Y on 2026-10-15). Weekdays beyond it keep the resolution that the other sources give them.

## Symbol reliability

The 5-Year TIPS chart-bar feed has sparse and irregular bars and can be missing for periods. Every sub-process above assembles it as it does any other symbol. [Render yield curve snapshots (2.6)](./2.6_Render_Yield_Curve_Snapshots.md) does not use the 5-Year TIPS to fix the dates of its curves.

## Why the 5D bars are appended

[Yield history (S6)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s6) is refreshed once on each weekday, and the daily `TimeRange` bars can lack the most recent day. On 2026-08-22 the `6M` `TimeRange` of US5YTIPS had bars through 2025-08-13 and then bars from 2026-08-22, without 2026-08-21, which its `3M` `TimeRange` held. The fallback from `6M` to `3M` applies only when `6M` returns no bars, so it does not detect a missing latest day. Appending the `5D` bars gives every range the same latest point. A symbol whose latest S6 point is more than the reach of the `5D` `TimeRange` old keeps the gap between that point and the first appended bar.
