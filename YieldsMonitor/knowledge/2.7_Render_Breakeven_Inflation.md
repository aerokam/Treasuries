# 2.7 Render breakeven inflation

**Specifies:** [2.7 Render breakeven inflation](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_YIELDSMONITOR)
**Constrained by:** [Render time series (2.5) §Display conventions](./2.5_Render_Time_Series.md#display-conventions), [§Pan and zoom](./2.5_Render_Time_Series.md#pan-and-zoom)
**Implemented by:** `YieldsMonitor/src/app.js#updateYieldCurves` (the breakeven build)
**Input:** [Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield-series), from [Assemble range data (2.1)](./2.1_Assemble_Range_Data.md); [SA Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield-series), from [Adjust for seasonality (2.4)](./2.4_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md)
**Output:** [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables), to the user

Read the [Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield-series) and the [SA Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield-series) of every symbol, and write the [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables) of the Breakeven Inflation tab: the [Breakeven Inflation](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#breakeven-inflation) of each of the five maturities that have both a nominal Treasury and a TIPS symbol, on the start date and the end date of the active range.

## Per maturity pair

For each of the five pairs, 1-Year, 2-Year, 5-Year, 10-Year and 30-Year, each a nominal Treasury symbol and a TIPS symbol of the same maturity label:

- Calculate the Breakeven: the [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) of the nominal Treasury less the Yield of the TIPS, on the same date.

## Snapshot dates

The start date and the end date are those of [Render yield curve snapshots (2.6)](./2.6_Render_Yield_Curve_Snapshots.md#snapshot-dates), which specifies the anchor symbol and its fallbacks:

- Start dataset: the Breakeven calculated from the first point of each side on the start date.
- End dataset: the Breakeven calculated from the last point of each side on the end date.
- If either side of a pair has no point on the date, the pair has a gap in that dataset.
- The datasets have the labels that [Render yield curve snapshots (2.6)](./2.6_Render_Yield_Curve_Snapshots.md#labeling) specifies.

## Hide non-trading hours (2D, 10D and Custom ranges only)

With the Hide non-trading hours checkbox ([Render time series (2.5)](./2.5_Render_Time_Series.md#time-series-options)) on, each side of a pair is read in the window that [Render yield curve snapshots (2.6)](./2.6_Render_Yield_Curve_Snapshots.md#hide-non-trading-hours) specifies, the opening window being the one of this curve, and the datasets have the labels it specifies, including the end dataset of a day still open, which has no close. A pair has a gap when either side has no point inside the window.

## SA overlay

When the Seasonally Adjusted checkbox is on ([Adjust for seasonality (2.4)](./2.4_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md)), two further datasets, labeled with "(SA)", hold for each pair the Yield of the nominal Treasury less the SA Yield of the TIPS, on the start date and the end date. The SA Yield is the first or the last point of the [SA Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield-series) of the TIPS on the date.

- A pair or date with no SA Yield has a gap, drawn as in [Render yield curve snapshots (2.6)](./2.6_Render_Yield_Curve_Snapshots.md). The SA Yield of an end date of today exists, because the TIPS of the current date comes from the Live Quotes.
- Unchecking the Quoted checkbox empties the Breakeven datasets without "(SA)", because each uses the Yield of a TIPS. The Yield of the nominal Treasury does not depend on either checkbox.
- The chart keeps all four datasets, empty while their checkbox is off, so a change of checkbox updates the data and does not rebuild the chart.
