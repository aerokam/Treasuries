- A point has no SA Yield when the SA Factors cannot be read, when the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) is on or after the [Maturity Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date), or when no TIPS resolves for its date.

<a id="bond-identity-today-and-history"></a>
## Bond identity: today and history

For each point of a TIPS symbol's Yield Series, resolve the TIPS behind the point:

- If the point is dated today (Eastern time) and the [Live Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#live-quotes) service gave a Maturity Date and Coupon Rate for the symbol, use them ([Read live quotes (2.2)](./2.2_Read_Live_Quotes.md)).
- Otherwise find the entry of CNBC_ROLLOVER_LOG with the latest `from` date on or before the date of the point.
  - Read the Coupon Rate from [TIPS reference data (S2)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s2) for the maturity of that entry. If more than one row has that maturity, use the row with the latest dated date.
  - If no entry applies, or S2 has no row with that maturity, the date has no TIPS and its points have no SA Yield. A maturity that predates S2, or a cohort not yet auctioned, is such a case.
- The quote service gives the TIPS of the current date only, so every earlier date resolves from the log.

<a id="the-cnbc-rollover-log"></a>
## The CNBC rollover log

CNBC_ROLLOVER_LOG (`YieldsMonitor/src/cnbc-rollover-log.js`) lists, for each of the five symbols, the maturity of the TIPS that the symbol quoted from each `from` date. Each entry is pinned individually; a calendar rule does not reproduce the record. The actual lag after a cohort checkpoint varies from cycle to cycle, and `US1YTIPS` and `US2YTIPS` have sometimes quoted a TIPS of another origin-auction family. Three methods pin an entry:

- Cross-check, where the candidates differ enough in Yield:
  - Calculate the Yield of each candidate TIPS from its [FedInvest (E1)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e1) `sell` price (the bid, as the CNBC quotes are on the bid side), or the `buy` price where the candidate has no bid that day, and compare it to the Yield that CNBC reported.
  - The `from` date is the first date that the fit moves from one candidate to the other.
- Issue-date fallback, where the candidates are too close in Yield for the cross-check (`US5YTIPS`, `US10YTIPS`, and part of `US30YTIPS`):
  - The `from` date is the first trading day after the last business day of the month that contains the dated date of the new cohort.
  - The error from a wrong day is bounded by the Yield difference that the cross-check could not resolve.
- Closest-remaining-maturity fallback, for `US1YTIPS` and `US2YTIPS` before their earliest cross-checked entry:
  - These symbols quote a TIPS near maturity, not a newly issued one, so the issue-date fallback does not apply.
  - The entry is the already-issued, unmatured TIPS with the maturity closest to date plus one year (`US1YTIPS`) or two years (`US2YTIPS`). A new entry is recorded each time that choice changes.
- A date left without an entry has no SA Yield. An entry is pinned by one of the methods wherever a plausible candidate exists, because a Yield with a lower-confidence SA Yield is preferred to a Yield with none. A gap is recorded only where no plausible candidate exists (see Known limitations).

<a id="automated-rollover-check"></a>
## Automated rollover check

`YieldsMonitor/scripts/checkCnbcRollover.js` runs daily (the `CheckCnbcRollover` task of `scripts/setup-windows-tasks.ps1`). For each of the five symbols, read the Maturity Date that the [Live Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#live-quotes) service gives and the last entry of CNBC_ROLLOVER_LOG:

- If the two maturities are equal, do nothing for the symbol.
- Otherwise find the flip date:
  - For each date from the latest back to 75 days earlier that has [FedInvest (E1)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e1) prices and a CNBC Yield in [Yield history (S6)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s6), calculate the Yield of the new candidate and of the old candidate from its FedInvest price, with Settlement Date equal to the date (FedInvest prices settle on the day), and compare both to the CNBC Yield of S6.
  - The date fits the new candidate when its Yield is within 10 bp of the CNBC Yield and the old candidate is at least 20 bp further. The date fits the old candidate by the same test reversed. Any other date is ambiguous and is skipped.
  - The flip date is the earliest date that fits the new candidate and follows the latest date that fits the old candidate.
  - If the walk ends without a date that fits the old candidate, or reaches one before any date that fits the new candidate, the symbol is left unpinned and the script logs it.
- Append the entry to CNBC_ROLLOVER_LOG and a row to the table below, run the unit tests, and commit and push both files if they pass. On a test failure, revert both files and log the result.

<a id="the-transform-per-point"></a>
## The transform, per point

For each point of the Yield Series of a TIPS symbol:

- Set the trade date to the Eastern-time date of the point.
- Set the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) to the next bond trading day after the trade date, skipping Saturdays, Sundays and [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday) of [Bond holidays (S16)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s16).
- Resolve the [Maturity Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date) and [Coupon Rate](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#coupon-rate) as [Bond identity: today and history](#bond-identity-today-and-history) specifies.
  - If the Settlement Date is on or after the Maturity Date, the point has no SA Yield.
- Read the [SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) at the Settlement Date and the [Maturity SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-sa-factor) from [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s4), and calculate the [SA Price Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-price-factor), as [Adjust for seasonality (3.2)](../../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) specifies.
  - If either factor is unavailable, the point has no SA Yield.
- Calculate the price that the Yield implies at the Settlement Date and Maturity Date.
- Calculate the SA Price: the price times the SA Price Factor.
- Calculate the SA Yield: the Yield that the SA Price implies at the same Settlement Date and Maturity Date.

## UI

The sidebar group "Show TIPS yields" has the checkboxes Quoted and Seasonally Adjusted (SA). It holds two pairs of states, one for Time Series and one for Yield Curves and Breakeven Inflation together ([Render yield curve snapshots (2.6)](./2.6_Render_Yield_Curve_Snapshots.md) and [Render breakeven inflation (2.7)](./2.7_Render_Breakeven_Inflation.md) are drawn by one render call). Switching tabs shows the checked states and the title of the pair that applies, "Time Series" or "Yield Curves / BEI".

- Defaults: Time Series has Quoted checked; Yield Curves and Breakeven Inflation have Seasonally Adjusted (SA) checked.
- Each checkbox can be unchecked, except that unchecking the one that is checked while the other is unchecked checks the other. Its data is loaded first if it is not loaded.
- A change updates the pair of the active tab only.
- Time Series overlay: the Yield first and the SA Yield second, on each chart of the five TIPS symbols ([Render time series (2.5)](./2.5_Render_Time_Series.md)). The chart of a nominal Treasury always shows the Yield.
- Yield Curves and Breakeven Inflation overlays: see [Render yield curve snapshots (2.6)](./2.6_Render_Yield_Curve_Snapshots.md) and [Render breakeven inflation (2.7)](./2.7_Render_Breakeven_Inflation.md).

## Known limitations

- A point dated before the first entry of its symbol in CNBC_ROLLOVER_LOG has no SA Yield. The first entries are `US1YTIPS` and `US2YTIPS` 2014-04-01, `US5YTIPS` 2006-05-01, `US10YTIPS` 1997-02-03 and `US30YTIPS` 2010-03-01.
  - [TIPS reference data (S2)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s2) holds no 5-Year TIPS dated between 1997-07-15 and 2006-04-15 except one 5-Year 6-Month TIPS dated 2004-10-15, and no 30-Year TIPS dated between 2001-10-15 (a 30-Year 6-Month TIPS) and 2010-02-15.
- A point with a maturity that is not in S2 has no SA Yield.
- The `US5YTIPS` feed has sparse and irregular bars ([Assemble range data (2.1)](./2.1_Assemble_Range_Data.md)). The SA Yield is calculated for it as for the other symbols.
- Nearly all entries of `US5YTIPS` and `US10YTIPS`, and the entries of `US30YTIPS` before 2026-03-02 other than those marked cross-checked in the table, are pinned by the issue-date fallback, because the cross-check cannot resolve them.
- The entries of `US1YTIPS` and `US2YTIPS` from 2014-04-01 through 2024-02-29 are pinned by the closest-remaining-maturity fallback. Each is a real TIPS, and the candidates are not necessarily close in Yield, so which TIPS CNBC quoted on a date is unresolved.
