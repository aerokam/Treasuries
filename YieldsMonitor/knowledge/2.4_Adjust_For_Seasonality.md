**Input:** [Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield-series), from [Assemble range data (2.1)](./2.1_Assemble_Range_Data.md); [Live Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#live-quotes), from [Read live quotes (2.2)](./2.2_Read_Live_Quotes.md); [TIPS reference data (S2)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s2); [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s4); [Bond holidays (S16)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s16)
**Output:** [SA Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield-series), for the five TIPS symbols, to [Render time series (2.5)](./2.5_Render_Time_Series.md), [Render yield curve snapshots (2.6)](./2.6_Render_Yield_Curve_Snapshots.md) and [Render breakeven inflation (2.7)](./2.7_Render_Breakeven_Inflation.md)

Carries each of the five TIPS [Yield Series](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield-series) (1Y, 2Y, 5Y, 10Y, 30Y) through the same Price to SA Price to [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) transform [Adjust for seasonality (3.2)](../../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) applies to an actual TIPS. CNBC's canonical-maturity symbols are not synthetic constant-maturity indices — each is the bid Yield of one specific, real TIPS — so the transform applies directly, using that bond's real Coupon Rate and Maturity Date rather than a constructed one. The seasonal effect amortizes with maturity and is at or below noise level by 10Y and 30Y, but is still calculated and shown at every maturity for curve completeness on the Yield Curves and Breakeven Inflation tabs, rather than leaving those curves visibly stop at 5Y.

## Bond identity: today vs. history

For each point in a TIPS symbol's Yield Series, the real bond behind that point is resolved one of two ways:

- If the point is dated today: use [Read live quotes (2.2)](./2.2_Read_Live_Quotes.md)'s own reading of the symbol's current Maturity Date and Coupon Rate.
- Otherwise: look up the maturity CNBC_ROLLOVER_LOG records the symbol as quoting on that date, then read that maturity's Coupon Rate from [TIPS reference data (S2)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s2) — the most recently dated cohort, if more than one row shares the maturity.
  - CNBC's quote service exposes only a symbol's current bond, not a historical mapping, so every date but today is resolved from this observed record rather than read live.

## The CNBC rollover log

CNBC_ROLLOVER_LOG (`YieldsMonitor/src/cnbc-rollover-log.js`) records which real TIPS each canonical-maturity symbol actually quoted, and from what date. It has nothing to do with seasonality itself — a calendar rule was tried first (roll every six months, on the fifteenth of the origin-auction month, always the same bond family) and falsified twice over: the actual lag after a checkpoint varies cycle to cycle, and `US1YTIPS`/`US2YTIPS` have sometimes matched a bond from an entirely different origin-auction family. No fixed rule reproduces this, so every entry is instead pinned individually:

- Where the empirical cross-check can resolve it: compute each candidate bond's real Yield from its FedInvest price (the `sell` price — the bid, matching CNBC's own bid-side quotes — falling back to `buy` only when a candidate has no active bid that day) and compare it to what CNBC actually reported; the transition date is where the fit flips from one candidate to the other.
- Where candidates sit too close in Yield to disambiguate this way (`US5YTIPS`, `US10YTIPS`, and part of `US30YTIPS`'s history): pin the flip to the first trading day after the last business day of the month containing the new cohort's dated date instead. The candidates being close bounds the SA error from picking the "wrong" day to a gap the cross-check itself could not resolve in the first place, which is a materially smaller error than the falsified calendar rule was ever off by.
- For `US1YTIPS`/`US2YTIPS` before their earliest cross-checked entry, where even the dated-date rule does not transfer (these symbols track a bond nearing maturity, not a newly issued one): pin to whichever already-issued, not-yet-matured bond has a maturity closest to the symbol's own target horizon from that date, walking forward and recording a new entry each time the pick changes.
- A quoted Yield with no SA counterpart at all is treated as worse than a lower-confidence SA value, so every stretch above is pinned by one of these methods rather than left a gap. A gap is recorded only where no plausible candidate bond exists at all — see Known limitations.

## Automated rollover check

`YieldsMonitor/scripts/checkCnbcRollover.js` runs daily and compares each of the five symbols' live bond identity to CNBC_ROLLOVER_LOG's own last entry:

- If they match, no action.
- If they differ:
  - Walk backward from the most recent trading day, computing the new candidate's implied Yield from its real FedInvest price at T+0 settlement — the FedInvest historical-price convention, distinct from the T+1 [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) the live transform above uses — and comparing it to CNBC's own archived quoted Yield for that day, until the fit flips from the new candidate back to the old one.
  - That boundary day is the flip date. Append the pinned entry to CNBC_ROLLOVER_LOG and to the table below, run the unit tests, and commit and push both files only if they pass.
  - On any failure — the tests fail, or no clean flip date is found within the lookback window — revert the edits and only log the finding, rather than leave a half-applied or unverified change behind.

## The transform, per point

For each historical or current Yield quote:

- Advance the quote's trade date to the next SIFMA bond-trading day — the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) this transform settles at.
- Resolve the Maturity Date and Coupon Rate per the bond-identity rule above.
- Calculate the price the quoted Yield implies, at that Settlement Date and Maturity Date.
- Calculate the SA Price: the price times the ratio of the [SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) at the Settlement Date to the SA Factor at the Maturity Date. SA Factors come from [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s4) — the exact date if published, else the most recent past occurrence of the same calendar month and day, since the daily SA Factor is a slowly drifting annual pattern and no future value is published.
- Calculate the SA Yield the SA Price implies, at that same Settlement Date and Maturity Date.

## UI

- **Toggle:** "Show TIPS yields" in the sidebar, two checkboxes — Quoted and Seasonally Adjusted (SA). One shared pair of checkbox elements, but two independent states: Time Series has its own; Yield Curves and Breakeven Inflation share a second one (those two tabs are already driven by one render call — see [2.6](./2.6_Render_Yield_Curve_Snapshots.md), [2.7](./2.7_Render_Breakeven_Inflation.md)). Switching tabs swaps the displayed checked state and the toggle's own label to whichever pair applies.
  - Default: Time Series starts Quoted only; Yield Curves and Breakeven Inflation start SA only.
  - Either can be unchecked on its own.
  - If the second-remaining checkbox is unchecked: switch the selection to the other one instead of leaving neither checked, loading its prerequisites first if needed. This rule applies within the active tab's own pair.
  - Changing the toggle updates only the active tab's own group (Time Series, or Yield Curves + Breakeven Inflation together) — the other group's state and charts are untouched.
- **Time Series overlay:** raw Yield first, SA Yield second, on every eligible chart — see [Render time series (2.5)](./2.5_Render_Time_Series.md).
- **Yield Curves and Breakeven Inflation overlays:** see [Render yield curve snapshots (2.6)](./2.6_Render_Yield_Curve_Snapshots.md) and [Render breakeven inflation (2.7)](./2.7_Render_Breakeven_Inflation.md).
- **Sidebar:** an additional reading next to each eligible symbol's Yield and Day Change — today's SA Yield. Gated on the Time Series tab's own Seasonally Adjusted checkbox specifically (not the Yield Curves/Breakeven Inflation one), the same toggle that drives the Time Series SA overlay line — empty when that checkbox is off, regardless of which tab is active or what the Yield Curves/Breakeven Inflation toggle is set to.

## Known limitations

- **US5YTIPS feed reliability:** the underlying feed for this symbol is flaky with large gaps (see [Assemble range data (2.1)](./2.1_Assemble_Range_Data.md)) — SA is calculated for it the same way as every other maturity, but the feed itself is the limiting factor.
- **US5YTIPS rollover-log gap before 2006-04-15:** not a missing-data problem for the whole stretch — CNBC's deep history exists back to 2004 — but 5-Year TIPS themselves had an issuance hiatus (the last pre-2004 cohort matured 2002-07-15; no new cohort until 2006-04-15), so a quote in that dead stretch isn't a real on-the-run 5-Year TIPS and is left an explicit gap, the same reasoning as US30YTIPS's 2001-2010 hiatus below.
- **US5YTIPS and US10YTIPS have almost no empirically cross-checked coverage:** structurally unresolvable by the yield cross-check (see The CNBC rollover log) — nearly all of their history, and part of US30YTIPS's before 2026-03-02, comes from the lower-confidence issue-date fallback instead. This is a deliberate, bounded-error choice, not an oversight.
- **US1YTIPS/US2YTIPS's 2014-04-01 through 2024-02-29 coverage is lower-confidence by construction:** the closest-remaining-maturity fallback picks a real, plausible bond for every date in this range, but the candidates are not necessarily close to each other in Yield, since which real-world bond family CNBC actually tracked on a given date is genuinely unresolved this far back.
- **US10YTIPS/US30YTIPS's SA effect is small by construction:** the seasonal effect amortizes with maturity and is at or below noise level beyond roughly six years to maturity — the SA line at these maturities sitting almost exactly on the raw Yield line is the correct, expected result, not a rendering defect.
- **Coverage boundary:** if the maturity resolved for a trade date is not in [TIPS reference data (S2)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s2) at all — predating the reference data, or a future checkpoint not yet auctioned — that point is skipped rather than guessed at.
