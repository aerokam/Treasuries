# 1.0 Seasonal Adjustments Explorer

**Implemented by:** `SeasonalAdjustments/index.html`, `SeasonalAdjustments/src/snap-data.js`, `SeasonalAdjustments/src/snap-slides.js`, `SeasonalAdjustments/src/sa-annotate.js`

Present the seasonal adjustment of TIPS yields as a sequence of full-page annotated charts, drawn from a frozen snapshot of market quotes and Ref CPI. The sequence follows [Canty (2009)](../../YieldCurves/knowledge/Canty.md), and the adjustment it reaches is the one specified in [Adjust for seasonality (3.2)](../../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md).

How the pages are drawn and selected is specified in [Render pages (11.2)](./11.2_Render_Pages.md), and the data they are drawn from in [Load snapshot (11.1)](./11.1_Load_Snapshot.md).

## Pages

| # | Title | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Sawtooth | The quoted TIPS real yield of each bond in the snapshot against its maturity date, from July 2027. The January and July maturities are bold and connected. |
| 2 | Index = Trend × Seasonal | A model index with the trend held at 100 and a seasonal component that rises 1% a month from January to a July peak and returns to 100 the following January. |
| 3 | Why the Month Matters | The [SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) across one year, with the settlement date and the four maturity dates, 15 January, April, July and October, marked on it. |
| 4 | The Extra Months | The SA Factor across the life of the TIPS maturing 2028-04-15 with a 1.250% coupon (CUSIP 91282CGW5): its settlement-to-maturity span is one whole year and a part year, and only the part year has a net seasonal change. |
| 5 | Trend × Seasonal | The last 400 days of Ref CPI NSA and Ref CPI SA from the snapshot, and below them their ratio, the SA Factor. |
| 6 | From Payments to One Ratio | The price of a TIPS as the discounted sum of its payments, each scaled by its Ref CPI ratio, reduced to the single ratio of the SA Factor at settlement to the SA Factor at maturity (Canty Eq. 14). |
| 7 | Near and Far | That ratio applied to the TIPS maturing 2028-10-15 and the TIPS of page 4: the [SA Price Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-price-factor) and the change from the quoted yield to the [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) of each. |
