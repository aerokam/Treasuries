# 1.0 Seasonal Adjustments Explorer

**Implemented by:** `SeasonalAdjustments/index.html`, `SeasonalAdjustments/src/snap-data.js`, `SeasonalAdjustments/src/snap-slides.js`, `SeasonalAdjustments/src/sa-annotate.js`

Present the seasonal adjustment of TIPS yields as a sequence of full-page annotated charts, drawn from a frozen snapshot of market quotes and Ref CPI. The sequence follows [Canty (2009)](../../YieldCurves/knowledge/Canty.md), and the adjustment it reaches is the one specified in [Adjust for seasonality (3.2)](../../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md).

<a id="pages"></a>
## Pages

- Draw one page at a time, each a single SVG chart with a title above it. The annotations (notes, arrows, rings, brackets) are drawn on the chart, and no legend or paragraph text stands outside it.
- Register each page in the `PAGES` list of `index.html` as a title, a drawing function and a chart height (520 for every page today). The table of contents, the page dots and the crumb are built from that list.
- Show the page with the left and right arrow keys, the Prev and Next buttons, a click on a dot, or an entry of the Contents drawer. The drawer lists each page as its number and title, and the Escape key closes it. The crumb states "n / N".
- Draw a page when it is shown.

| # | Title | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Sawtooth | The quoted TIPS real yield of each bond in the snapshot against its maturity date, from July 2027. The January and July maturities are bold and connected. |
| 2 | Index = Trend × Seasonal | A model index with the trend held at 100 and a seasonal component that rises 1% a month from January to a July peak and returns to 100 the following January. |
| 3 | Why the Month Matters | The [SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) across one year, with the settlement date and the four maturity dates, 15 January, April, July and October, marked on it. |
| 4 | The Extra Months | The SA Factor across the life of the TIPS maturing 2028-04-15 with a 1.250% coupon (CUSIP 91282CGW5): its settlement-to-maturity span is one whole year and a part year, and only the part year has a net seasonal change. |
| 5 | Trend × Seasonal | The last 400 days of Ref CPI NSA and Ref CPI SA from the snapshot, and below them their ratio, the SA Factor. |
| 6 | From Payments to One Ratio | The price of a TIPS as the discounted sum of its payments, each scaled by its Ref CPI ratio, reduced to the single ratio of the SA Factor at settlement to the SA Factor at maturity (Canty Eq. 14). |
| 7 | Near and Far | That ratio applied to the TIPS maturing 2028-10-15 and the TIPS of page 4: the [SA Price Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-price-factor) and the change from the quoted yield to the [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) of each. |

## Data

- Read two CSV files in `SeasonalAdjustments/data/` when the page opens. Nothing is fetched from R2.
  - `YieldsSaSao.snapshot.csv` has the columns of [SA and SAO yields (S10)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s10), one row per outstanding TIPS (53 rows). Drop a row without a maturity or an ask yield, and sort the rest by maturity.
  - `RefCpiNsaSa.snapshot.csv` has the columns of [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s4). Sort it by date, oldest first.
- State the quotes as of 2026-08-31 and the settlement date as 2026-09-01, one bond trading day later. The footer reads "Frozen snapshot — market quotes as of Aug 31, 2026, " and the number of TIPS loaded. When a file does not load, the footer reads "Snapshot data failed to load: " and the reason.
- Set the Term of each bond, in years, to the days from the settlement date to its maturity divided by 365.25. The slides use it for display only.
- Build the seasonal wave, one SA Factor for each of 365 calendar days, from the most recent calendar year of the snapshot with at least 360 daily values. A day with no value takes the value of the day before. A single year is used so that the wave has one annual cycle and no seam where two years join, and the maturity factor of [Adjust for seasonality (3.2)](../../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) is likewise one year's value.
- Compute the prices and yields of page 7 from the wave with `shared/src/bond-math.js#priceFromYield` and `#yieldFromPrice`, so that they agree with the wave of pages 3 to 5. They are not read from the `sa_yield` column of the snapshot.
