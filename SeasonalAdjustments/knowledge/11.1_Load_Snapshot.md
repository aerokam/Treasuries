# 11.1 Load snapshot

**Specifies:** [11.1 Load snapshot](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_SEASONALADJUSTMENTS)
**Implemented by:** `SeasonalAdjustments/src/snap-data.js` (`loadSnapshot`, `buildWave`, `Sat`)
**Input:** [Yield curves snapshot (S22)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s22) and [Ref CPI NSA and SA snapshot (S23)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s23)

Read the two snapshot files and build the seasonal wave. No job writes the files.

<a id="data"></a>
- Read the two files when the page opens. Nothing is fetched from R2.
  - Yield curves snapshot has 53 rows, one for each outstanding TIPS. Drop a row without a maturity or an ask yield, and sort the rest by maturity.
  - Ref CPI NSA and SA snapshot: sort it by date, oldest first.
- State the quotes as of 2026-08-31 and the settlement date as 2026-09-01, one bond trading day later. The footer reads "Frozen snapshot — market quotes as of Aug 31, 2026, " and the number of TIPS loaded. When a file does not load, the footer reads "Snapshot data failed to load: " and the reason.
- Set the Term of each bond, in years, to the days from the settlement date to its maturity divided by 365.25. The slides use it for display only.
- Build the seasonal wave, one SA Factor for each of 365 calendar days, from the most recent calendar year of the snapshot with at least 360 daily values. A day with no value takes the value of the day before. A single year is used so that the wave has one annual cycle and no seam where two years join, and the maturity factor of [Adjust for seasonality (1.3.2)](../../knowledge/1.3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) is likewise one year's value.
