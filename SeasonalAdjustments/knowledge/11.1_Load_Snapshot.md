# 11.1 Load snapshot

**Specifies:** [11.1 Load snapshot](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_SEASONALADJUSTMENTS)
**Implemented by:** `SeasonalAdjustments/src/snap-data.js` (`loadSnapshot`, `buildWave`, `Sat`)
**Input:** the snapshot of [SA and SAO yields (S10)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s10) and the snapshot of [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s4), two files kept in `SeasonalAdjustments/data/`
**Output:** [SAO Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yields) and the [SA Factors](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor), with the seasonal wave, to [Render pages (11.2)](./11.2_Render_Pages.md); [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables) to the user (the footer)

Read the two snapshot files and build the seasonal wave. No job writes the files.

<a id="data"></a>
- Read two CSV files in `SeasonalAdjustments/data/` when the page opens. Nothing is fetched from R2.
  - `YieldsSaSao.snapshot.csv` has the columns of [SA and SAO yields (S10)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s10), one row per outstanding TIPS (53 rows). Drop a row without a maturity or an ask yield, and sort the rest by maturity.
  - `RefCpiNsaSa.snapshot.csv` has the columns of [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s4). Sort it by date, oldest first.
- State the quotes as of 2026-08-31 and the settlement date as 2026-09-01, one bond trading day later. The footer reads "Frozen snapshot — market quotes as of Aug 31, 2026, " and the number of TIPS loaded. When a file does not load, the footer reads "Snapshot data failed to load: " and the reason.
- Set the Term of each bond, in years, to the days from the settlement date to its maturity divided by 365.25. The slides use it for display only.
- Build the seasonal wave, one SA Factor for each of 365 calendar days, from the most recent calendar year of the snapshot with at least 360 daily values. A day with no value takes the value of the day before. A single year is used so that the wave has one annual cycle and no seam where two years join, and the maturity factor of [Adjust for seasonality (3.2)](../../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) is likewise one year's value.
