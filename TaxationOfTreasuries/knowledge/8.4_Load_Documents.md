# 8.4 Load documents

**Specifies:** [8.4 Load documents](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TAXATIONOFTREASURIES)
**Implemented by:** `TaxationOfTreasuries/index.html` (`DOCS`, `load`)
**Input:** [Taxation documents (S20)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s20); [Document Selection](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#document-selection) from the user

Fetch the file of the selected document from S20, by its path in `DOCS`, and give its [Document Text](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#document-text) to [Render documents (8.5)](./8.5_Render_Documents.md).

- `DOCS` lists the entries of the left pane in order: Foundation, Treasury Bills, Notes & Bonds, TIPS OID Reference, the OID Calculator ([Render OID calculator §Left pane](./8.3_Render_Oid_Calculator.md#left-pane)), and Research Thread. The OID Calculator has no file.
- The selection is Foundation when the page opens.
- When the fetch fails, give the reason in place of the text: "HTTP " and the status the server returned, or the message of the failure.
