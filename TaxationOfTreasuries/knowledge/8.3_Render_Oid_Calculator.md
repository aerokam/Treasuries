# 8.3 Render OID calculator

**Specifies:** [8.3 Render OID calculator](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TAXATIONOFTREASURIES)
**Implemented by:** `TaxationOfTreasuries/src/oid-calculator.js` (`render`), `TaxationOfTreasuries/index.html` (`DOCS`, `load`)
**Input:** the rows of [Load OID inputs (8.1)](./8.1_Load_Oid_Inputs.md), the result or reason of [Calculate TIPS OID (8.2)](./8.2_Calculate_Tips_Oid.md); [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) from the user

Show the OID Calculator in the content pane, beside the reference documents in the left pane.

<a id="left-pane"></a>
## Left pane

- Add an entry "OID Calculator", with the tag "TIPS / calculator", after "TIPS OID Reference". Selecting it replaces the content pane with the calculator.

<a id="range"></a>
## Range

- Start Date and End Date: date fields of the [shared date picker](../../knowledge/SharedModules.md#m22), empty when the page opens. They state the range of [Calculate TIPS OID §OID of the entries](./8.2_Calculate_Tips_Oid.md#entries).
- Index Ratio: a choice of "Not rounded", selected when the page opens, and "Rounded as Treasury rounds it" ([Calculate TIPS OID §OID](./8.2_Calculate_Tips_Oid.md#oid)). It applies to every entry, and a change recalculates them.

<a id="entry-form"></a>
## Entry form

One row of fields above the table of entries:

- CUSIP: a drop-down list with a blank first choice. Each other choice shows the CUSIP, the coupon, "due " and the Maturity Date of a TIPS outstanding in the range. The list is empty until both dates of the range are entered and the Start Date is not after the End Date, and it changes when the range changes. While an entry is being edited ([Edit](#edit)), the list also holds its CUSIP.
- Quantity: a number field of whole numbers of 1 or more, blank when the page opens.
- Start Date and End Date: date fields of the shared date picker.
  - Until the user changes one, it shows the date of the range, or the date [Calculate TIPS OID §OID of the entries](./8.2_Calculate_Tips_Oid.md#entries) gives for the chosen TIPS, and changes when the range or the CUSIP changes.
  - A Start Date entered before the Original Issue Date of the chosen TIPS is replaced in the field by the Original Issue Date.
  - The End Date field cannot be changed while the End Date of the range is after the Maturity Date of the chosen TIPS, and shows the Maturity Date.
- Add: a button. Pressing Enter in a field of the form does the same.
  - Calculate the OID of the entry. When there is a reason, show its message ([Messages](#messages)) below the form and add nothing.
  - Otherwise add the entry to the table, clear the CUSIP and the Quantity, and return the Start Date and End Date to those of the range.

<a id="table"></a>
## Table of entries

Shown below the form once there is an entry, with the columns CUSIP, Maturity Date, Coupon, Quantity, Start Date, End Date, OID and a column of buttons.

- One row per entry, in the order of [Calculate TIPS OID §OID of the entries](./8.2_Calculate_Tips_Oid.md#entries). Start Date and End Date show the dates of the entry. When the End Date is after the latest known Ref CPI, the OID cell also shows "as of " and the date used.
- Below the rows of a CUSIP with two or more entries, a row "Subtotal OID of " and the CUSIP, with the subtotal in the OID column.
- The last row, Total OID, shows the total.
- The buttons of a row:
  - <a id="edit"></a>Edit: removes the entry from the table and puts its CUSIP, Quantity, Start Date and End Date in the form, which keeps those dates as they are whatever the range, until Add is pressed.
  - Remove: removes the entry from the table.
- Below the table, these notes, each when it applies:
  - When the End Date of an entry is after the latest known Ref CPI: "The latest known Ref CPI is that of " and that date, ". The OID of an entry whose End Date is after it is calculated as of that date."
  - When the Ref CPI of a date is the NSA value of S8 and the latest known Ref CPI does not replace it: "Treasury has not yet published the Ref CPI of " and the dates, separated by ", ", ". Each is known from the CPI-U of the month three months earlier, as 31 CFR 356 Appendix B §I.B.3 states for the first day of a month."
  - When an OID is negative: "A negative OID is a deflation adjustment."

<a id="messages"></a>
## Messages

Below the form:

- While a date of the range is empty: "Enter a Start Date and an End Date for the range." While the Start Date of the range is after its End Date: "The Start Date is after the End Date."
- When Add is pressed with no CUSIP chosen, in place of an entry added: "Choose a CUSIP."
- When Add is pressed, in place of an entry added, a reason of [Calculate TIPS OID §Inputs with no result](./8.2_Calculate_Tips_Oid.md#invalid) shows its message:
  - The Quantity is invalid: "Quantity is a whole number of 1 or more."
  - A date is missing: "Enter a Start Date and an End Date."
  - The Start Date is after the End Date: "The Start Date is after the End Date."
  - The Start Date is after the Maturity Date: "The Start Date is after the Maturity Date, " and the Maturity Date, ".".
  - The End Date is before the Original Issue Date: "The End Date is before the Original Issue Date, " and the Original Issue Date, ".".
  - The End Date is after the Maturity Date: "The End Date is after the Maturity Date, " and the Maturity Date, ". For a TIPS held to maturity, enter the Maturity Date."
  - S3 has no Ref CPI for a date: "There is no Ref CPI for the Start Date." or "There is no Ref CPI for the End Date.", then " Ref CPI values are available from " and the first date of S3, " through " and the latest known Ref CPI date, ".".

<a id="help"></a>
## Help text

Show the help text above the range, in these items:

- "OID is the inflation adjustment of a TIPS from a Start Date to an End Date: Par × (Index Ratio of the End Date − Index Ratio of the Start Date)."
- "The Start Date and End Date at the top state the range. The CUSIPs offered are the TIPS outstanding in it, and a new entry takes its dates."
- "Choose a CUSIP, enter the Quantity, Start Date and End Date of one acquisition, and press Add. A CUSIP acquired on different dates has one entry for each."
- "Quantity is the number of $1,000 par units. A Quantity of 1 is $1,000 par."
- "Start Date is the first day the TIPS is held in the tax year:"
  - "A TIPS bought in the tax year: the Settlement Date of the purchase, not the Trade Date."
  - "A TIPS bought in an earlier year: January 1 of the tax year."
  - "A Start Date before the Original Issue Date of the TIPS is replaced by it."
- "End Date is the last day of the period:"
  - "A TIPS held at the end of the tax year: January 1 of the next year."
  - "A TIPS sold during the tax year: the Settlement Date of the sale."
  - "A TIPS held to maturity: the Maturity Date."
- "Index Ratio, not rounded, is the Ref CPI of the date divided by the Dated Date Ref CPI. Rounded as Treasury rounds it, the ratio is truncated to six decimal places and then rounded to five. Compare the OID of each CUSIP, the subtotal when it has more than one entry, with Box 8 of the Form 1099-OID."
- "When Treasury has not yet published the Ref CPI of a date:"
  - "The first of a month: the Ref CPI is the CPI-U of the month three months earlier, as 31 CFR 356 Appendix B §I.B.3 states. January 1 takes the CPI-U of October."
  - "Any other date: the OID is calculated as of the latest known Ref CPI, and the result states its date."
