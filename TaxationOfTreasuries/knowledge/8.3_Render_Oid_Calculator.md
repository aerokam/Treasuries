# 8.3 Render OID calculator

**Specifies:** [8.3 Render OID calculator](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TAXATIONOFTREASURIES)
**Implemented by:** `TaxationOfTreasuries/src/oid-calculator.js` (`render`), `TaxationOfTreasuries/index.html` (`DOCS`, `load`)
**Input:** the rows of [Load OID inputs (8.1)](./8.1_Load_Oid_Inputs.md), the result or reason of [Calculate TIPS OID (8.2)](./8.2_Calculate_Tips_Oid.md); [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) from the user

Show the OID Calculator in the content pane, beside the reference documents in the left pane.

<a id="left-pane"></a>
## Left pane

- Add an entry "OID Calculator", with the tag "TIPS / calculator", after "TIPS OID Reference". Selecting it replaces the content pane with the calculator.

<a id="inputs"></a>
## Inputs

- CUSIP: a text field with the CUSIPs of S2 as suggestions, each with its Maturity Date and coupon. The value is trimmed and upper-cased.
- Show all: a button beside the CUSIP field ([Show all](#show-all)).
- Quantity: a whole number, 1 when the page opens.
- Start Date and End Date: date fields, empty when the page opens.
- Index Ratio: a choice of "Not rounded", selected when the page opens, and "Rounded as Treasury rounds it" ([Calculate TIPS OID §OID](./8.2_Calculate_Tips_Oid.md#oid)).
- Calculate on every change. Until the CUSIP and both dates are entered, show "Enter a CUSIP, a Start Date and an End Date." in place of a result.

<a id="result"></a>
## Result

- Show the TIPS as its coupon, "TIPS due " and its Maturity Date.
- Show a table of rows Dated Date, Start Date and End Date, with the columns Date, Ref CPI and Index Ratio. The Dated Date row shows the Dated Date Ref CPI and an Index Ratio of 1. Ref CPI shows 5 decimal places. Index Ratio shows 5 decimal places when rounded as Treasury rounds it and 8 when not rounded.
- Show Par and the OID, the OID in dollars and cents, as `Par × (Index Ratio of the End Date − Index Ratio of the Start Date used)` with its values.
- When the Start Date is before the Original Issue Date, show in the Start Date row the Start Date used, and the note "The Start Date is before the Original Issue Date. The OID is calculated from the Original Issue Date, " and that date, ".".
- When the End Date is after the latest known Ref CPI, show in the End Date row the date used, and the note "The latest known Ref CPI is that of " and that date, ". The OID is calculated as of that date, not as of the End Date entered."
- For the Start Date used and for the End Date, when its Ref CPI is the NSA value of S8 and the latest known Ref CPI does not replace it, show the note "Treasury has not yet published the Ref CPI of ", the date, ". It is known from the CPI-U of ", the month and year of the value, ", as 31 CFR 356 Appendix B §I.B.3 states for the first day of a month."
- When the OID is negative, show the note "A negative OID is a deflation adjustment."

<a id="show-all"></a>
## Show all

Pressing "Show all" replaces the result with a table of every TIPS outstanding in the range of the Start Date and End Date fields ([Calculate TIPS OID §OID of every TIPS in a range](./8.2_Calculate_Tips_Oid.md#all)), and the button reads "Show one CUSIP". The CUSIP and Quantity fields are hidden. Pressing "Show one CUSIP" restores the fields and the result of one TIPS. Until both dates are entered, show "Enter a Start Date and an End Date." in place of the table.

- Quantity controls above the table:
  - A number field and the button "Set all", which sets the Quantity of every row to the number.
  - A number field and the button "Set checked", which sets the Quantity of the checked rows to the number.
- The table has one row per TIPS, with the columns a checkbox, CUSIP, Maturity Date, Coupon, Quantity, Start Date, End Date and OID.
  - The checkbox of the header checks every row. A click on the checkbox of a row with the Shift key held checks every row from the last row clicked to it.
  - Quantity is a number field of whole numbers of 0 or more, 1 when the table is shown.
  - Start Date and End Date are date fields that show the dates of the range. A Start Date entered before the Original Issue Date of the TIPS is replaced in the field by the Original Issue Date. The End Date field of a row whose End Date is its Maturity Date cannot be changed.
  - A row whose Start Date or End Date the user has changed keeps its dates when the Start Date or End Date field of the range changes, and shows the button "Use range", which sets its Start Date and End Date to those of the range. A row not changed takes the dates of the range.
  - OID shows the OID of the row in dollars and cents. When the End Date of the row is replaced by the latest known Ref CPI, the cell also shows "as of " and the date used. A row with no OID shows the message of its reason in the cell ([Messages](#messages)).
- The last row of the table, Total OID, shows the total OID, and when rows have no OID, "no OID for " the number of rows, " row" for 1 and " rows" otherwise.
- When the End Date of a row is after the latest known Ref CPI, show below the table the note "The latest known Ref CPI is that of " and that date, ". The OID of a row whose End Date is after it is calculated as of that date."
- When the Ref CPI of a date is the NSA value of S8 and the latest known Ref CPI does not replace it, show below the table the note "Treasury has not yet published the Ref CPI of " and the dates, separated by ", ", ". Each is known from the CPI-U of the month three months earlier, as 31 CFR 356 Appendix B §I.B.3 states for the first day of a month."
- The Index Ratio choice applies to every row.

<a id="messages"></a>
## Messages

A reason of [Calculate TIPS OID §Inputs with no result](./8.2_Calculate_Tips_Oid.md#invalid) shows its message in place of a result:

- No row of S2 has the CUSIP: "No TIPS has this CUSIP."
- The Quantity is invalid: "Quantity is a whole number of 1 or more." In the table: "Quantity is a whole number of 0 or more."
- A date is missing: "Enter a Start Date and an End Date."
- The Start Date is after the End Date: "The Start Date is after the End Date."
- The Start Date is after the Maturity Date: "The Start Date is after the Maturity Date, " and the Maturity Date, ".".
- The End Date is before the Original Issue Date: "The End Date is before the Original Issue Date, " and the Original Issue Date, ".".
- The End Date is after the Maturity Date: "The End Date is after the Maturity Date, " and the Maturity Date, ". For a TIPS held to maturity, enter the Maturity Date."
- S3 has no Ref CPI for a date: "There is no Ref CPI for the Start Date." or "There is no Ref CPI for the End Date.", then " Ref CPI values are available from " and the first date of S3, " through " and the latest known Ref CPI date, ".".

<a id="help"></a>
## Help text

Show the help text above the inputs, in these items:

- "OID is the inflation adjustment of a TIPS from a Start Date to an End Date: Par × (Index Ratio of the End Date − Index Ratio of the Start Date)."
- "Quantity is the number of $1,000 par units. A Quantity of 1 is $1,000 par."
- "Start Date is the first day the TIPS is held in the tax year:"
  - "A TIPS bought in the tax year: the Settlement Date of the purchase, not the Trade Date."
  - "A TIPS bought in an earlier year: January 1 of the tax year."
  - "A Start Date before the Original Issue Date of the TIPS is replaced by it."
- "End Date is the last day of the period:"
  - "A TIPS held at the end of the tax year: January 1 of the next year."
  - "A TIPS sold during the tax year: the Settlement Date of the sale."
  - "A TIPS held to maturity: the Maturity Date."
- "Show all lists every TIPS outstanding between the Start Date and the End Date. Each has its own Quantity, Start Date and End Date, and the total OID is the sum of their OID. Set the Quantity of the TIPS not held to 0."
- "Index Ratio, not rounded, is the Ref CPI of the date divided by the Dated Date Ref CPI. Rounded as Treasury rounds it, the ratio is truncated to six decimal places and then rounded to five. Compare the OID of each with Box 8 of the Form 1099-OID."
- "When Treasury has not yet published the Ref CPI of a date:"
  - "The first of a month: the Ref CPI is the CPI-U of the month three months earlier, as 31 CFR 356 Appendix B §I.B.3 states. January 1 takes the CPI-U of October."
  - "Any other date: the OID is calculated as of the latest known Ref CPI, and the result states its date."
