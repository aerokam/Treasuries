# 8.5 Render documents

**Specifies:** [8.5 Render documents](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TAXATIONOFTREASURIES)
**Implemented by:** `TaxationOfTreasuries/index.html` (`DOCS`, `load`, `preprocessFootnotes`, `patchLinks`)
**Input:** the [Document Text](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#document-text) of [Load documents (8.4)](./8.4_Load_Documents.md)

Show the left pane and, in the content pane beside it, the selected document.

<a id="left-pane"></a>
## Left pane

- One button for each entry of `DOCS`, in order, with its label and, beneath it, its tag. The button of the selected entry is highlighted, and the header shows its label.

<a id="content-pane"></a>
## Content pane

- While the file is fetched, show "Loading…". When there is a reason in place of the text, show "Failed to load: " and the reason.
- Show the markdown of the text from its top, or from the anchor of the link that selected it.
- A footnote reference `[^id]` that has a definition `[^id]: text` becomes a superscript number linking to the definition, numbered in the order of first reference, and a definition with no reference is numbered after the others. The definition shows its number in bold, its text and "↩", a link back to its first reference.
- A heading without an id takes one from its text: lower case, punctuation removed, each run of spaces a hyphen. The h2, h3 and h4 headings end with a chip "↑ ToC", which scrolls to the heading `table-of-contents`, or to the top when the document has none.
- A link to an anchor of the document scrolls to it within the content pane. A link to the file of another entry of the left pane selects that entry and scrolls to its anchor, when the link has one.
