# 12.2 Calculate TIPS OID

**Specifies:** the OID Calculator of [Taxation of Treasuries](../index.html)
**Implemented by:** `shared/src/tips-oid.js` (`calculateTipsOid`), `shared/src/ref-cpi.js` (`lookupRefCpi`, `indexRatio`, `roundHalfUp`)
**Input:** the rows of [Load OID inputs (12.1)](./12.1_Load_Oid_Inputs.md); the CUSIP, [Quantity](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#quantity), [Acquisition Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#acquisition-date), [Valuation Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#valuation-date) and Index Ratio rounding from the user

Calculate the [OID](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#oid) of one TIPS from its Acquisition Date to the Valuation Date.

<a id="oid"></a>
## OID

- Take the TIPS as the row of S2 whose `cusip` is the CUSIP.
- Set [Par](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#par) to the Quantity × $1,000.
- Look up the [Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) of the Acquisition Date and of the Valuation Date by the exact date, with `lookupRefCpi`.
- Set the [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio) of each date to its Ref CPI divided by the Dated Date Ref CPI of the TIPS, with `indexRatio`. The user selects one of two roundings for both dates:
  - Not rounded: the ratio as the division gives it.
  - Rounded as Treasury rounds it: truncated to six decimal places and then rounded to five, as [31 CFR 356 Appendix B](../../knowledge/31_CFR_356_Definitions_And_Formulas.md) states for the payments Treasury calculates.
- Set OID to Par × (Index Ratio of the Valuation Date − Index Ratio of the Acquisition Date), rounded to cents. A negative OID is a deflation adjustment.

The calculation uses the dates as entered. A purchase and a sale are each stated at their [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), as the [Acquisition Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#acquisition-date) and [Disposition Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#disposition-date) define.

<a id="latest-ref-cpi"></a>
## Valuation Date after the last Ref CPI

- When the Valuation Date is after the last date of S3, calculate the OID at the last date of S3. The result states that date and that it replaces the Valuation Date.
- When the Acquisition Date is after the last date of S3, there is no result.

<a id="invalid"></a>
## Inputs with no result

The calculation returns a reason, and [Render OID calculator (12.3)](./12.3_Render_Oid_Calculator.md) states it, when:

- no row of S2 has the CUSIP;
- the Quantity is not a whole number of 1 or more;
- a date is missing;
- the Acquisition Date is after the Valuation Date;
- the Acquisition Date is before the [Dated Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#dated-date) of the TIPS, or after its [Maturity Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date);
- the Valuation Date is after the Maturity Date;
- S3 has no Ref CPI for the Acquisition Date, or for a Valuation Date inside the range of S3.
