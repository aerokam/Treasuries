# 12.3 Render OID calculator

**Specifies:** the OID Calculator of [Taxation of Treasuries](../index.html)
**Implemented by:** `TaxationOfTreasuries/src/oid-calculator.js` (`render`), `TaxationOfTreasuries/index.html` (`DOCS`, `load`)
**Input:** the rows of [Load OID inputs (12.1)](./12.1_Load_Oid_Inputs.md), the result or reason of [Calculate TIPS OID (12.2)](./12.2_Calculate_Tips_Oid.md); [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) from the user

Show the OID Calculator in the content pane, beside the reference documents in the left pane.

<a id="left-pane"></a>
## Left pane

- Add an entry "OID Calculator", with the tag "TIPS / calculator", after "TIPS OID Reference". Selecting it replaces the content pane with the calculator.

<a id="inputs"></a>
## Inputs

- CUSIP: a text field with the CUSIPs of S2 as suggestions, each with its Maturity Date and coupon. The value is trimmed and upper-cased.
- Quantity: a whole number, 1 when the page opens.
- Acquisition Date and Valuation Date: date fields, empty when the page opens.
- Index Ratio: a choice of "Not rounded", selected when the page opens, and "Rounded as Treasury rounds it" ([Calculate TIPS OID §OID](./12.2_Calculate_Tips_Oid.md#oid)).
- Calculate on every change. Until the CUSIP and both dates are entered, show "Enter a CUSIP, an Acquisition Date and a Valuation Date." in place of a result.

<a id="result"></a>
## Result

- Show the TIPS as its coupon, "TIPS due " and its Maturity Date.
- Show a table of rows Dated Date, Acquisition Date and Valuation Date, with the columns Date, Ref CPI and Index Ratio. The Dated Date row shows the Dated Date Ref CPI and an Index Ratio of 1. Ref CPI shows 5 decimal places. Index Ratio shows 5 decimal places when rounded as Treasury rounds it and 8 when not rounded.
- Show Par and the OID, the OID in dollars and cents, as `Par × (Index Ratio of the Valuation Date − Index Ratio of the Acquisition Date)` with its values.
- When the Valuation Date is after the last date of S3, show in the Valuation Date row the date used, and the note "Ref CPI is published through " and the last date of S3, ". The OID is calculated as of that date, not as of the Valuation Date entered."
- When the OID is negative, show the note "A negative OID is a deflation adjustment."

<a id="messages"></a>
## Messages

A reason of [Calculate TIPS OID §Inputs with no result](./12.2_Calculate_Tips_Oid.md#invalid) shows its message in place of a result:

- No row of S2 has the CUSIP: "No TIPS has this CUSIP."
- The Quantity is invalid: "Quantity is a whole number of 1 or more."
- A date is missing: "Enter an Acquisition Date and a Valuation Date."
- The Acquisition Date is after the Valuation Date: "The Acquisition Date is after the Valuation Date."
- The Acquisition Date is before the Dated Date: "The Acquisition Date is before the Dated Date, " and the Dated Date, ".".
- The Acquisition Date is after the Maturity Date: "The Acquisition Date is after the Maturity Date, " and the Maturity Date, ".".
- The Valuation Date is after the Maturity Date: "The Valuation Date is after the Maturity Date, " and the Maturity Date, ". For a TIPS held to maturity, enter the Maturity Date."
- S3 has no Ref CPI for a date: "There is no Ref CPI for the Acquisition Date." or "There is no Ref CPI for the Valuation Date.", then " Ref CPI is published from " and the first date of S3, " through " and the last date of S3, ".".

<a id="help"></a>
## Help text

Show the help text above the inputs, in these items:

- "OID is the inflation adjustment of a TIPS from its Acquisition Date to a Valuation Date: Par × (Index Ratio of the Valuation Date − Index Ratio of the Acquisition Date)."
- "Quantity is the number of $1,000 par units. A Quantity of 1 is $1,000 par."
- "Acquisition Date is the Settlement Date of the purchase, not the Trade Date. For a tax year after the year of purchase, enter January 1 of that tax year."
- "Valuation Date is the date the OID is calculated for:"
  - "A TIPS held at the end of the tax year: January 1 of the next year."
  - "A TIPS sold during the tax year: the Settlement Date of the sale."
  - "A TIPS held to maturity: the Maturity Date."
- "Index Ratio, not rounded, is the Ref CPI of the date divided by the Dated Date Ref CPI. Rounded as Treasury rounds it, the ratio is truncated to six decimal places and then rounded to five. Compare the OID of each with Box 8 of the Form 1099-OID."
- "When Ref CPI is not yet published for the Valuation Date, the OID is calculated as of the last date with a published Ref CPI, and the result states that date."
