# 4.4 Rebalance ladder

**Specifies:** [4.4 Rebalance ladder](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_TLM_REBALANCE) and the three processes it decomposes into  
**Implemented by:** `TipsLadderManager/index.html`, `TipsLadderManager/src/rebalance-lib.js`, `TipsLadderManager/src/before-state-lib.js`  
**Reference:** [2.0 TIPS Ladders](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md): [Within-Year Allocation Policy](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#within-year-allocation-policy), [Retained Bracket Excess](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#retained-bracket-excess)  
**Reference:** [3.0 TIPS Ladder Rebalancing](./3.0_TIPS_Ladder_Rebalancing.md): [Per-Year DARA from Portfolio](./3.0_TIPS_Ladder_Rebalancing.md#per-year-dara-from-portfolio), [Before-State Preview and Bracket-Year Excess Detection](./3.0_TIPS_Ladder_Rebalancing.md#before-state-preview-and-bracket-year-excess-detection), [Funding the rebalance](./3.0_TIPS_Ladder_Rebalancing.md#funding-the-rebalance), [DARA Reference Date](./3.0_TIPS_Ladder_Rebalancing.md#dara-reference-date), [Bracket Identification Rules](./3.0_TIPS_Ladder_Rebalancing.md#bracket-identification-rules)

Two of the three processes run independently of each other and of Run, the moment holdings load:
4.4.1 resolves what per-year target the eventual Run will size to, and 4.4.2 values the holdings as
they stand today for immediate display — [architecturally decoupled](#preview-before-state) from
the sizing engine, not a second copy of it. 4.4.3 runs only when the user clicks Rebalance Ladder,
against whichever per-year target is on the form by then (4.4.1's resolution, or anything the
holder has since edited). The sizing engine — [ladder-core.js
(M5)](../../knowledge/SharedModules.md#m5) — is specified in [SharedModules](../../knowledge/SharedModules.md#m5), and the rules it applies in [2.0 TIPS Ladders](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md).

## Processes

<a id="derive-per-year-dara-from-holdings-or-dara-plan"></a>
### 4.4.1 Derive per-year DARA from holdings or DARA plan

**Implemented by:** `rebalance-lib.js#deriveHoldingsYearRange`, `#inferFirstYearFromHoldings`, `#inferLastYearFromHoldings`, `#computePortfolioARAByYear`, `#getGapYears`, `#hasGapCoverageIntent`, `#derivePerYearDara`, `index.html#_initRebalDaraFromPortfolio`, `#_applyDaraBasis`

Read [Holdings](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#holdings) and [TIPS Market
Data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-market-data), and a loaded [DARA
Plan](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#dara-plan) where one was imported, and produce [Per-Year
DARA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#per-year-dara):

- Infer the ladder's first and last year from the holdings, when neither was loaded
  from a DARA Plan's range.
- A loaded [DARA Plan](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#dara-plan) is honored exactly as stated,
  for every year it names, restated to the Ref CPI date now in effect (3.0 §DARA Reference Date) —
  no derivation from holdings, no self-financing scale, since the holder's stated figure is not
  the engine's to revise (3.0 §Funding the rebalance).
- With no DARA Plan loaded, derive Per-Year DARA from the current portfolio instead (3.0 §Per-Year DARA from
  Portfolio): every year's raw [ARA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ara) from current holdings,
  a held year's P+I plus incoming later-maturity interest, an empty year's incoming
  later-maturity interest alone.
  - A gap year takes 0 unless the ladder shows genuine intent to cover it — a held excess at the
    Upper Bracket year, the same `findSpikes` signal 4.4.2's bracket-candidate detection uses
    (3.0 §Per-Year DARA from Portfolio, "Ruling, 2026-09-30"). With that intent present, a gap year
    instead extends the local slope of its nearer held neighbor.
  - A bracket-candidate year flagged as a spike against the other held years' shape has its
    per-year entry left unset, pending the holder's review (3.0 §Before-State Preview and
    Bracket-Year Excess Detection — the flagging is [4.4.2](#preview-before-state)'s
    independent computation; this process only leaves the slot open for it).

**Output:** [Per-Year DARA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#per-year-dara), to [Rebalance the ladder (4.4.3)](#rebalance-the-ladder).

<a id="preview-before-state"></a>
### 4.4.2 Preview before-state

**Implemented by:** `before-state-lib.js#computeBeforeState`, `#detectBracketFlags`, `#getActiveLowerBracketYear`, `#getLowerBracketCandidateYears`

Read [Holdings](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#holdings) and [TIPS Market
Data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-market-data) and produce the before-state preview, the
moment holdings load — never gated behind Run, and never calling the sizing engine: a standalone
valuation of what is held today, architecturally decoupled from [Rebalance the ladder
(4.4.3)](#rebalance-the-ladder) (3.0 §Before-State Preview and Bracket-Year Excess Detection):

- Value every funded year's current Quantity, Cost and Amount directly from held quantities and
  market data — no DARA, no duration matching.
- Flag a candidate bracket year (the lower bracket candidates, the Active Lower Bracket, the Upper
  Bracket, the Future 30Y cover pair) as excess when its raw ARA stands off a curve fitted through
  every held funded year's raw ARA — `shape-math.js#findSpikes` — rather than merely exceeding a
  single number.
- A flagged year is pre-filled with the curve's value and shown with its excess, rather than
  left blank — an amber indicator marks the figure as a guess pending the holder's review, not a
  confirmed one.

**Output:** the before-state preview, to [Render and export (4.5)](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TIPSLADDERMANAGER).

<a id="rebalance-the-ladder"></a>
### 4.4.3 Rebalance the ladder

**Implemented by:** `rebalance-lib.js#runFundedRebalance`, `#runRebalance`, `#inferScaledDARAFromPortfolio`

When the user clicks Rebalance Ladder: read [Per-Year DARA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#per-year-dara),
[Holdings](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#holdings) and [TIPS Market
Data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-market-data), and produce the rebalance result:

- Resolve the bond set and size the ladder against [Per-Year
  DARA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#per-year-dara) — [ladder-core.js
  (M5)](../../knowledge/SharedModules.md#m5)'s `selectLadderBonds`/`sizeLadder`, the identical
  engine [Build the ladder (4.3.2)](./4.3_Build_Ladder.md#build-the-ladder) calls.
- When [Per-Year DARA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#per-year-dara) is still exactly the derived Per-Year DARA [4.4.1](#derive-per-year-dara-from-holdings-or-dara-plan) produced, not edited since load,
  and a gap or Future 30Y year is in range: re-run the sizing at a single scale factor, binary
  searched to the highest level at which the plan pays for itself (net cash ≥ 0) — 3.0 §Funding the
  rebalance. A stated plan, or a derived Per-Year DARA the holder has since edited, is never scaled.
- Within each funded year, resolve which held CUSIP sells and which target CUSIP buys —
  [`allocation-policy.js`'s `rankForYear`/`levelValues`](#within-year-order), per the maturity/SA-yield/equal-value
  ordering the holder's allocation policy selects (2.0 §Within-Year Allocation Policy). A bracket or
  cover year's excess CUSIP is resolved separately, by duration matching (2.0 §Retained Bracket
  Excess), never by this ranking.
- Diff the sized target against current holdings into buy/sell quantities per CUSIP, and sum the
  net cash the whole set of trades requires.
- Build one Before/After result row per held or target CUSIP, carrying the same named Amount/Cost
  figures [Build the ladder (4.3.2)](./4.3_Build_Ladder.md#build-the-ladder) computes, both before
  and after the trade (2.0 §Algorithm, §Amount of a Gap Year and a Future 30Y Year).
- Compute the weighted average modified duration, yield and SA yield across every row's after-trade
  market value ([Weighted Modified Duration](../../knowledge/SharedModules.md#weighted-modified-duration)).

**Output:** the rebalance result, to [Render and export (4.5)](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TIPSLADDERMANAGER).

<a id="priority-order-picker"></a>
## Priority-order picker

`#rank-picker-overlay` and `#rank-picker-modal` are the modal of the per-year override of the [Within-Year Allocation Policy](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#within-year-allocation-policy). The "Set priority order…" choice of the Allocation policy dropdown (`#rebal-alloc-policy`, [4.5 §Row 2](./4.5_Render_And_Export.md#row-2)) opens it, and nothing else does. The choice uses the mousedown, change and click handling of the "Select maturities…" choice of the Maturity preference dropdown (`#build-maturity`), applied to `#rebal-alloc-policy`. The modal has the frame of [modal.js (M8)](../../knowledge/SharedModules.md#m8) and the same close, cancel and apply buttons as the maturity picker. One modal covers every year in range.

Each year is a row of **chips**, one per candidate or held CUSIP in the current priority order, with **◀ / ▶** buttons on each chip to reorder it. `#rank-picker-desc` states the convention: "Left = preferred to hold/buy first. Right = sold first if this year needs to shrink." It adds that under Equal split a not-held maturity can rank ahead of a held one ([2.0 §Within-Year Allocation Policy](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#within-year-allocation-policy)), which is not an error. A year left untouched follows what the global policy currently produces for it (`rankForYear`, seeded through `_rankPickerYearOrder`), so opening the modal and applying with no edits changes nothing. Apply writes to `_yearRankOverrides`. Choosing a different named global policy clears every override.

<a id="within-year-order"></a>
## Within-year order (`allocation-policy.js`)

Within-year target and sell ordering of a Rebalance (`TipsLadderManager/src/allocation-policy.js`). The policies are defined in [2.0 §Within-Year Allocation Policy](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#within-year-allocation-policy), and the wiring into the per-year sweep is in [3.0 §Target CUSIP resolution](./3.0_TIPS_Ladder_Rebalancing.md#target-cusip-resolution). The functions are pure, with no ladder state and no I/O. The excess CUSIP of a bracket, gap or cover year never comes from here ([2.0 §Duration Matching (Brackets)](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#duration-matching-brackets) and [§Retained Bracket Excess](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#retained-bracket-excess) specify that identification). A bracket year that is also a funded year calls `rankForYear` for its funded need, with `policy` forced to `'maturity'` whatever the caller's `allocationPolicy` ([2.0 §Within-Year Allocation Policy](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#within-year-allocation-policy), Scope).

#### `rankForYear({ candidates?, held?, piMap?, tipsMarketData?, policy?, rankOverride?, maturityPref? })`

**Inputs:**
- `candidates` — the `yearTipsListMap[year]` entries of the year (outstanding TIPS, driven by `maturityPref` and `yearOverrides`, [2.0 §Maturity Selection Within a Funded Year](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#maturity-selection)).
- `held` — the current holdings of the year, which may include maturities no longer in `candidates`.
- `piMap` — `{ cusip → piPerBond }` for every held CUSIP (`'equal'` uses it to value each holding).
- `tipsMarketData` — `'saYield'` uses it to look up the SA yield of each candidate.
- `policy` — `'equal'` (default), `'maturity'` or `'saYield'`.
- `rankOverride` — an explicit `cusip[]`, highest priority first (the per-year override). When present it replaces the sort of the policy.
- `maturityPref` — `'last'` (default), `'first'`, `'semiannual'`, `'all'` or `'select'`. Only its direction (`'last'` or `'first'`) matters here, as the tie-break of the policies.

**Returns:** an array of `{ cusip, held }` over the union of `candidates` and `held`, highest buy and hold priority first. It does not change the quantities of `held`: the ranking decides order only, never whether a trade happens ([2.0 §Within-Year Allocation Policy](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#within-year-allocation-policy)) and never the direction of a trade, which the net change in the total of the year sets ([3.0 §Target CUSIP resolution](./3.0_TIPS_Ladder_Rebalancing.md#target-cusip-resolution), Direction of the trades of a funded year).

**`rankOverride`:** every override CUSIP in the union is placed first, in the order of the override. Any candidate or held CUSIP the override does not name (for example after `maturityPref` changed) is appended afterward in candidate order, so an override does not drop a CUSIP from consideration.

**`dir` (maturity tie-break direction):** `dir = maturityPref === 'first' ? -1 : 1`. `+1` sorts the latest-maturing first (the default, and the meaning of `'last'`) and `-1` the earliest first. It applies to the primary sort of `'maturity'`, to `'saYield'` when both SA yields are missing, and to `'equal'` when two candidates have the same held value.

**`'maturity'`:** `sort((a, b) => dir * (maturityOf(b) - maturityOf(a)))`.

**`'saYield'`:** sorts by `tipsMarketData.get(cusip).saYield`, highest first. A CUSIP with no SA yield ranks after one that has a value (`av == null → 1`, `bv == null → -1`). When both are missing, the maturity direction orders them. Equal SA yields keep their order in the union (candidates, then held).

**`'equal'`:** sorts by the current held value, lowest first, `(heldByCusip.get(cusip)?.qty ?? 0) × (piMap[cusip] ?? 0)`. A candidate not held has value 0 and ranks first. Equal values are ordered by the maturity direction. This ranking decides only which CUSIP is `rank[0]` in a run, and the levelling of the values over a trade is `levelValues`.

#### `levelValues(values, needed)`

**Inputs:** `values` is `Map<cusip, currentPIValue>`, and `needed` is the target total P+I value of the set after the trade.

**Returns:** a new `Map<cusip, leveledPIValue>` that sums to `needed`.

Growth (`needed > Σ values`) is added to the member with the lowest value, and shrinkage is taken from the member with the highest value. Members tied at the extreme move together. The set is merged into groups of equal value: at each step the value of the extreme group moves toward the value of the next group, either using up the remaining amount on the way or reaching that group and merging into it, until the amount is used up or one group remains. The remainder then divides evenly over that last group.

`'maturity'` and `'saYield'` are fixed preference orders, so draining the least preferred CUSIP fully before the next is correct. `'equal'` has no fixed order and keeps the maturities near parity. Draining a shrinkage onto the CUSIP that ranked last at the start of the trade can pass parity and reverse which CUSIP is larger. With two maturities held at values 7 and 9 and a shrink to a total of 12, draining the 9-side to 5 leaves it below the 7-side. `levelValues` gives 6 and 6.

<a id="credited-years"></a>
## Credited years

**Rebalance years.** Every funded year credited with any of these amounts is in `rebalYearSet`, so the credits reduce the funded-year quantity and trigger selling, which prevents an Amount After above the DARA. The target-sizing `needed` subtracts the same three amounts, so a Build, export, import and rebalance round trip has zero trades through these years.

**PLI-zeroed year sell-all rule.** When Pre-Ladder Interest fully covers the funded need of a year, the target CUSIP quantity is set to 0 and all older TIPS in that year (when the portfolio holds several TIPS maturing the same year) are sold to 0. Otherwise those holdings remain and their principal and interest appear as income in the "Amount After" column of that year.
