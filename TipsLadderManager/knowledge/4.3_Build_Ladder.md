# 4.3 Build ladder

**Specifies:** [4.3 Build ladder](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_TLM_BUILD) and the two processes it decomposes into  
**Implemented by:** `TipsLadderManager/index.html`, `TipsLadderManager/src/build-lib.js`

Building is a thin shell around the sizing engine both Build and Rebalance share: 4.3.1 resolves
what per-year target to size to, 4.3.2 sizes against it and shapes the result for [Render and
export (4.5)](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TIPSLADDERMANAGER). The engine itself — [ladder-core.js
(M5)](../../knowledge/SharedModules.md#m5) — is specified at [2.0 TIPS Ladders](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md),
not here or in M5's registry entry; this spec covers only what Build's code does around it.

## Processes

<a id="resolve-per-year-dara"></a>
### 4.3.1 Resolve per-year DARA

**Implemented by:** `index.html#_runBuildFromForm`, `#getDaraByYear`, `#_getDefaultDara`, `#_effectiveYearRange`

Read the ladder parameters (the scalar DARA field, First/Last Year or a picked maturity range, and
the per-year DARA panel — populated by hand or by a loaded [DARA Plan](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#dara-plan))
and produce [Per-Year DARA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#per-year-dara):

- If the per-year panel holds any value — typed by hand or carried in from a loaded DARA Plan —
  every year in range takes its panel value, a blank or cleared entry reading as 0 rather than
  falling back to the scalar.
- Otherwise every year in range takes the scalar DARA field's value, flat.
- When the maturity picker ("Select maturities…") is in use: any in-range year with no explicit
  pick is forced to 0, regardless of whichever of the two rules above would otherwise apply — a
  year the holder didn't choose a maturity for is a year they didn't choose to fund.

A First Year left blank takes the Default First Year (DD §5.1), and the First Year choices begin
at it. `runBuild` takes the same default when called without a first year, and keeps one it is
given unless that year precedes the Default First Year, which it then takes. A TIPS maturing no more
than `FIRST_YEAR_BUFFER_MONTHS` months after the Settlement Date is not chosen for a funded year
under any Maturity preference (2.0 §Maturity Selection Within a Funded Year; DD §5.1).

**Output:** [Per-Year DARA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#per-year-dara), to [Build the ladder (4.3.2)](#build-the-ladder).

<a id="build-the-ladder"></a>
### 4.3.2 Build the ladder

**Implemented by:** `build-lib.js#runBuild`

Read [Per-Year DARA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#per-year-dara) and [TIPS Market
Data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-market-data) and produce the build result:

- Resolve the bond set — [ladder-core.js (M5)](../../knowledge/SharedModules.md#m5)'s
  `selectLadderBonds`, per the maturity preference, coupon preference and any per-year maturity
  override the ladder parameters carry (2.0 §Maturity Selection Within a Funded Year).
- Size the ladder against that bond set and [Per-Year DARA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#per-year-dara)
  — M5's `sizeLadder` (2.0 §Algorithm), the identical engine [Rebalance ladder
  (4.4.3)](./4.4_Rebalance_Ladder.md#rebalance-the-ladder) calls. Never passes Available Cash or a
  non-default settlement-year coupon-counting choice: both are Rebalance-only (2.0 §Available Cash),
  and a loaded file's values for either must not apply unseen to a build.
- For each year in range, build one output row per funded sub-rung from `sizeLadder`'s result:
  CUSIP, maturity, funded-year quantity, excess quantity, the Funded Year Amount and Cost, the Excess Cost, and the Amount
  of each gap year and Future 30Y year, figures 2.0's named formulas define (§Algorithm, §Amount of a Gap Year and a Future 30Y Year) — [bond-math.js
  (M3)](../../knowledge/SharedModules.md#m3)'s `bondCalcs`/`calculateMDuration` price each row.
  - A year with more than one funded TIPS (2.0 §Multi-TIPS Funded-Year Split) emits one row per
    sub-rung; the year-level Amount, LMI and credit figures ride the first row only.
  - A year carrying bracket or Future 30Y cover excess rides that excess on its representative
    row, never split across sub-rungs.
- Compute the weighted average modified duration, yield and SA yield across every row's market
  value (4.0 §Weighted Modified Duration).

**Output:** the build result, to [Render and export (4.5)](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TIPSLADDERMANAGER).
