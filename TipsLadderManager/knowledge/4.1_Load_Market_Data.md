# 4.1 Load market data

**Specifies:** [4.1 Load market data](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_TLM_LOAD) and the four processes it decomposes into  
**Implemented by:** `shared/src/market-data.js`, `TipsLadderManager/src/rebalance-lib.js`

TipsLadderManager reads Market quotes (S7) today. FedInvest prices (S1) is a dormant, code-only
cross-check path — [4.1.2](#fetch-and-parse-fedinvest-prices) — never selected in production; it
is documented below but not drawn on [4.1's Level 3 diagram](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_TLM_LOAD).

<a id="source-selection"></a>
## Source selection

`loadMarketData()` (`shared/src/market-data.js`) is the one entry point. The source selector `YIELD_SOURCE` is private to that module and every consumer calls the function, TipsLadderManager and [Load TIPS data (6.1)](../../TipsReference/knowledge/6.1_Load_TIPS_Data.md) alike, so a consumer cannot stay on the dormant source when another moves on. No UI control selects FedInvest prices (S1). Setting `YIELD_SOURCE` to `'fedinvest'` selects it for a local cross-check.

The function returns the raw rows and the three dates whose derivation depends on the source (`settleDateStr`, `tradeDateStr`, `defaultRefCpiDateStr`):

- Market quotes (S7): the settlement date is the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) plus one bond trading day, and the trade date is the Download Date ([4.1.1](#fetch-and-parse-market-quotes)).
- FedInvest prices (S1): the settlement date is T+0 ([Download FedInvest prices §Determine settlement date](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#determine-settlement-date)) and is also the trade date ([4.1.2](#fetch-and-parse-fedinvest-prices)).
- `defaultRefCpiDateStr` is the next bond trading day after the trade date, whichever source is active ([3.0 §Ref CPI Date](./3.0_TIPS_Ladder_Rebalancing.md#ref-cpi-date)).

`fetchMarketQuotesData()` and `fetchTipsData()` return rows of one shape, `{ settlementDate, cusip, maturity, coupon, datedDateRefCpi, price, yield }`, so the ladder math (`buildTipsMarketData`, `ladder-core.js`) does not depend on the source. Callers build the TIPS market data themselves ([4.1.4](#build-tips-market-data)), because `market-data.js` does not import `rebalance-lib.js`.

A script that calculates ladder numbers outside the app loads through `market-data.js`: it calls `loadMarketData()`, or `fetchMarketQuotesData()` with `fetch` shimmed to serve local copies when offline, and does not parse a store itself. A script that opens `YieldsFromFedInvestPrices.csv` reads the dormant source while the app reads Market quotes (S7), and every figure it produces differs from the app.

## Processes

<a id="fetch-and-parse-market-quotes"></a>
### 4.1.1 Fetch and parse market quotes

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/market-data.js#fetchMarketQuotesData`, `shared/src/market-quotes.js#parseTipsQuotes`, `shared/src/market-quotes.js#parseDownloadDate`, `shared/src/market-quotes.js#downloadDateIso`, `shared/src/market-data.js#nextBondTradingDay`, `shared/src/bond-math.js#yieldFromPrice`

Read [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7) and the [TIPS reference data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s2) and [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday) that [Fetch auxiliary TIPS data (4.1.3)](#fetch-auxiliary-tips-data) produces, and produce the [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields):

- Read the `Date downloaded` footer line and set the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) to its date.
- Set the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) to the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) advanced one bond trading day past any [Bond Holiday](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday) (T+1) — a real broker trade settlement.
- For each TIPS row [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7) states (one per CUSIP, first occurrence wins):
  - Set the [Maturity Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date) to the row's value, or to [TIPS reference data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s2)'s value for the CUSIP when the row states none.
  - Set the dated date [Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) to [TIPS reference data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s2)'s value for the CUSIP.
  - If there's no usable ask [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price), or no [Maturity Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date): the security has no [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield).
  - Otherwise: calculate the [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) from the ask [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) at the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) — the same yield-from-price calculation every other source and app in the portal uses ([bond-math.js (M3)](../../knowledge/SharedModules.md#m3)). The quoted yield column is not read.

**Output:** the [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields), to [Build TIPS market data (4.1.4)](#build-tips-market-data).

<a id="fetch-and-parse-fedinvest-prices"></a>
### 4.1.2 Fetch and parse FedInvest prices

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/market-data.js#fetchTipsData`, `shared/src/csv.js#parseCsv`

**Status: dormant.** This code path exists and works, but `shared/src/market-data.js`'s source
selector never selects it in production — TipsLadderManager reads Market quotes (S7) exclusively
([4.1.1](#fetch-and-parse-market-quotes)). It is kept as a manual cross-check, and specified here for
that reason, but is not part of the diagram at [4.1's Level 3](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_TLM_LOAD). FedInvest prices (S1) is also the only source with per-CUSIP prices of a past trading day: [Download FedInvest prices §A past trading day](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md) states the script that writes them, and [Explore past markets (4.6)](./4.6_Explore_Past_Markets.md) reads such files.

Read [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1) and produce the [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields):

- Set the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) to the store's first line.
- Drop every row whose type is not TIPS.
- For each kept row: set the [Maturity Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date), [Coupon Rate](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#coupon-rate), dated date [Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi), [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) and [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) to the values the row itself already states — [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1) was written with the [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) already calculated ([1.1.3 Calculate yields](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#calculate-yields)).

**Output:** the [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields), to [Build TIPS market data (4.1.4)](#build-tips-market-data).

<a id="fetch-auxiliary-tips-data"></a>
### 4.1.3 Fetch auxiliary TIPS data

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/market-data.js#fetchAuxTipsData`, `shared/src/market-data.js#parseTipsRefRows`, `shared/src/csv.js#parseCsv`, `shared/src/settlement.js#parseHolidaySet`

Read [Ref CPI (S3)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s3), [TIPS reference data (S2)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s2), [SA and SAO yields (S10)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s10) and [Bond holidays (S16)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s16), and produce the retrieved form of each:

- Read [Ref CPI (S3)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s3) and [TIPS reference data (S2)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s2) in full; either failing to load stops [Load market data (4.1)](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_TLM_LOAD) entirely, since neither source is optional.
- Read [SA and SAO yields (S10)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s10), keeping only the [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) column — the ask and SAO yield columns are parsed but not used here.
- Read [Bond holidays (S16)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s16), on a three-second timeout.
  - If it cannot be read in time: produce an empty [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday) set — a settlement-date calculation then falls back to skipping weekends alone.

**Output:** [TIPS reference data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s2) and [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), to [Fetch and parse market quotes (4.1.1)](#fetch-and-parse-market-quotes); [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), also to the rest of the app directly, for coupon and payment-date rolling elsewhere; [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield), to [Build TIPS market data (4.1.4)](#build-tips-market-data); [Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi), to the rest of the app directly, for Ref CPI lookups elsewhere.

<a id="build-tips-market-data"></a>
### 4.1.4 Build TIPS market data

**Implemented by:** `TipsLadderManager/src/rebalance-lib.js#buildTipsMarketData`

Read the [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields) that [Fetch and parse market quotes (4.1.1)](#fetch-and-parse-market-quotes) produced, and the [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) that [Fetch auxiliary TIPS data (4.1.3)](#fetch-auxiliary-tips-data) produced, and produce [TIPS Market Data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-market-data):

- For each TIPS: keep its CUSIP, [Maturity Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date), [Coupon Rate](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#coupon-rate), dated date [Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi), [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) and [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) unchanged, and attach its [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield), or none where [SA and SAO yields (S10)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s10) has no entry for that CUSIP.

The app's "Mkt" tab (the TIPS Ref/Mkt popup, `TipsLadderManager/index.html#_showTipsRefModal`) displays this table directly, titled "TIPS Mkt Data", adding an Index Ratio and Adjusted Price computed at display time from the currently selected Ref CPI date. The Index Ratio is unrounded and shown to nine decimal places; the Adjusted Price is the Price times that Index Ratio, shown to three.

**Output:** [TIPS Market Data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-market-data), to [Import holdings and DARA plan (4.2)](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_TLM_IMPORT), [Build ladder (4.3)](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TIPSLADDERMANAGER) and [Rebalance ladder (4.4)](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TIPSLADDERMANAGER).
