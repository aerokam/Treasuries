# 3.1 Data Pipeline (TIPS Logic)

## 1.0 Data Context

The TIPS data that the app loads comes from three kinds of store:

- [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7), the default source of prices and yields.
- [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1), the dormant source.
- [TIPS reference data (S2)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s2), [Ref CPI (S3)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s3), [SA and SAO yields (S10)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s10) and [Bond holidays (S16)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s16), which the app reads for either price source.

The data flow diagrams are [Level 2 TipsLadderManager](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TIPSLADDERMANAGER) and [Level 3 Load market data](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_TLM_LOAD).

## 3.0 Data Elements

- **[Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield)**: the yield to maturity that the ladder uses for discounting.
- **Dated date [Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi)**: the Ref CPI of the dated date of a TIPS, which gives its [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio). Market quotes (S7) does not carry it, so the app takes it from TIPS reference data (S2). For FedInvest prices (S1) it is the `datedDateCpi` column, which [1.1.2](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#select-prices-and-add-tips-reference-data) took from TIPS reference data (S2).

## 4.0 Yield Sources

The app prices from Market quotes (S7). The UI does not name the source. The info strip (`#info-source`) shows `Trade: MM/DD/YYYY · Settle: MM/DD/YYYY`: the trade date is the as-of date of the data, and the settlement date is the next bond trading day ([4.5 §Row 5](./4.5_Render_And_Export.md#info-strip), [3.0 §Ref CPI Date](./3.0_TIPS_Ladder_Rebalancing.md#ref-cpi-date)). The Ref CPI date is the settlement date and is not shown or set in either mode.

`fetchMarketQuotesData()` (Market quotes (S7)) and `fetchTipsData()` (FedInvest prices (S1)) in `shared/src/market-data.js` return rows of one shape, `{ settlementDate, cusip, maturity, coupon, datedDateRefCpi, price, yield }`, so the ladder math (`buildTipsMarketData`, `ladder-core.js`) does not depend on the source.

**One entry point: `loadMarketData()` (`shared/src/market-data.js`).** The source selector `YIELD_SOURCE` is private to that module and every consumer calls the function, TipsLadderManager and TipsReference alike, so a consumer cannot be left on the dormant source when another moves on. The function returns the raw rows and the three dates whose derivation depends on the source (`settleDateStr`, `tradeDateStr`, `defaultRefCpiDateStr`):

- FedInvest: the settlement date is T+0 ([1.1.1](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#determine-settlement-date)) and is also the trade date.
- Market quotes: the settlement date is T+1 from the download date, and the trade date is the download date.
- `defaultRefCpiDateStr` is the next bond trading day after the trade date.

Callers build the TIPS market data themselves (`buildTipsMarketData`, `rebalance-lib.js`), because `market-data.js` is a leaf and does not import an orchestrator. It is in `shared/src/` so that other apps share it without repeating the source decision ([Load TIPS data (6.1)](../../TipsReference/knowledge/6.1_Load_TIPS_Data.md)).

**A script that calculates ladder numbers outside the app loads through `market-data.js`.** A script that opens `YieldsFromFedInvestPrices.csv` itself reads the dormant source while the app reads Market quotes, and every figure it produces differs from the app. It calls `loadMarketData()` (or `fetchMarketQuotesData()`, with `fetch` shimmed to serve local copies when offline) and does not parse the file.

**FedInvest is dormant.** No UI control selects it. Setting `YIELD_SOURCE` to `'fedinvest'` in `shared/src/market-data.js` selects it for a local cross-check ([4.1.2](./4.1_Load_Market_Data.md#fetch-and-parse-fedinvest-prices)). FedInvest is also the only source with per-CUSIP prices for a past date, and `scripts/getFedInvestPricesForDate.js` writes a file in the composition of S1 for a past trading day ([1.1 §A past trading day](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md)). A year-over-year test builds a ladder in the market of a year ago, exports it, and loads the export against today's data.

| | **Market quotes (S7), default** | **FedInvest prices (S1), dormant** |
|---|---|---|
| Price | the ask price | the `BUY` price, else `SELL`, else `END OF DAY` ([1.1.2](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#select-prices-and-add-tips-reference-data)) |
| Yield | calculated from the ask price by `yieldFromPrice()` (`shared/src/bond-math.js`) | read from the `yield` column, which [1.1.3](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#calculate-yields) calculated with the same `yieldFromPrice()` |
| Settlement date | T+1 from the download date ([3.1.6](../../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md#determine-settlement-date)) | T+0, the date that FedInvest states its prices are for ([1.1.1](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#determine-settlement-date)) |
| Use | the default: the ask price is what a real order pays | the cross-check, and the prices of a past trading day |

**The yield is calculated from the price, and the quoted yield column is not read.** `yieldFromPrice()` gives a more accurate yield for a TIPS than the quoted ask yield to maturity (measured in 2026-07). YieldCurves does the same for TIPS (`tipsYieldsFromPrices` in `shared/src/tips-yields.js`).

**Two settlement dates.** `settlementDate` (it sets the yield, duration and Index Ratio calculations, and depends on the source as above) is a different date from the settlement date of the accrued interest in the Trade Ticket. The Trade Ticket uses the next bond trading day after the trade date whichever source is active (`ttSettlementDate` in `index.html`, [4.5.3 §Trade Ticket](./4.5.3_Show_Trade_Ticket.md#trade-ticket)). Using one for the other gives a wrong accrued interest.

**Bond-calendar primitives are in `shared/src/settlement.js`.** `nextBondTradingDay(isoDateStr, holidaySet)` (`market-data.js`, a wrapper for ISO strings) and `actualPaymentDate(d, holidaySet)` both build on `nextBusinessDay` and `parseHolidaySet` of `settlement.js`, the one implementation of weekend and SIFMA holiday skipping, which YieldsMonitor, SeasonalAdjustments and YieldCurves also use. The two functions differ:

- `nextBusinessDay` always advances at least one day.
- `actualPaymentDate` returns the date unchanged when it is a trading day, and rolls forward only when it is not.

Every coupon and principal payment date, the Cash Flow Calendar ([4.5.4 §Cash Flow Calendar](./4.5.4_Show_Cash_Flow_Calendar.md#cash-flow-calendar)) and the settlement-year LMI ([2.0 §Settlement-Year Coupon Treatment](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#settlement-year-coupon-treatment)) use `actualPaymentDate`, because a payment on a trading day is paid on that day.
