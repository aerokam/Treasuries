<a id="trade-ticket"></a>
## Trade Ticket

A separate modal (`#trade-ticket-overlay`/`#trade-ticket-modal` in `index.html`), triggered by the
`Trade Ticket` button in Row 2 (6.0 §Form Card Layout). Not COLS-driven — built directly from
`buildDetails`/`rebalDetails` by `buildTradeTicketHTML(mode)`, independent of the main table.
Draggable/resizable (6.0 §Modal Frame Standard).

**Scope:** Build shows the full purchase list (`fundedYearQty + excessQty` per CUSIP, qty > 0).
Rebalance splits into **Buys** and **Sells** sections (`qtyAfter − qtyBefore` per CUSIP, sign
determines section); named "Trade Ticket" rather than "Purchase List" because Rebalance's list is
buys *and* sells, not purchases only.

**Columns:** CUSIP, Maturity, Qty, Cost, Accrued Interest, **Total Cost** (Build and Buys) /
**Total Proceeds** (Sells — identical formula, proceeds framing). Each row's Qty is always
positive; Sells price off `Math.abs(delta)` so Cost/Accrued/Total read as positive proceeds.

```
cost              = qty × costPerBond                                    // DD Cost per TIPS
accruedPerBond    = accruedInterestAdjusted(coupon, ttSettlementDate, bond.maturity,   // DD Accrued Interest (Adjusted):
                      Ref CPI of ttSettlementDate, datedDateRefCpi)                      // accruedInterest (nominal) / 100 × the Index Ratio of Treasury's rule × 1,000,
                                                                                         // rounded to 5 decimal places
accruedInterest   = qty × accruedPerBond
totalCost         = cost + accruedInterest
```

`ttSettlementDate` is `localDate(_defaultRefCpiDateStr)`, the next bond trading day after the trade date. It is the "Settlement Date" that the app uses for `indexRatio` everywhere ([3.0 §Ref CPI Date](./3.0_TIPS_Ladder_Rebalancing.md#ref-cpi-date)), so the day count (A/E) of the accrued interest and the Index Ratio of one formula are stated at one date, and an order placed today settles on it. It is not the `settlementDate` global of the market data. The two dates are the same under Market quotes, and `settlementDate` is one bond trading day earlier under the dormant FedInvest source, which settles T+0 ([3.1 §4.0 Yield Sources](./3.1_Data_Pipeline.md)).

`bond.maturity` (a Date, needed for the day-count calc) is resolved from the global `tipsMarketData` by
CUSIP — the detail row itself carries `maturityStr` (formatted), not a Date.

<a id="trade-ticket-naming"></a>
**Naming:** the column is "Cost", the name of the `Cost`, `Cost Before` and `Cost After` columns of the main table and of the "Cost per TIPS" popups ([Popup Routing](./4.5.2_Show_Drill_Popups.md#popup-routing)). "Principal" is the [Inflation-Adjusted Principal](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#inflation-adjusted-principal) of a TIPS (`principalPerBond` = 1,000 × `paymentIndexRatio`), which does not depend on the market price and is not what a buyer pays.

**Popup routing:** cells carry `class="tt-drill" data-tt-idx data-tt-key`, routed by a dedicated
click handler in `index.html` (separate from the main table's `td.drillable` handler) via
`showPopup` (`popup.js`), not the `#drill-overlay`/`buildDrillHTML` mechanism used by the main
table:

| `data-tt-key` | Builder (`drill.js`) | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `cost` | `buildTradeTicketCostDrill` | Price → Index Ratio → Cost per TIPS → Quantity → Cost |
| `accrued` | `buildTradeTicketAccruedDrill` | A → E → Coupon÷2 → Accrued Interest per TIPS → Quantity → Accrued Interest |
| `total` | `buildTradeTicketTotalDrill` | Cost → Accrued Interest → Total Cost / Total Proceeds |

**Spec reference:** [Bond Basics §Accrued Interest](../../knowledge/Bond_Basics.md) (nominal day count); [TIPS Basics §Accrued Interest](../../knowledge/TIPS_Basics.md) (Index Ratio extension); [Cost per TIPS](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#cost-per-tips); [4.0 §accruedInterest](./4.0_Computation_Modules.md).
