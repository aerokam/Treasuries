## Bond Ladder Concepts

A [Bond Ladder](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-ladder) has one rung per time interval. **In this specification each rung is one calendar year**; monthly and quarterly constructions are possible and are not covered here.

Terms: [Funded Year](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#funded-year) and its synonym [Rung](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#rung), [Ladder](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ladder), [DAA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#daa), [AA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#aa), [LMI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#lmi), [Last-Year Interest](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#last-year-interest), and [P+I](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#pi).

---

## Construction Method

Work backwards from the longest to the shortest maturity:

- The last funded year has no later maturities, so its LMI is 0.
- The LMI of a funded year is the Annual Interest of the securities maturing in later years, so each funded year is calculated after every later funded year.

---

## Basic Algorithm

- Sort the securities by Maturity Date, latest first.
- For each security, latest first:
  - Calculate its Annual Interest.
  - Store the Annual Interest by maturity year, for the funded years before it.
- For each funded year, latest first:
  - Select the securities maturing in the year. They can have different maturity months.
  - Calculate the LMI: the stored Annual Interest of the securities maturing in later years.
  - Calculate the required P+I: DAA − LMI.
  - Calculate the Quantity: ROUND(required P+I / P+I of one security).
  - Calculate the AA: Quantity × P+I of one security + LMI.
