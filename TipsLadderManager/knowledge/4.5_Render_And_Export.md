# 4.5 Render and export

**Specifies:** [4.5 Render and export](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_TLM_RENDER) and the six processes it decomposes into
**Implemented by:** `TipsLadderManager/index.html`, `TipsLadderManager/src/render.js`, `TipsLadderManager/src/drill.js`, `TipsLadderManager/src/popup.js`, `TipsLadderManager/src/modal.js`
**Reference:** [5.0 UI Schema](./5.0_UI_Schema.md) states the column schema, the group header rows, the popup routing, the trade ticket and the cash flow calendar in detail; [2.1 Export](./2.1_Export.md) states the files of the Export menu; [6.0 UI Layout](./6.0_UI_Layout.md) states where each control is.

Draw the result of the mode on screen, Build or Rebalance, and write it out. The two modes have different interfaces and different results. The code that more than one of the six processes calls is in the shared modules [drill.js (M6)](../../knowledge/SharedModules.md#m6), [popup.js (M7)](../../knowledge/SharedModules.md#m7) and [modal.js (M8)](../../knowledge/SharedModules.md#m8); each process below names what it takes from the mode on screen.

<a id="render-ladder-table"></a>
### 4.5.1 Render ladder table

**Implemented by:** `index.html#renderBuildOutput`, `#renderOutput`, `#_renderBeforeStatePreview`, `render.js#renderTable`, `render.js#COLS`
**Input:** the [Build Result](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#build-result) from [Build ladder (4.3)](./4.3_Build_Ladder.md), in Build and in Explore; the [Before-State Preview](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#before-state-preview) and the [Rebalance Result](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#rebalance-result) from [Rebalance ladder (4.4)](./4.4_Rebalance_Ladder.md), in Rebalance; [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) from the user
**Output:** the [Ladder Table](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ladder-table) to the user, and to [Show drill popups (4.5.2)](./4.5_Render_And_Export.md#show-drill-popups); the [Per-Year DARA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#per-year-dara) typed in a group header row, as [Ladder Parameters](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ladder-parameters), to [4.3.1](./4.3_Build_Ladder.md#resolve-per-year-dara) and [4.4.1](./4.4_Rebalance_Ladder.md#derive-per-year-dara-from-holdings-or-dara-plan)

Draw the [Ladder Table](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ladder-table) of the mode on screen:

- Draw every column from the one `COLS` array, filtered to the mode on screen: the columns of Build, or of Rebalance ([5.0 §Column Definitions](./5.0_UI_Schema.md)).
- Group the rows by funded year, and draw a header row above each group, with the aggregates of the group, the per-year DARA input, and the bracket-year excess flag ([5.0 §Funded Year Group Header Row](./5.0_UI_Schema.md#funded-year-group-header-row), [§DARA Input](./5.0_UI_Schema.md#dara-input), [§Aggregation rule](./5.0_UI_Schema.md#aggregation-rule), [§Bracket-Year Excess Flag](./5.0_UI_Schema.md#bracket-year-excess-flag)).
- Draw the Coupon Counting link on the header row of the settlement year, in Rebalance only.
- In Rebalance, draw the [Before-State Preview](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#before-state-preview) as soon as holdings load, and the [Rebalance Result](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#rebalance-result) after Run.
- Draw the totals row.
- Write the per-year DARA typed in a header row to the per-year DARA that 4.3.1 and 4.4.1 read, and recalculate the preview or an already-run Build from it.
- Expand and collapse a group by a click on its header row, and select the rungs of a range of rows ([3.0 §Per-Rung DARA Selection](./3.0_TIPS_Ladder_Rebalancing.md#per-rung-dara-selection)).

<a id="show-drill-popups"></a>
### 4.5.2 Show drill popups

**Implemented by:** `index.html` (the `td.drillable` click handler, `_showTipsRefModal`), `drill.js#buildDrillHTML`, `drill.js#buildDurationPopupRows`, `popup.js#showPopup`
**Input:** the rows of the [Ladder Table](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ladder-table) from [Render ladder table (4.5.1)](./4.5_Render_And_Export.md#render-ladder-table); [TIPS Market Data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-market-data) from [Load market data (4.1)](./4.1_Load_Market_Data.md); [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) from the user
**Output:** the popup of the cell or link clicked, as [Ladder Table](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ladder-table), to the user

Show how a figure of the [Ladder Table](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ladder-table) was calculated, or the TIPS Ref and Mkt tables, in a popup:

- Route a click on a drillable cell by its column key to the builder of its popup ([5.0 §Popup Routing](./5.0_UI_Schema.md#popup-routing)), and give the popup the title that section states.
- Show a nested drill from a figure inside a popup.
- Show the Ref and Mkt tables of the TIPS the page loaded from the link in the status line.

<a id="show-trade-ticket"></a>
### 4.5.3 Show trade ticket

**Implemented by:** `index.html#buildTradeTicketHTML`, `index.html#_ttTable`, `drill.js#buildTradeTicketCostDrill`, `drill.js#buildTradeTicketAccruedDrill`, `drill.js#buildTradeTicketTotalDrill`, `popup.js#showPopup`, `modal.js#makeDraggableResizable`
**Input:** the [Build Result](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#build-result) or the [Rebalance Result](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#rebalance-result) of the mode on screen; [TIPS Market Data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-market-data) from [4.1](./4.1_Load_Market_Data.md); [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) from the user
**Output:** the [Trade Ticket](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#trade-ticket) to the user

Show the [Trade Ticket](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#trade-ticket) when the user clicks the Trade Ticket button ([5.0 §Trade Ticket](./5.0_UI_Schema.md#trade-ticket)). Build lists the purchase of every CUSIP with a quantity above 0. Rebalance lists the Buys and the Sells, by the sign of the change in quantity.

### 4.5.4 Show cash flow calendar

**Implemented by:** `index.html#buildCashFlowData`, `index.html#_groupCashFlowByYear`, `index.html#_cashFlowRowsHTML`, `index.html#_renderCashFlowMode`, `drill.js#buildCashFlowDateDrill`, `drill.js#buildCashFlowEventDrill`, `popup.js#showPopup`, `modal.js#makeDraggableResizable`
**Input:** the [Holdings](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#holdings) from [Import holdings and DARA plan (4.2)](./4.2_Import_Holdings_And_DARA_Plan.md); the after-trade holdings of the [Rebalance Result](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#rebalance-result); [TIPS Market Data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-market-data), the [Daily Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#daily-ref-cpi) and the [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday) from [4.1](./4.1_Load_Market_Data.md); [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) from the user
**Output:** the [Cash Flow Calendar](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#cash-flow-calendar) to the user, and as a csv file

Show the [Cash Flow Calendar](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#cash-flow-calendar) of the holdings, Before, or After a Rebalance, when the user clicks the Cash Flow button, and write it to a csv file on the Export CSV button of the calendar ([5.0 §Cash Flow Calendar](./5.0_UI_Schema.md#cash-flow-calendar)).
