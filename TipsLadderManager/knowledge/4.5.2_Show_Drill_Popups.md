# 4.5.2 Show drill popups

**Specifies:** [4.5.2 Show drill popups](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_TLM_RENDER)
**Implemented by:** `index.html` (the `td.drillable` click handler, `_showTipsRefModal`), `drill.js#buildDrillHTML`, `drill.js#buildDurationPopupRows`, `popup.js#showPopup`
**Input:** the rows of the [Ladder Table](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ladder-table) from [Render ladder table (4.5.1)](./4.5.1_Render_Ladder_Table.md); [TIPS Market Data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-market-data) from [Load market data (4.1)](./4.1_Load_Market_Data.md); [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) from the user
**Output:** the popup of the cell or link clicked, as [Ladder Table](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ladder-table), to the user

Show how a figure of the [Ladder Table](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ladder-table) was calculated, or the TIPS Ref and Mkt tables, in a popup:

- Route a click on a drillable cell by its column key to the builder of its popup ([4.5.2 §Popup Routing](./4.5.2_Show_Drill_Popups.md#popup-routing)), and give the popup the title that section states.
- Show a nested drill from a figure inside a popup.
- Show the Ref and Mkt tables of the TIPS the page loaded from the link in the status line.

---

<a id="popup-routing"></a>
## Popup Routing

`drill.js` exports a single function:

```js
buildDrillHTML(d, colKey, summary)
```

The title of the popup (`index.html`, the `td.drillable` click handler) is:

- For the Amount of a gap year or a Future 30Y year: the year and " — Amount".
- For an Amount column of a funded year (`amtBefore`, `amtAfter`, `amount`): the funded year, " — " and the column label, as "2029 — Amount After", "2029 — Amount Before" and (Build) "2029 — Amount". The whole funded year is the subject of the popup, so the title names the year and not one TIPS.
- For any other column: the maturity month and year of the row (the funded year where there is none), " — " and the column key.

Returns HTML string for the popup content. Routes by `colKey`:

| colKey(s) | Popup content | Spec reference |
|---|---|---|
| `amtBefore`, `amtAfter`, `amount` | bondVars → qty → principal + ownCoupon + laterMatInt [+ preLadderCreditForYear if > 0, Build only]. **Multi-TIPS funded year** (`d.fundedRungs.length > 1`, Build only): replaced by a per-sub-rung breakdown — for each TIPS, month/year × qty × piPerBond = subtotal, then LMI/credit, funded-year total, DARA, surplus. Single-TIPS layout (Inflation-Adjusted Principal × qty) is unchanged when `fundedRungs` has length ≤ 1. **Settlement year** (`d.fundedYear === summary.settlementYear`): the "Later maturity interest (LMI)" row's label and description flag the settlement-year exception (2.0 §Settlement-year LMI is remaining coupons only) — label becomes "Remaining later maturity interest", description becomes "coupons from TIPS maturing after this year, paid after today, based on the Coupon Counting choice". The text names neither the settlement year nor a date, so "this year" and "today" are correct on every row and every day. The label states the exception, as the Ref CPI date row of the Cash Flow Calendar does ([4.5.4 §Structure](./4.5.4_Show_Cash_Flow_Calendar.md#cash-flow-structure)). A non-zero `d.availableCashCredit` (or `..Before` for `amtBefore`) adds an "Available cash" line (2.0 §Available Cash). | Amount of a gap year or a Future 30Y year (group header row; Build, Rebalance Before, Rebalance After) | Gap year: P+I of its Simulated Outstanding Future 10Y TIPS → quantity → later maturity interest and Pre-ladder credit → Amount. ### Nested (Level-3) drills

Rows emitted with a `drillKey` carry `class="drill-l3" data-l3="<key>"`; the global click handler opens a second-level popup via a dedicated builder. Current keys:

| `data-l3` key | Builder | Content | Spec |
|---|---|---|---|
| `refCPI` | `buildRefCpiDrill` | Ref CPI interpolation — shows the settlement date as "Settlement Date", day/days-in-month, and the m-3/m-2 CPI-U anchors for that date. Call site injects `settlementDate` = `_fmtMDY(_defaultRefCpiDateStr)` into the `d` object. | 3.0 §Ref CPI Date |
| `indexRatio` | `buildIndexRatioDrill` | Index Ratio calculation | [DD Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio) |
| `pipb-<i>` | `buildPIPerBondDrill` | P+I per TIPS for holding `i` (splits principal vs last-year coupon) | TIPS_Basics.md §Last-Year Interest |
| `plcpool` / `plcpool:<slice>` | `buildPreLadderPoolDrill` | **Pre-Ladder Interest**: the rows "Pre-ladder coupon interest" (the years before the first funded year × the annual ladder coupon income), "Pre-ladder interest from the Simulated Outstanding Future 10Y TIPS and the Excess TIPS" and "Pre-ladder interest from the Simulated Outstanding Future 30Y TIPS and the Excess TIPS" (each when above 0), and "Net Cash" (when it is 0.5 or more in size), then "Total Pre-Ladder Interest", then "Applied to this year" (when above 0): the slice credited to the year, equal to the Pre-ladder credit row of its Amount popup. Triggered from the funded-year "Pre-ladder credit" line (`plcpool`, slice read from the detail row), each gap-year "↳ PLI credit" line in the gap-year Amount popup (`plcpool:<slice>`), and the Pre-ladder credit number in the Quantity row of a gap year in the Gap Dur popup (same `plcpool:<slice>` key). | 2.0 §Pre-Ladder Interest |
| `gappi-<year>` | `buildSyntheticPIDrill` | **P+I per synthetic TIPS**: Par ($1,000) + coupon interest (half the annual synthetic coupon rate, since these TIPS always mature in January — the Jan–Jun bucket, one coupon payment in the maturity year) = P+I. Reached from the P+I per TIPS number in the Quantity row of a year in the Gap Dur popup. Reads `gapParams.breakdown[].{piPerBond,synCpn}` (4.0 §gapParamsCore) verbatim. | TIPS_Basics.md §Last-Year Interest, 4.0 §gapParamsCore |
| `gaplmi-<year>` | `buildSyntheticLMIDrill` | **LMI for one Gap year**: splits `laterMatInt` into synthetic coupon from longer gap years already sized in the same sweep (each reconstructable from `gapParams.breakdown`, itself further drillable via `gapdur-<year>`) and coupon from actual TIPS maturing above the gap years (funded years, bracket excess) — the latter shown as a lump sum since `gap-math.js` doesn't retain a per-source breakdown of `lmiAboveByYear`. Reached from the LMI number in the Quantity row of a year in the Gap Dur popup. | 4.0 §gapParamsCore |
| `gapdur-<year>` | `buildGapYearDurationDrill` | **Synthetic TIPS duration derivation**: yield interpolation (anchors labeled "Most recently issued 10-year TIPS" and "Feb 2040 TIPS" — see §Duration Disclosure Standards below for why yield, not duration, is interpolated) → synthetic coupon → the full Macaulay-duration walk (coupon-date schedule, `E`/`DSC`/`w`, per-period cash flows/PVs) → Modified duration. | `f30pi-<year>` | `buildSyntheticPIDrill` (kind `'future30y'`) | **P+I per Synthetic TIPS of a Future 30Y year**: Par ($1,000) + coupon interest (half the annual synthetic coupon rate, since these TIPS mature in February, the Jan–Jun bucket, one coupon payment in the maturity year) = P+I. | `f30lmi-<year>` | `buildFuture30yLMIDrill` | **LMI for one Future 30Y year**: the annual interest of the Synthetic TIPS of each later Future 30Y year (`future30yParams.breakdown[].annualInt`), which sum to `laterMatInt`. | `f30dur-<year>` | `buildFuture30yYearDurationDrill` | **Synthetic Future 30Y-year duration derivation**: same structure as `gapdur-<year>`, but the "Yield Interpolation" step becomes "Anchor Yield" — every Future 30Y year is priced flat off one bond ("Most recently issued 30-year TIPS"), not interpolated between two, since there is no upper anchor past the last funded year. `gapdur-<year>`/`f30dur-<year>` are reached from the "Gap Dur"/"Future 30Y Dur" `.info-link` popups (`buildDurationPopupRows`/`buildFuture30yDurationPopupRows` below), not from a `td.drillable` cell: the Modified Duration number in the row of each year in those popups, and that row itself, are `drill-l3` triggers (§Quantity, cost and duration in one section below). The click handler resolves `summary` from `currentMode` directly rather than from `_currentDrillData`, matching the `plcpool` case above. `gappi-<year>`, `gaplmi-<year>` and `plcpool:<slice>` are reached from the Quantity row of a gap year in the Gap Dur popup, and `f30pi-<year>` and `f30lmi-<year>` from the Quantity row of a year in the Future 30Y Dur popup.

<a id="index-ratio-decimals"></a>
**Index Ratio decimals in drills.** A drill row that shows the Index Ratio shows nine decimal places where the Index Ratio feeds Cost per TIPS or sale proceeds (`indexRatio`, unrounded, DD Index Ratio). It shows five decimal places where it feeds Inflation-Adjusted Principal per TIPS, a coupon, a principal payment or Accrued Interest (`paymentIndexRatio`, the ratio of Treasury’s rule, DD Inflation-Adjusted Principal). The product of the price, the Index Ratio shown and 1,000 then reproduces the Cost per TIPS shown.

#### Gap year rows in the Bracket Amount and excess popups

`gapBreakdownRows` (`drill.js`) renders each gap year's theoretical-quantity row in the Bracket Amount and excess-quantity-after popups. The formula spells out the term names (`round((DARA − LMI) ÷ P+I)`), and separate "↳ P+I per synthetic TIPS" / "↳ LMI" / "↳ PLI credit" sub-rows underneath give the actual figures.

<a id="duration-disclosure-standards"></a>
### Duration Disclosure Standards

Both **Duration Calcs** and **Future 30Y Duration Calcs** popups must follow a consistent, transparent structure:

1.  **Average duration**: Display the Average Duration of the gap years, or of the Future 30Y years, at the top.
2.  **Synthetic TIPS by year**: List every Synthetic TIPS among them in one section, each year with its Quantity, Cost per TIPS, Cost and Modified Duration (§Quantity, cost and duration in one section below).
3.  **Average Derivation**: Explicitly show the cost-weighted mean calculation, Σ(Cost × Modified Duration) ÷ total cost (2.0 §Average Gap Year Duration is Cost-Weighted).
4.  **Bracket Years and Cover Years**: List the existing TIPS used for coverage and their Modified Durations.
5.  **Weight Solver**: Show the weights derived from the matching formula.
6.  **Fulcrum Visualization**: Display the `renderDurationBeam` visual.

**Why yield is interpolated, not duration.** Each synthetic bond's yield is interpolated from real
anchor bonds, then its coupon is reconstructed from that yield (`syntheticCoupon`, mirroring how
Treasury sets a new issue's coupon ≈ prevailing yield), and only then is duration computed from
`{maturity, coupon, yield}` via `calculateDurationDetail` — the same function every other duration in
this app goes through. Interpolating duration directly between the two anchor bonds' own durations
was considered and rejected: those bonds carry whatever coupon was set at their own auction, not an
at-the-money coupon for today's curve, so a duration blended from their two durations would carry
their coupons' divergence from at-the-money as unexplained noise, and it would be a second duration
methodology existing alongside `calculateDurationDetail` rather than one method used everywhere.

<a id="quantity-cost-duration"></a>
**Quantity, cost and duration in one section.** The Gap Dur and Future 30Y Dur popups list the years of the Synthetic TIPS in one section that is visible without a click (`synthYearListRows` in `drill.js`). Every number in the section is calculated there, explained beside the number, or a link to where it is calculated. Each formula shows its variable names first and then its numbers. Each year has four rows, in this order:

1.  **Year row**: the year with its assumed maturity date (Jan 15 for a gap year, Feb 15 for a Future 30Y year), the words Modified Duration, and the Modified Duration (for example 2039 (Jan 15), Modified Duration, 10.11). The Modified Duration number is a link and the row is a link; both open the duration derivation of the year (`gapdur-<year>`, `f30dur-<year>`).
2.  **Quantity** = (DARA − LMI) ÷ P+I per TIPS, then rounded to whole TIPS (for example ($40,000 − $10,176) ÷ $1,015.00 = 29.38, rounded to 29). A gap year with a Pre-ladder credit reads (DARA − LMI − Pre-ladder credit) ÷ P+I per TIPS. A Future 30Y year has no Pre-ladder credit. The Pre-ladder credit of a gap year is sized from the interest of the actual TIPS above it (2.0 §Pre-Ladder Interest), so with the Synthetic LMI included the quotient can be negative; the Quantity is then 0 and the numbers end with ", minimum 0" after the quotient. DARA is the DARA of the ladder. LMI, P+I per TIPS and the Pre-ladder credit are links to their calculations (`gaplmi-<year>`, `gappi-<year>` and `plcpool:<slice>` for a gap year; `f30lmi-<year>` and `f30pi-<year>` for a Future 30Y year).
3.  **Cost per TIPS** = (Price ÷ 100) × Index Ratio × 1,000 (DD Cost per TIPS), for example (99.7293 ÷ 100) × 1.000000000 × 1,000 = $997.29. The Price is not a link. A note under the equation states that the Price is computed from the Yield and the Coupon Rate of the Synthetic TIPS, whose Index Ratio is taken as 1.000000000 (DD Synthetic TIPS).
4.  **Cost** = Quantity × Cost per TIPS (for example 29 × $997.29 = $28,921).

After the years, the Gap total cost row (Future 30Y total cost in the Future 30Y Dur popup) shows Σ Cost with the Cost of each year. The Average duration of the gap years row (the Average duration of the Future 30Y years) shows Σ(Cost × Modified Duration) ÷ Gap total cost with the numbers. When the total cost is $0 the row shows Σ Modified Duration ÷ number of years, the simple mean (2.0 §Average Gap Year Duration is Cost-Weighted).

Hovering over the Cost or the Modified Duration of a year highlights where that number is used in the total cost and average duration rows, and hovering over a number in those rows highlights the number it comes from. The two sides share a `data-hl` key (`hlNum` in `drill.js`). A variable name in the Cost formula highlights the Quantity and Cost per TIPS rows through the `formula-var` source highlight that every other popup uses. Every number is read from the entry of the year in the breakdown of `gapParams` or `future30yParams` (4.0 §gapParamsCore), the entry the Amount popup of the same year reads, so the Quantity here equals the quantity in that Amount popup.
