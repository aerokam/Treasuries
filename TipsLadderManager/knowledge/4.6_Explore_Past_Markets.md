# 4.6 Explore past markets

**Specifies:** [4.6 Explore past markets](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TIPSLADDERMANAGER)
**Status: prototype.**
**Implemented by:** `TipsLadderManager/index.html` (`_loadExploreMarkets`, `_marketFromRows`, `_curveOf`, `_yieldAtTerm`, `_renderExplorePicker`, `_selectExploreMarket`, `_useMarket`, `_enterExploreState`, `_leaveExploreState`, `_applyShape`), `shared/src/market-data.js#parseFedInvestTipsRows`, `TipsLadderManager/src/rebalance-lib.js#buildTipsMarketData`
**Input:** the price files of past days, kept in `TipsLadderManager/data/explore/`; [TIPS Market Data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-market-data) of the live market from [Load market data (4.1)](./4.1_Load_Market_Data.md); [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) from the user (the date chosen)
**Output:** [TIPS Market Data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-market-data), the settlement date and the [Ladder Parameters](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ladder-parameters) to [Build ladder (4.3)](./4.3_Build_Ladder.md); [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables) to the user (the picker)

Build in the market of a past trading day. The Explore tab has the interface and the output of Build, and the market of the day chosen: that day's FedInvest prices as of that day, that day's Ref CPI, that day as the settlement date, and only the TIPS outstanding that day. The [Ladder Table](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ladder-table) of the Build is drawn by [Render ladder table (4.5.1)](./4.5_Render_And_Export.md#render-ladder-table), and has no SA Yield column.

<a id="load-past-markets"></a>
### Load past markets

- When the Explore tab opens, fetch the price file of each of the dates 2026-10-05, 2026-02-27, 2025-03-03, 2024-09-16 and 2023-04-06 from `data/explore/YieldsFromFedInvestPrices-<date>.csv`, in parallel.
- Read each file with `parseFedInvestTipsRows` and build its [TIPS Market Data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-market-data) with `buildTipsMarketData`, without SA Yields.
- Set the settlement date, the trade date and the Ref CPI date of a market to its date.
- When a file does not load, state "Prices of " the date ": HTTP " and the status.

<a id="explore-picker"></a>
### Draw the picker

- Draw the TIPS yield curve of each market on one chart, with the term from the settlement date of the market, 0 to 30 years, on the horizontal axis: each TIPS of 0.5 years or more to maturity that has a yield, shortest first. Draw the selected market's curve thicker.
- Draw one chip for each market, Today first, with its date and its yield at 5, 10, 20 and 30 years. Interpolate the yield linearly between the two TIPS either side of the term. A term up to a year past the longest TIPS takes the yield of the longest TIPS, and any other term outside the curve shows "—".

<a id="select-a-market"></a>
### Select a market

- When the user clicks a chip, make its market the market of the app and run the Build at once. The DARA stays as typed.
- Keep the number of funded years, and the Last Year's distance from the settlement year, of the ladder shown. In the market chosen, the Last Year is the latest year offered no later than that distance from the settlement year, and the First Year is the earliest year offered that leaves that number of funded years. Where the market offers no such year, its default stays.
- Choosing Today, or leaving Explore, returns to the live market.

<a id="explore-state"></a>
### Start state

- Start Explore from the form of the most recent Rebalance Ladder or Build Ladder click, whichever came last. Rebalance counts only once a holdings file or a DARA plan file other than the sample has been loaded. Build the result at once when Explore starts from a click not yet used.
- Set aside the form and the result of Build while Explore is on screen, and restore them when Explore is left. Keep the form of Explore while it is not on screen.
