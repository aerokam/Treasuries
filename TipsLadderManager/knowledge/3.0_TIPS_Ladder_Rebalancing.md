## Purpose

Rebalance a TIPS ladder to its per-year [DARA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#dara) targets, which can differ from year to year ([2.0 §Average Gap Year Duration is Cost-Weighted](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#average-gap-year-duration-is-cost-weighted)). A rebalance rebuilds the ladder from the current portfolio toward the per-year DARA targets, buying and selling TIPS as required (`runRebalance`, `src/rebalance-lib.js`).

## Context

A TIPS ladder needs a rebalance as new TIPS are issued, which fills former gap years so that their coverage moves from bracket excess into the rung of the year, and as market prices change the income of the ladder. A rebalance realigns the ladder to its per-year DARA targets using the current portfolio.

## Rebalancing-Specific Concepts

<a id="ladder-range"></a>
### Ladder Range

- **firstYear**: the earliest maturity year in the holdings.
- **lastYear**: the latest maturity year in the holdings. When the holdings carry Cover Excess and no last year is given, the last year is recovered from that excess when it is later ([2.0 §Future 30Y Rungs](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#future-30y-rungs)).
- **Gap years**: the [Gap Years](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#gap-years) in `[firstYear, lastYear]` with no holdings, determined from the outstanding TIPS in the market data.
- **DARA for a year in `[firstYear, lastYear]`**:
  - A year with TIPS in the market data and no holdings: the interest that arrives from later holdings ([Per-Year DARA from Portfolio](#per-year-dara-from-portfolio)). The year is a hole and has no trade ([Intentional empty rungs](#intentional-empty-rungs-and-the-rung-validation-waiver)).
  - A gap year: 0, unless the holdings show that the ladder covers the gap years, and then a value read from the shape of the ladder ([Per-Year DARA from Portfolio](#per-year-dara-from-portfolio) step 2).
- **`lastYearOverride`** (optional, from the Last Year field): replaces the derived `lastYear`. It can be a gap year or a later year, for example 2042. The field defaults to the derived `lastYear`.
  - **Shortening**: when the override is earlier than the derived `lastYear`, the holdings of the years after it are sold in full and those years are excluded from the rebuild.
  - **Extending**: when the override is later, the added years take the per-year DARA entered for them, which the per-year DARA panel fills with the value of the DARA field. The TIPS for those years are identified from the market data and buy rows are generated.
- **`firstYear` inference**: `inferFirstYearFromHoldings` (`rebalance-lib.js`) infers the first year of the ladder from a Holdings Export or a tipsladder.com Format file whose holdings are all excess in the earliest year:
  - It applies when `derivedFirstYear` is the year of the [Active Lower Bracket](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#active-lower-bracket), every holding of that year has `excessQty` equal to `qty` (pure bracket, no funded quantity), and gap years exist after it.
  - `numGapYears = round(totalExcessPI / roughDARA)` and `firstYear = 2040 − numGapYears`, where `totalExcessPI = excessQty × piPerBond` of the lower bracket plus `excessQty × piPerBond` of the 2040 bracket, and `roughDARA` is the median ARA of the funded years that are not bracket years and not gap years. The result must lie in the gap years, and otherwise there is no inference.
  - The formula holds because the P+I of the bracket excess is about `numGapYears × DARA` ([2.0 §Gap Year Coverage Model](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#gap-year-coverage-model)).
  - The inferred gap year is the default selection of the First Year field. A broker positions export and a Plain CUSIP/Quantity Format file have no `excessQty`, so there is no inference and `firstYear` is `derivedFirstYear`.
- **`firstYearOverride`** (optional, from the First Year field): replaces the derived `firstYear`. The holdings of the years in `[derivedFirstYear, firstYearOverride)` are sold in full and those years are excluded from the rebuild. The bracket identification excludes the holdings of those years, so that they do not satisfy the lower bracket search. The per-year DARA table follows the override: it starts at the First Year field when that is set, and a stored per-year value outside `[firstYear, lastYear]` is dropped from the table.
  - **firstYear as a gap year.** The ladder starts inside the gap years and no holdings exist below them. The active lower bracket is identified from the market data: `identifyBrackets` falls back to the market data when the holdings search finds no lower bracket. The 2-bracket weights apply. The rebalance generates a buy of the lower bracket excess (`qtyBefore = 0`, `excessQtyAfter = ROUND(totalCost × lowerWeight / costPerBond)` of the lower bracket). Build inserts the lower bracket into `yearBondMap` and `rangeYears` before the sweep with a funded quantity of 0.
  - **Retained excess with firstYear inside the gap years.** With the Brackets control on **Multi-bracket**, `minGapYear` is `firstYear`, and `minGapYear − 1` is itself a gap year. The active lower bracket search walks the market data for the longest-dated 10-year maturity before `minGapYear`, as `anchorBefore` does, and does not test the year `minGapYear − 1`. That maturity is also `brackets.lowerYear`. With no retained maturity distinct from it, the lower brackets reduce to the 2-bracket case.

### lastYear as a Gap Year

When `lastYearOverride` is a gap year (`lastYear ∈ gapYears`), the upper bracket year (2040) is not a funded rung, and all its holdings are bracket excess:

- `fundedYearQty(2040) = 0` in Phase 3 and Phase 4 (`yearDara(2040) = 0`).
- `excessQtyBefore(2040)` follows the cost formula `ROUND(totalCost × weight_2040 / costPerBond_2040)`. With `fundedYearQtyAfter(2040) = 0`, `fundedYearQtyBefore = qtyBefore − excessQtyBefore` is about 0 for a correctly built portfolio.
- Phase 2 (`calculateGapParameters`): the LMI of 2040 uses the actual current holdings quantity, because no funded-year target exists for 2040.
- `rungCount = lastYear − firstYear + 1`: gap years count as rungs, and bracket excess funds their income.

**Default for `lastYear`**: the Last Year field defaults to the latest maturity year in the holdings, so a held 2040 TIPS gives `lastYear = 2040` and funds 2040 as a rung. To end the ladder at a gap year and treat a held 2040 TIPS as bracket excess, the holder selects the last gap year in the field, which lists every TIPS year available.

<a id="ref-cpi-date"></a>
### Ref CPI Date

Neither Build nor Rebalance has a Ref CPI date control. Both look up the Ref CPI of the settlement date, and the info strip shows Trade and Settle only. The settlement date is the date at which every figure is stated.

A Rebalance is a set of trades that settle on the settlement date, so its costs and amounts have meaning only at that date. A Build prices at the market of today. Stating those prices at an older Ref CPI would combine the Index Ratios of one date with the prices of another, which gives a ladder that existed on no day. A saved ladder keeps its real target through [DARA Reference Date](#dara-reference-date), from the date recorded in the file. Loading the Holdings Export of a ladder built a year ago prices the holdings at the market of today, which is the purpose of a Rebalance. A ladder stated at an older date is tested with a file whose bonds were quoted then. The end-to-end Available Cash test names the bonds that matured this year from the TIPS reference data.

**Ref CPI interpolation popup** (level-3 drill from a Ref CPI row): shows the settlement date as "Settlement Date" and the m-3 and m-2 CPI-U values for it.

<a id="dara-reference-date"></a>
### DARA Reference Date

DARA is a real amount, stated at a Ref CPI date. The ARA of a rung is `qty × 1,000 × (RefCPI_active ÷ baseCPI) × (1 + coupon ÷ 2 × nPay)`, so the ARA and the DARA of a comparison `ARA vs DARA` move with the active Ref CPI. DARA and its Ref CPI date are a pair: 40,000 at one date and 40,000 at another are different real amounts, differing by the inflation between them.

One Ref CPI date governs a session, so within a session the pair is invisible. It becomes visible when a ladder is saved and reloaded later: the exported `#fundedYear,dara` block is a list of bare numbers, and reading them at a later date re-denominates every one.

- **The export records the basis.** `#params` carries `refCpiDate=<YYYY-MM-DD>`, the Ref CPI date in effect when the file was written, which is the settlement date ([2.1](./2.1_Export.md)). The Holdings Export and the DARA Plan File both write it.
- **The import scales.** When the recorded date differs from the Ref CPI date in effect, every DARA value is multiplied by `RefCPI(active) ÷ RefCPI(recorded)`. The date in effect is the settlement date, so the recorded date of the file is all that the scaling reads. A target of 40,000 stated at an earlier date becomes the equivalent figure at the current date, and the ladder keeps its real purchasing power (`_restateDaraToActiveBasis`, `index.html`).
- **Visibility.** A scaled import says so in the status strip: the Ref CPI date of the file, the date scaled to, and the factor.
- **No way to decline the scaling.** Using the values as written reads a target stated at an earlier date as stated at the settlement date, which cuts the real target by the inflation in between. A holder who wants a different target edits the DARA.
- **A file with no recorded Ref CPI date.** A hand-written DARA plan, and a file written without the date, have a `#fundedYear,dara` block with no date. The values are used as written, and the status strip offers **Set Ref CPI date**, which opens a date picker. Supplying a date scales the values from it, and ignoring the offer leaves them unchanged. The offer stays, so a date entered wrongly can be replaced, and the picker reopens on the date last supplied. The offer states a fact about the file and does not choose the date at which the app prices.
- **Rationale.** Restating the DARA keeps the basis at the date of today. Setting the Ref CPI date back to the build date of a ladder also holds its real target steady and is consistent for the gap and bracket math, because the Synthetic TIPS are modeled as issued on that date too ([2.0 §Synthetic TIPS for Gap Years](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#synthetic-tips-for-gap-years)). It breaks at issuance: a gap-year TIPS issued later has a dated date Ref CPI from its dated date, so against a back-dated Ref CPI its Index Ratio is below 1 and it delivers less than the Synthetic TIPS it replaces, which forces a purchase unrelated to the real target.
- **Invariant.** With the DARA restated, inflation alone produces no trades. The funded quantity `(DARA − LMI) ÷ P+I` scales with the price level in all three terms. The bracket excess quantity `gap cost × weight ÷ cost per bond` scales in both cost terms. Only the Synthetic TIPS quantity moves, correctly, because a Synthetic TIPS issued today covers less real value than one modeled at the earlier date, and the bracket bonds covering it have accreted by the same factor.

**Two dates, two offers.** The rebalance reads two dates, and they are separate entries with separate offers that share the picker frame they open in:

- **The Ref CPI date of the file.** It is offered only where the loaded file carries per-year amounts and records no Ref CPI date, because per-year amounts are the only thing it acts on.
- **Cash received since** ([2.0 §Available Cash](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#available-cash)), which sets the payments that Available Cash includes. It is offered wherever the app filled that figure from a file that records no date, a broker positions export included, which carries no per-year amounts. It is not a Ref CPI date and restates no amount. Its choices are the coupon payment dates on which the portfolio was paid.

A file that records its own `refCpiDate` drives both automatically. That date is the settlement Ref CPI date in effect when the file was saved, so it is the basis of the amounts and the point after which the cash flows have been received.

**A file with no `#fundedYear,dara` block** (a broker positions export, a Plain CUSIP/Quantity Format file, and a Holdings Export or tipsladder.com Format file without the block) has no DARA, whatever other columns it carries. The excess column of a Holdings Export is a quantity and not a per-year amount. The per-year values are derived from the holdings at the Ref CPI date in effect ([Per-Year DARA from Portfolio](#per-year-dara-from-portfolio)), so they are in the current basis, and the Ref CPI date offer is not shown. The per-year amounts shown for these files are the reading of the holdings at the basis of today, and restating them to an earlier date would misstate what the holdings are worth now. Scaling per-year amounts to a Ref CPI date requires a file that carries them: a DARA plan, or an export of this app.

### DARA

<a id="per-year-dara-from-portfolio"></a>
#### Per-Year DARA from Portfolio (File Load)

When a holdings file without a `#fundedYear,dara` block (a broker positions export, a Plain CUSIP/Quantity Format file, or a Holdings Export or tipsladder.com Format file without the block) is loaded in Rebalance, the per-year DARA panel is filled with values derived from the current portfolio: the ARA that each rung already produces. Nothing is scaled or solved at load. The status strip says: "This file contains no DARA Plan, so the Per-Year DARA is derived from its holdings at the settlement Ref CPI date." A file with the block is read as stated ([4.2.2](./4.2_Import_Holdings_And_DARA_Plan.md#parse-dara-plan-and-parameters)).

1. **Raw ARA of every year in range** (`computePortfolioARAByYear`, range form). Process the holdings longest to shortest and accumulate the LMI, which is the annual interest of the full held quantity of each later bond.
   - For a held year, `ARA = Σ(fundedQty × piPerBond) + LMI`, where `fundedQty` is `qty − excessQty` when the file states `excessQty` and `qty` otherwise.
   - For a year with TIPS in the market data and no holdings, `ARA = LMI`.
   - Every year in `[firstYear, lastYear]` is written to the per-year store, empty years included, so that the panel shows the current income of each rung. An empty year between two holdings shows the coupon that arrives from the later bond (about $230 in one example), and not a full rung.
2. **A gap year is 0 unless the holdings show that the ladder covers the gap years.** A gap year cannot be held, so no holding states its DARA, and a ladder can stop short of the gap years or hold nothing near them.
   - The signal is a held excess at the Upper Bracket year (2040), found in the market data alone and not in the holdings. A retained lower bracket in 2032 to 2034 says nothing about the gap years ([2.0 §Retained Bracket Excess](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#retained-bracket-excess)).
   - The 2040 year has the signal when it is a spike by the test of step 3 (`hasGapCoverageIntent`, `rebalance-lib.js`). With no signal, every gap year is 0.
   - With the signal, a gap year takes the local slope of the nearer neighboring held funded year (`inferShapeValue`, `shape-math.js`), so that a step in the ladder is read as a step. With fewer than five held funded years, or when neither side of the gap year has two held points, the gap year takes the total of the held years spread evenly over the span of years from the earliest to the latest (`evenSpread`).
   - Future 30Y years after the last issued TIPS are not derived for these files. They appear through an export of this app, which carries a `#fundedYear,dara` block.
   - The rule is a coverage signal and not an assumption. Extending a slope across a holdings hole gave a negative gap-year target for an account that holds nothing between two funded years fourteen years apart, and that target inflated the whole ladder about 5 times in the self-financing scale ([Funding the rebalance](#funding-the-rebalance)). `TipsLadderManager/KNOWN_ISSUES.md` records the case.
3. **Bracket-candidate flag (held years only).** A curve fitted through the raw ARA of the held funded years (`findSpikes`, `shape-math.js`, [4.0](./4.0_Computation_Modules.md)) replaces a single median. A gap-adjacent bracket-candidate year that stands off the curve as a spike is flagged, and its per-year store entry is left unset and not capped. Empty years with only LMI are excluded from the fit. With fewer than five held funded years there is no curve, and a held candidate is flagged when its raw ARA exceeds the fair share: the total of the held years spread evenly over the span of years from the earliest to the latest held year (`evenSpread`). [Before-State Preview and Bracket-Year Excess Detection](#before-state-preview-and-bracket-year-excess-detection) gives the candidates, the choice of the lower bracket candidate, and what happens when the holder supplies its DARA. The flag is a preview flag and changes no other year of the derived Per-Year DARA.
4. **Scalar DARA field**: the median of the held years, rounded. It is the fallback when the holder clears the per-year store and types a scalar.
5. **The panel opens** so that the holder can review or edit individual years before running.

The per-year store is the shape that the rebalance preserves. At load nothing is scaled or solved: the panel shows the portfolio as it is, except the flagged bracket-candidate years of step 3, whose store entry is left unset pending the holder's value. The self-financing adjustment happens at Run. The holder can reshape before running: type a scalar in the DARA field, edit individual years, or select rungs on the table and use Set DARA or Infer DARA ([Per-Rung DARA Selection](#per-rung-dara-selection)).

<a id="before-state-preview-and-bracket-year-excess-detection"></a>
#### Before-State Preview and Bracket-Year Excess Detection

**The Before state renders on load, before Run.** When holdings are loaded or accounts are selected in Rebalance, the table shows Qty Before, Cost Before and Amount Before for every ordinary funded year, from the current holdings and the market data (price and Index Ratio). No DARA and no duration matching is involved. `src/before-state-lib.js` (`computeBeforeState`) holds this computation and does not call or import `runRebalance` or `runFundedRebalance` ([Funding the rebalance](#funding-the-rebalance)). It is a valuation of the holdings, and not a second copy of the sizing math.

The After columns (Qty, Cost and Amount After, Qty Δ, Cash Δ: [5.0 §Rebalance-only columns](./5.0_UI_Schema.md)) and the controls that depend on them (the Ladder CSV and Holdings options of the Export menu, Trade Ticket, the Duration links, Avg Yld and Avg Mod Dur: [6.0 §Form Card Layout](./6.0_UI_Layout.md)) are blank, hidden or disabled until the holder clicks **Rebalance Ladder**. Expand All and Collapse All ([6.0 §Collapsible Groups](./6.0_UI_Layout.md)) are available as soon as the Before table renders.

**Bracket-year excess detection** runs at the preview, before any Run, over candidate years that are fixed by the market data:

- **Lower bracket candidates**: the years from `LOWEST_LOWER_BRACKET_YEAR` to the year before the active lower bracket year ([DD §5.1](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#5.1-issuance-dependent-values), [Bracket Identification Rules](#bracket-identification-rules)).
- **Active Lower Bracket candidate**: the year of the [Active Lower Bracket](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#active-lower-bracket).
- **Upper bracket candidate**: 2040.
- **Future 30Y cover candidates**: the lower cover and the upper cover ([2.0 §Future 30Y Cover Pair](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#future-30y-cover-pair)).

Each candidate year that is held has its raw ARA (step 1 above) checked against the curve fitted through the raw ARA of every held funded year (`findSpikes`; the candidate is part of the fit). A candidate that stands off the curve as a spike is excess. With fewer than five held funded years the check is the fair share of step 3 above.

- A candidate that is not a spike has no flag. It is an ordinary funded year, with no Gap sub-row.
- **Lower bracket candidates** (every January TIPS year from 2032 to the year before the active lower bracket year, which is evaluated separately): if exactly one candidate is a spike, that year is flagged. If more than one is, the one with the highest excess (`rawARA` above the curve) is flagged and the others are ordinary funded years. `identifyBrackets` ([Bracket Identification Rules](#bracket-identification-rules)) acts on one cross-year retained maturity only, so the preview flags at most one, and does not show an excess that Run would not honor. Equal excess goes to the latest-maturing year.
- **Active Lower Bracket, upper bracket (2040) and the Future 30Y covers**: each is evaluated alone and may flag together with any other. They do not compete for a slot with the lower bracket candidates or with each other. The active bracket year is kept out of the lower candidate pool because it carries both funded income and gap-coverage excess and would otherwise win the tie-break for having the largest raw ARA, and it must be able to flag beside a retained lower bracket flag. A ladder whose last year is before the first gap year has no flag for the active lower bracket year and 2040.

**A flagged year** shows a best guess immediately. The DARA input of the year ([5.0 §Funded Year Group Header Row](./5.0_UI_Schema.md)) is pre-filled with the value of the curve at that year, and the Gap sub-row of the year shows the excess `raw ARA − that value`, as if it had been entered. The pre-filled value carries an amber ⚠ marker, "auto-set from the ladder's shape", which marks a guess pending review. `index.html` collects the flagged years in `_bracketAutoCapYears` and passes them to `src/render.js#renderTable`, which renders the marker and its tooltip (`daraInputHTML`). The tooltip opens "Auto-set from the ladder's shape." and then gives one of two causes: a maturity year whose ARA stands out against the other maturity years that hold TIPS, or a gap year or Future 30Y rung for which no TIPS have been issued and no quantity exists to infer a DARA from.

- The excess of a flagged lower bracket, Active Lower Bracket or 2040 candidate is presented as covering the gap years it funds ([2.0 §Gap Years](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#gap-years-section)). The DARA inputs of the gap years are pre-filled and flagged the same way where they have no other source.
- The excess of a flagged Future 30Y cover candidate is presented as covering the Future 30Y years in `[firstYear, lastYear]` ([2.0 §Future 30Y Rungs](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#future-30y-rungs)).

When the holder edits the DARA input of a flagged year to another value, the ⚠ clears and the excess is recalculated immediately as `raw ARA − entered DARA`, which updates the Gap sub-row. This is subtraction and not duration matching, for the guess and for the entered value. Loading a standalone DARA plan ([2.1 §Standalone DARA-plan file](./2.1_Export.md#standalone-dara-plan-file)) clears every ⚠ in the same way, because the plan states a value for each year it covers.

**Qty Before and Cost Before of a flagged year.** For an unflagged year they do not depend on a funded and excess split: the held quantity is the funded-side figure. For a flagged year, showing the full held quantity and cost beside a much smaller Amount would be inconsistent, so once the DARA of the year is known (guessed or entered) the quantity and cost are split with the conversion used for empty rungs, `round((DARA − LMI) / piPerBond)` ([Intentional empty rungs](#intentional-empty-rungs-and-the-rung-validation-waiver)). This is bond arithmetic and not duration matching.

- `fundedYearQtyBefore = round((DARA − LMI_before) / piPerBond)`, where `LMI_before` is the incoming later-maturity interest of the year already computed for the raw ARA, and `piPerBond` is that of the flagged CUSIP.
- `fundedYearCostBefore = fundedYearQtyBefore × costPerBond`
- `excessQtyBefore = heldQty − fundedYearQtyBefore`, at least 0. A DARA above the raw ARA of the year is the holder's choice and gives an excess of 0.
- `excessCostBefore = excessQtyBefore × costPerBond`

The funded row and the Gap sub-row of a flagged year then show quantity, cost and Amount that agree, as the split after Run does.

**Scope: preview only.** The flagging has no effect on the duration-match and self-financing engine at Run. Rebalance Ladder reads the per-year DARA that is on the form (the `daraByYear` map, [Per-Year DARA (`daraByYear`)](#per-year-dara-darabyyear)), whatever the preview displayed.

<a id="funding-the-rebalance"></a>
#### Funding the rebalance: self-financing scale for a derived Per-Year DARA (Run)

**Self-financing sizes a plan that the tool inferred, and never overrides a plan that the holder stated.** A derived Per-Year DARA (a broker or other file with no stated DARA, taken from the ARA of the current holdings) has no target that the holder asked for, and the scale gives it one that pays for itself. A **stated** DARA is the holder's target and is never scaled, whether or not a row has been edited since. A stated DARA is any of:

- a value typed into a per-year row
- a value stamped across a selection with **Set DARA** ([Per-Rung DARA Selection](#per-rung-dara-selection))
- a value loaded from a file: a Holdings Export of this app, or a DARA Plan File ([2.1](./2.1_Export.md#standalone-dara-plan-file))

Net cash (`costDeltaSum`) then reports the gap between the target and what the ladder returns. Negative means that reaching the stated target costs more than the ladder currently pays and the holder funds the difference. Raising every rung to add cash to the ladder is the common case and gives a negative net cash. Positive means that the ladder pays for more than the target needs.

**Only a derived Per-Year DARA that is not edited is scaled**, that is, with no per-rung edit and no **Set DARA** or **Infer DARA** since load (`daraPlanUnedited`, found by comparing the live per-year store with the snapshot at load). An edit of a derived Per-Year DARA turns the scale off for the whole plan and not only the edited row, because no stated shape underlies a derived Per-Year DARA to sweep the other rows around. Net cash (`costDeltaSum`) is then a small non-negative number: the proceeds of what is sold cover what is bought, with no external cash added and no surplus left unspent. The funded-rung targets are scaled by one factor, so the ladder keeps its shape, which can be uneven:

- When the duration-match bracket excess (sized from the Gap Total Cost, [2.0 §Excess Quantities](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#excess-quantities)) is a net **purchase**, the cash to buy it is raised by selling across the funded rungs, and the scale factor lowers their targets.
- When [Retained Bracket Excess](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#retained-bracket-excess) exceeds its duration-matched target and is sold down ([2.0 §Retained Bracket Excess](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#retained-bracket-excess)), the bracket trade is a **sale**, which frees cash. The same search then raises the funded-rung targets, spending the proceeds by buying a little more everywhere, so that no surplus is left as net cash.

This is one coupled solve, because the inputs depend on each other: the gap-year DARA is the funded-year median, the Gap Total Cost depends on that median, the excess to buy depends on the cost, and the cash raised (and so the factor) depends on the excess. A binary search on one DARA level removes the loop: at each trial level `L` the derived Per-Year DARA is rebuilt at `k = L / median` (each rung × `k`), the rebalance is run, and `L` is driven to the highest value at which `costDeltaSum ≥ 0`.

- **Implementation**: `inferScaledDARAFromPortfolio` with `flat = false` (shape-preserving). It is the one solve behind both Run and the selection action **Infer DARA** ([Per-Rung DARA Selection](#per-rung-dara-selection)), which uses `flat = true` over a selection. The displayed panel is not rewritten, and the holder keeps seeing the derived shape.
- **The scale is applied only when a gap year with coverage signal, or a Future 30Y year, is in range.** The scale settles the cash of the duration-match excess trade at those years. A structural gap year in range is not by itself the signal (step 2 of [Per-Year DARA from Portfolio](#per-year-dara-from-portfolio); `hasGapCoverageIntent` tests the same held excess at 2040). Run first runs the rebalance on the per-year store. The scaled run happens only if the engine reports gap years with the coverage signal or Future 30Y years in range and the plan is a derived Per-Year DARA exactly as loaded (`!daraPlanIsStated && daraPlanUnedited`). Future 30Y funding does not depend on the coverage signal: its trigger is the years after the last issued TIPS. With neither present there is nothing to buy, the direct run nets to about 0, and no scale is applied. `runFundedRebalance` (`rebalance-lib.js`) holds this orchestration, and the page passes the per-year store and the flags `daraPlanUnedited` and `daraPlanIsStated`. If no self-financing level exists, the result of the direct run stands and its net cash shows the shortfall.
  - **The pivot (median) of the search covers held years only.** The scale factor is `level / median`, where `median` is the median of the natural ARA of the years swept. A year that is not held (only the LMI that arrives from a later bond, or a gap year with no coverage signal) is excluded, as it is from the curve fit of step 3. A median with many near-zero LMI-only entries would otherwise raise the factor far above 1 when the unscaled ladder is already close to self-financing.
  - The scaled solve of a derived Per-Year DARA recovers its shape from `computePortfolioARAByYear` (range form) and `derivePerYearDara`, for every year in `[firstYear, lastYear]`, which matches the derived Per-Year DARA of the display panel. An empty interior year (a maturity the holder holds none of, and not a gap year) is recovered at its incoming LMI, the small figure shown on the form, and not at the scalar DARA. The scalar DARA would size the empty year as a full rung inside `runRebalance`, and the search would read that purchase as excess to fund and lower every swept rung.
  - **A stated plan is used exactly as given, with no cover-income correction.** A stated plan already carries the DARA of each year, and the correction would count that income twice. A derived Per-Year DARA is re-derived and corrected (`derivePerYearDara` and its cover-income correction, which the recovery needs, because the recovery under-reports the cover years). Re-deriving a stated plan would replace the targets of the holder with a derived Per-Year DARA and would propose trades across the whole ladder on a reload of the export of this app on the same day.
- **The search trials the ladder that the caller executes.** The Maturity preference, the Within-Year Allocation Policy and the per-year overrides ([Target CUSIP resolution](#target-cusip-resolution)) decide the CUSIP that each year buys, and so the cost of each trade. A search under the defaults solves for a ladder that is not built, and its level can satisfy `costDeltaSum ≥ 0` under `equal` and `last` and still fall short when executed under the settings of the holder. Every trial therefore carries `maturityPref`, `allocationPolicy`, `yearRankOverrides` and `yearOverrides` of the caller.
- **Relationship to 2.0.** The scale applies on top of [2.0 TIPS Ladders](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md). 2.0 [§Algorithm](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#algorithm-longest-to-shortest-sweep) sizes each rung from its DARA and credits the interest of the bracket excess down as LMI, and does not balance cash. The self-financing scale selects the one DARA level at which the sizing of 2.0 nets to non-negative cash, for a derived Per-Year DARA only.

<a id="intentional-empty-rungs-and-the-rung-validation-waiver"></a>
#### Intentional empty rungs and the rung-validation waiver

An empty interior funded year is an intentional hole by default. The default depends on the per-year DARA of the year and not on where the DARA came from:

- **Empty year of a derived Per-Year DARA, not edited**: its DARA is its incoming LMI, so `round((DARA − LMI) / piPerBond)` is 0. The year is a hole and has no trade.
- **Explicitly raised empty year**: when the holder lifts the DARA of the year above its incoming LMI by at least the P+I of one TIPS (a manual edit, or a selection **Set DARA**), the year sizes to at least 1 TIPS and fills. The panel value is the stated intent, and no other flag marks a hole. A stated value is never scaled ([Funding the rebalance](#funding-the-rebalance)), and raising an empty year of a derived Per-Year DARA turns the scale off for the whole plan, as any other edit does.

For the first case, the check of `sizeLadder` that every funded year funds at least one bond (it throws `daraTooLowYear`) is waived for the unheld in-range years of a rebalance (`optionalYears`). An empty year whose target rounds to 0 is a hole, and not a DARA that is too low. The waiver matters at the knife-edge, where the derived DARA of an empty year is about its incoming LMI and rounds either side of the preliminary LMI estimate. A year that the holder funds has a target of at least 1 and does not reach the throw. Build passes no `optionalYears`, because every Build year is a required rung and its too-low-DARA error stands.

#### Scalar DARA Field

The main DARA field is the fallback for funded years with no per-year entry. A scalar typed by the holder applies to every year that the per-year panel does not override. When the field is blank, the page uses `_daraByYearPanelDefault`, set at file load from the median of the portfolio.

- The field is blank, with the placeholder "by year", whenever one number would mislead, that is, for any variable per-year shape. It shows the number only for a flat ladder, so the field and the per-year table agree.
- While the field is blank, `_daraByYearPanelDefault` follows the median of the live per-year store, and not a figure left from before a selection Infer or Set DARA or a change of First Year or Last Year.
- A number typed into a blank field starts from nothing. An invalid entry (for example below $1,000) stays on screen as typed until Run rejects it.

#### Re-run Behaviour

On every click of Rebalance Ladder, `_daraByYearPanelDefault` is set to `finalDara` before `getDaraByYear()` is called, so that the stored per-year values apply whether or not the panel is visible.

### Pre-Ladder Interest (PLI) in Rebalance

With `preLadderInterest = true`, the pool, its zeroing and its partial-credit distribution are calculated by the shared `sizeLadder` (`ladder-core.js`), which Build also calls ([2.0 §Credit Pass](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#credit-pass)). Rebalance derives the bond set with `selectLadderBonds(firstYear, lastYear)`, runs `sizeLadder`, and uses its `zeroedFundedYears`, `partialCreditYear`, `partialCredit`, `pliCreditByGapYear` and `pliCreditByFundedYear`. A Build, export, import and rebalance gives zero trades and zero net cash because both modes take the distribution from the same function.

### Build ≡ Rebalance "Before" and "After" (unification invariant)

The "Amount After" of Rebalance for every funded year equals the "Amount" of Build for that year. For a no-trade round trip of a file of this app, "Amount Before" equals it too, because the held ladder is the target. Every Build funded year renders in the Rebalance table. This is the contract of the Build, export, import and rebalance round trip, and `tests/run.js` checks it ("rebal After == build amount per year" and "rebal Before == build amount per year": flat, with PLI, variable, and variable with PLI).

**Scope: Build with `maturityPref = 'last'`.** The zero-trade round trip is guaranteed for a Build with the Maturity preference **Last to mature**. Rebalance restores the Maturity preference of an imported file from the `#params` row, but its target side assumes one target CUSIP per funded year (`bst.targetCUSIP`) and does not reconstruct a multi-TIPS target for a Build with **Semiannual** or **All maturity months** ([2.0 §Maturity Selection Within a Funded Year](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#maturity-selection)). The Coupon preference is Build-only and is not restored by Rebalance, so a Build with the Coupon preference **Lower** and a Maturity preference other than **Last to mature** round-trips with **Higher**, which can select different same-month TIPS than the Build holds. [Target CUSIP resolution](#target-cusip-resolution) gives the choice of the single target CUSIP among the candidates of each run.

1. **One Amount rule: `fundedYearAmount` (`ladder-core.js`).** `build-lib.js` and the Before and After sweeps of Rebalance call one function, which returns `amount = principal + ownCoupon + laterMatInt + ownExcessCoupon + credit`, and adds the interest of the Simulated Outstanding Future 10Y TIPS and Future 30Y TIPS, the interest of the Excess TIPS and the Cover Excess, and the Net Cash of the issue dates in the year, all credited to the funded year ([2.0 §Gap Year Simulated Outstanding TIPS](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#gap-year-simulated-outstanding-tips) and [§Future 30Y Simulated Outstanding TIPS](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#future-30y-simulated-outstanding-tips), each §Interest credited to a funded year).
   - For a zeroed year (funded entirely by the pre-ladder pool), `credit = max(0, DARA_y − fixedIncome)`, which makes the row land on its DARA. Otherwise `credit = partialCredit`, the non-zeroed pre-ladder share of the year, else 0.
   - `sizeLadder` sizes the pool against the preliminary later-maturity interest, which decides which years are zeroed, so `pliCreditByFundedYear[y]` is a credit on the preliminary basis. The ARA of a row is built from the corrected components (`lAfter` and the excess coupon of the year `exIntA`). The shared rule reconciles the credit of a zeroed year to the corrected components. Without it, a zeroed year would exceed its DARA by `lAfter − prelimLMI`.
   - A non-zeroed year that the later-maturity interest alone over-covers exceeds its DARA, and Build shows the same value. The rung needs no bonds.
   - The Amount is display only. Trades use the sized quantities.
2. **Fully covered rungs render.** Rebalance emits the After rows from the holdings and the buys. A rung that is fully covered (zeroed by PLI, or over-covered by LMI) has no holding and no buy. A final pass over `rangeYears` emits any such rung as an informational row (`qtyBefore = qtyAfter = 0`, Amount After `postARAByYear[y]`), as Build renders every ladder year.
   - A synthetic-row pass emits a buy into `targetCUSIP` when that CUSIP has no current holdings (for example the Multi-bracket `newLowerYear`, or an ordinary `all` or `saYield` year whose first-ranked candidate was never held). The pass is gated on the target CUSIP and not on the year having no holdings of any kind. A year can hold other CUSIPs, already emitted by the main loop, while the target is a new buy that the loop does not see. Without this gate the buy would be missing from the table, the Trade Ticket and the export while the Amount After of the year includes it.
3. **Before runs the same sweep with held quantities.** "Amount Before" is the valuation of the current holdings. It uses the held quantities and the same `fundedYearAmount` rule as After, with the same components: principal, coupon, LMI, the coupon of the held excess (`exIntB`), the simulated and cover coupons, and the credit.
   - The pre-ladder credit in Before applies to files of this app only (those with a per-year DARA block). Broker files (inferred DARA) have no pre-ladder credit in Before. For a broker file with trades, Before differs from After, and the difference is the purpose of Before.
   - Available Cash credits the Before state the way it credits the target: the earliest funded year first, each year taking the lesser of the remaining cash and its shortfall `max(0, DARA − ARA before)`, which is added to the ARA of the year (`availableCashCreditBefore`).
   - **Bracket display**: a designated gap bracket renders with `*` and an excess sub-row with quantity 0 even at 0 excess, in Build and in Rebalance (`render.js isBracket` uses `excess > 0 || isGapBracket` for both modes).

**Before and the gap year rebalances.** Before is calculated as a Build is, with the gap years in place of the Future 30Y years. The Excess TIPS is not sized in Before: it is the excess that the file declares, or what the funded-first rule leaves ([Excess Quantity Before](#named-quantities-and-formulas)). The Simulated Outstanding Future 10Y TIPS are sized as in a Build ([2.0 §Gap Year Simulated Outstanding TIPS](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#gap-year-simulated-outstanding-tips)), and their coupons, the coupons of the Excess TIPS and the Net Cash are credited to the funded years by the rule used in After. At each issue date the Excess TIPS goes to the quantity that the duration match gives: the Excess TIPS above it is sold, and a bracket short of it buys the difference. The quantity held matters until the duration match falls to it. For a file of this app the DARA block holds a DARA for each year, the gap years included, and the declared excess is the excess that a Build would size, so Before equals After.

**Before and the Future 30Y rebalances.** Before is calculated as a Build is, so a build, export, import and rebalance round trip has no trades. The excess is not sized in Before: it is the excess that the file declares, or what the funded-first rule leaves ([Excess Quantity Before](#named-quantities-and-formulas)). The Simulated Outstanding Future 30Y TIPS are sized as in a Build ([2.0 §Future 30Y Simulated Outstanding TIPS](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#future-30y-simulated-outstanding-tips)), and their coupons, the coupons of the Cover Excess and the Net Cash are credited to the funded years by the rule used in After. At each issue date the Cover Excess goes to the quantity that the duration match gives, so the quantity held matters only before the first issue date. The Net Cash of an issue date in Before is negative when less Cover Excess is held than the duration match sets. For a file of this app the DARA block gives the DARA of the Future 30Y years and the declared excess is the excess that a Build would size, so Before equals After.

<a id="dara-inference-with-future-30y-years"></a>
**DARA inference with Future 30Y years.** When `dara = null`, the inferred DARA is the median, over the funded years, of the Amount calculated from the holdings: P+I, LMI, the coupons of the held excess, the simulated coupons, the Cover Excess coupons and the Net Cash (`runRebalance`, `rebalance-lib.js`).

- No holdings include Simulated Outstanding Future 30Y TIPS. The held Cover Excess implies their quantities. The Cover Excess costs the same as the Synthetic TIPS of the Future 30Y years, and the last Future 30Y year is recovered from the split of the excess between the lower cover and the upper cover ([2.0 §Future 30Y Rungs](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#future-30y-rungs)). The one flat DARA that sizes those Synthetic TIPS to the cost of the held excess gives their quantities (`impliedFuture30yInterest`), and from them the simulated coupons and the Net Cash. A holder with no Cover Excess has no Future 30Y years and no simulated coupons.
- The gap years are inferred the same way (`impliedGapInterest`): the one flat DARA that sizes their Synthetic TIPS to the cost of the held Excess TIPS gives their quantities, and from them the Simulated Outstanding Future 10Y TIPS coupons and the Net Cash.
- The natural-ARA recovery (`inferScaledDARAFromPortfolio`) adds the same coupons back at each funded year, so the recovered map matches the identity of Build.

<a id="per-year-dara-darabyyear"></a>
### Per-Year DARA (`daraByYear`)

`daraByYear` is an optional `Map<year, number>` that lets any ladder year (funded, gap or Future 30Y) have its own annual real amount. When it is given:

- Each funded year in Phase 4 uses `daraByYear.get(year) ?? DARA`. A year absent from the map takes the scalar DARA.
- Gap years use `daraByYear.get(year) ?? DARA` in the synthetic quantity formula and in the PLI pool estimate. A higher gap-year DARA raises the Synthetic TIPS quantities, and so `gapParams.totalCost` and the bracket excess quantities.
- Future 30Y years use `daraByYear.get(year) ?? DARA` in the hypothetical quantity formula. A higher Future 30Y DARA raises `future30yTotalCost` and the Cover Excess quantities.
- The funded-year target quantity of a bracket year uses the DARA of that year from the map.
- When `daraByYear` is null, the result is that of the scalar DARA.

The rule covers the 2040 upper bracket target and every 2041+ rung that feeds its LMI estimate, and not only the funded years that a caller sizes directly. The preliminary long-to-short sweep of the gap parameter calculation (it estimates the income that arrives at 2040 from the rungs above it) and the 2040 target quantity read `daraByYear.get(year) ?? DARA` for each year. A caller that supplies a non-flat `daraByYear` depends on the gap and bracket math reading it for every rung. A stale scalar would size the bracket excess for a different income level and leave a large net cash surplus after Run.

<a id="per-rung-dara-selection"></a>
### Per-Rung DARA Selection

The selection depends on the downward flow of LMI.

<a id="selecting-rungs"></a>
#### Selecting rungs

A **selection** is a set of funded years chosen on the ladder table (`#simple-table` in Rebalance, `#build-table` in Build) for the current session. It is not persisted.

- **Quick click** on a funded-year group-header row (press and release without moving off the row) toggles expand and collapse of the row. It does not select.
- **Press and hold** (600 ms without moving) on a group-header row selects that row, the anchor of a following Shift-click, and does not toggle expand and collapse.
- **Click and drag** across rows builds a range selection as the pointer moves. A drag that begins moving before the hold threshold preempts the hold, and range selection begins at once. When the hold has fired (the starting row is selected) and the pointer then moves into other rows without release, the same drag extends the range. A hold and a drag are one gesture. The trailing click is suppressed, because the gesture selected and did not toggle.
- **Shift-click** a second row selects the range between the last-clicked row (the anchor) and that row. With no anchor, a Shift-click selects the clicked row as the anchor. It does not toggle expand and collapse.

While at least one rung is selected, a toolbar (`#selection-toolbar`, [6.0 §Selection Toolbar](./6.0_UI_Layout.md)) shows a year-range label, a **Set DARA** input with Apply, an **Infer DARA** button (Rebalance only, because Build has no holdings to self-finance against), and a Clear button. Switching between Build and Rebalance, or loading a file, clears the selection.

<a id="two-distinct-actions"></a>
#### Two distinct actions

- **Set DARA** (`_runSelectionSetDara`, both modes) stamps one typed value across every selected rung, marks each as pinned (`_daraManualYears`, as a hand-typed per-rung edit does), and clears the selection.
- **Infer DARA** (`_runSelectionInfer`, Rebalance only) is a scoped self-financing solve (`inferScaledDARAFromPortfolio`, `flat = true`) over the selected years, against the current holdings. It applies at once, with no separate Apply, and clears the selection.

Both actions apply at once and close the toolbar. `#selection-dara-input` is cleared on close. The value stays visible in the rung rows of the per-year DARA panel once they render again.

Both actions change only the DARA of the selected rungs, and none outside the selection. Both hold a pinned year fixed inside the selection: a year that the holder typed in a row stays, and its cash flow still counts toward the self-finance target.

#### No cascade: DARA is fixed once set, the rebalance absorbs the rest

LMI flows only downward, from longer to shorter maturities, so a change of the DARA of a selection changes the income that arrives at every year below it. That change does not rewrite the DARA of those years. The DARA is the stated target of the holder for the year (for example a plan of required minimum distributions by segment) and stays as set. A shift in the incoming LMI changes what Run buys or sells for the year so that it still reaches its unchanged DARA. This is the sizing identity of 2.0, `DARA_y = trade + incoming credits`, applied at Run.

- The net cash of the whole portfolio after one scoped Infer is not guaranteed to be near 0. Only the net cash of the selected segment is (through `inCashScope`, below). Inferring every segment in turn (the top range, then the bottom range) brings the whole portfolio to about 0, because the net cash targets of the scopes together cover everything. Inferring one segment leaves the rest as it was.
- `inferScaledDARAFromPortfolio` solves for `scopeYears`, holds the years outside at `fixedDaraByYear`, and keeps the years typed by hand fixed inside the scope through `pinnedDaraByYear` while counting their cash flow. A trade outside `[firstYear, lastYear]` (for example the upper bracket when `lastYear` is inside the gap years) is credited to the scope that reaches `lastYearOverride`, through `inCashScope`.

#### No edit history

There is no Undo and no Revert-to-loaded. A selection covers as many rungs as needed in one action, so a mistake is corrected with another selection that sets or infers the correct value. `_daraLoadedSnapshot` (the map at load or import, per mode) lets `_daraPlanUnedited()` tell whether a derived Per-Year DARA changed since load ([Funding the rebalance](#funding-the-rebalance)). `_daraManualYears` records the rows that the holder stated, so that a later selection **Infer DARA** holds them fixed ([Two distinct actions](#two-distinct-actions)). Neither drives a restore.

#### Persisting a DARA plan across sessions

File load derives the per-year DARA from the current holdings ([Per-Year DARA from Portfolio](#per-year-dara-from-portfolio)), so a fresh broker export (Vanguard, Fidelity or Schwab) replaces any per-year shape of an earlier session. A broker file has no `#fundedYear,dara` block and no DARA data to overlay a plan on ([4.2.2](./4.2_Import_Holdings_And_DARA_Plan.md#parse-dara-plan-and-parameters)). The standalone DARA Plan File ([2.1 §Standalone DARA-plan file](./2.1_Export.md#standalone-dara-plan-file), Export and Import menu items, no CUSIP rows) keeps the shape: export the per-year shape once, and import it onto any later holdings upload, on any device. It is the only persistence.

## Named Quantities & Formulas (Rebalance)

<a id="target-cusip-resolution"></a>
### Target CUSIP resolution (Within-Year Allocation Policy)

For each ordinary (non-bracket) funded year in Phase 4, `rankForYear({ candidates: _canon.yearTipsListMap[year], held: yi.holdings, policy: allocationPolicy, maturityPref, rankOverride: yearRankOverrides.get(year) })` ([2.0 §Within-Year Allocation Policy](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#within-year-allocation-policy), [4.0](./4.0_Computation_Modules.md)) orders the union of the candidates and the held TIPS of the year, highest buy and hold priority first. The quantity formulas below apply after the order:

```
targetCUSIP = rank[0].cusip
nonTarget   = rank.slice(1), limited to the CUSIPs currently held
```

**Sell order for `maturity` and `saYield`, and for a year override.** `nonTarget`, in rank order starting with the least preferred to hold, is drained one CUSIP at a time, each fully to zero before the next, until the P+I `need` of the year is met or `nonTarget` is exhausted. The drain stops at the first CUSIP that is not fully drained and leaves every later CUSIP, `targetCUSIP` included, untouched. A smaller P+I per bond further down the rank does not let that CUSIP sell first, even if it would divide the leftover residual more evenly. Only after every `nonTarget` member is sold to zero does the remaining gap apply to `targetCUSIP`: a deficit (buy) or a residual too small for a whole-bond sale (shrink). While a `nonTarget` member still holds a quantity, the residual stays as a small under-shoot or over-shoot and `targetCUSIP` is not touched.

**Sell order for `equal`.** The target and non-target set is levelled toward equal value together (`levelValues`, [2.0 §Within-Year Allocation Policy](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#within-year-allocation-policy)): growth is added to the member with the lowest value, shrinkage is taken from the member with the highest value, and the level is recalculated at every step. `targetCUSIP` takes the rounding remainder once every other member is at its levelled value, as the last TIPS of a Build does ([2.0 §Multi-TIPS Funded-Year Split](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#multi-tips-funded-year-split)).

**Direction of the trades of a funded year.** The DARA sets the total P+I of the year (`needed`), and the policies choose which maturity trades. The change in that total sets the direction of every trade of the year, under any policy:

```
needed <  P+I of the year's TIPS now held    the year only sells
needed >  P+I of the year's TIPS now held    the year only buys
needed == P+I of the year's TIPS now held    the year does not trade
```

A year does not buy one maturity and sell another. Quantities are whole TIPS, so a maturity whose quantity rounds against the direction of the year keeps the quantity it holds, and the remainder that lands on `targetCUSIP` moves in the direction of the year only.

**Bracket years: the excess and the funded need of the year.** `rankForYear` does not choose the excess CUSIP of a bracket year. [Bracket Identification Rules](#bracket-identification-rules) and [2.0 §Duration Matching](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#duration-matching-brackets) identify it, and a rebalance does not buy a retained maturity into it beyond its duration-matched target. A bracket year that is also a [Funded Year](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#funded-year) has a funded need, and that role is not fixed to the excess CUSIP.

- A genuine shortfall (the existing funded coverage of the excess CUSIP, priced as an ordinary year prices its held CUSIPs, is below the need of the year) is resolved by the same `rankForYear` call as an ordinary year, with `policy: 'maturity'`. Only the direction of the Maturity preference applies, because `equal` and `saYield` are cash-flow preferences of ordinary years that the bracket and duration-match logic does not consult.
- A bracket year does not sell an existing funded holding to relabel it under another maturity. The Maturity preference steers the new money of a shortfall that the current holdings do not cover. If the excess CUSIP already funds the need of the year in full or more, nothing moves, whatever the Maturity preference, and the pick of `rankForYear` for the year is not used for a trade. Only when a shortfall exists is the existing funded contribution of the excess CUSIP held in place and never sold, and the shortfall alone is bought into the CUSIP that `rankForYear` prefers. Selling the whole position and buying the same dollar amount elsewhere has no economic reason.
- With **Last to mature** (the default) this changes nothing: the [Active Lower Bracket](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#active-lower-bracket) is the most recently issued 10-year TIPS, so ranking by latest maturity gives the excess CUSIP. With **First to mature**, **Semiannual**, **All maturity months** or **Select maturities**, a shortfall can resolve to another maturity of the same bracket year than the one holding the excess. The year then splits into two rows, each priced from its CUSIP, with no same-year buy and sell of one maturity.
- A same-maturity-year retained maturity ([Retained Lower Bracket](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#retained-lower-bracket)) is a candidate in this ranking like any other held maturity of the year. It matures earlier than the active maturity, so it is often the maturity that the preference wants for a shortfall. When it wins, its excess share (solved by `bracketWeightsN`, held, never increased) is combined with the shortfall on the one row, and the placement of that maturity by the retained-maturity rule ([Retained Maturities](#retained-maturities-holdings-based)) is skipped. Any other same-maturity-year retained maturity not chosen as the funded CUSIP is placed by that rule as usual.
- The sell order among the other holdings of a bracket year (neither the excess CUSIP nor its funded CUSIP) is the maturity-direction rank, which is the oldest-first rule of [2.0 §Retained Bracket Excess](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#retained-bracket-excess) under **Last to mature**.

A year that is only a gap year or a Future 30Y year has no funded role and no funded need, so only the excess CUSIP applies.

<a id="named-quantities-and-formulas"></a>
**Funded-Year Quantity Before, estimate** (`fundedYearQtyBeforeRaw_b`, held by year in `bracketTargetFundedYearQtyBefore`, `src/rebalance-lib.js`):

```
fundedYearQtyBeforeRaw_b = ROUND((DARA − LMIFromAbove_Before − nonTargetPI_b − creditedInterest_b) / piPerBond_b)
```

- `LMIFromAbove_Before` is the annual interest of all actual holdings in the years after year b, funded and excess holdings both.
- `nonTargetPI_b` is the P+I of the non-target CUSIPs held in the same bracket year.
- `creditedInterest_b` is the interest credited to year b ([2.0 §Interest credited to a funded year](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#gap-interest-credited)).
- The DARA is 0 for a year after `lastYear` and for a cover year before `firstYear`. A cover year in range is a funded year like any other.
- The estimate applies to every bracket year, whatever the file format, and replaces nothing that the file states: an imported `excessQty` for the CUSIP sets the funded quantity to `qty − excessQty` ([Excess Quantity Before](#excess-quantity-before)).
- The estimate reads holdings only and never `fundedYearQtyAfter` or any after-side target.

<a id="excess-quantity-before"></a>
**Excess Quantity Before** (`excessQtyBefore`, the honest current-holdings split):

- When the file states `excessQty` for the CUSIP (Holdings Export or tipsladder.com Format), it is the excess.
- For a same-maturity-year retained maturity, `excess = MAX(0, qty − ownFundedNeedQty)`.
- Otherwise `excess = MAX(0, qtyBefore_b − fundedYearQtyBeforeRaw_b)`, the funded-first rule: the funded claim of the year is applied first and the remainder of the held quantity is the excess.

The excess also has two uses outside this split. In duration matching it is the cover and bracket excess, the recorded intent of the build. In funded sizing its annual interest counts as later-maturity interest, because `LMIFromAbove_Before` counts funded and excess holdings both. The size of the imported cover excess recovers the intended first funded year and last funded year ([2.0 §Future 30Y Rungs](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#future-30y-rungs)). A correctly built ladder has a split that reproduces its imported excess, so a build, export, import and rebalance round trip has no trades.

**Excess Quantity After** (`excessQtyAfter`):

```
excessQtyAfter_b = ROUND(totalCost × weight_b / costPerBond_b)
```

It is the excess for duration matching after the rebalance. `costPerBond` is the [Cost per TIPS](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#cost-per-tips), `(price / 100) × Index Ratio × 1,000`. A Future 30Y cover year takes `future30yLowerExQty` and `future30yUpperExQty` from the same function that sizes a Build ([2.0 §Excess Quantities](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#excess-quantities)).

**Reconciling the honest split with the after target (no same-maturity buy and sell).** The funded and excess portions of a bracket-target CUSIP are the same held maturity. A subtraction of Before from After per portion goes wrong only when the two deltas have opposite signs, because one portion would buy the maturity while the other sells it. When both move the same way, or one does not move, the honest split already reconciles `fundedYearQtyBefore + Δ = fundedYearQtyAfter` per portion and passes through. Otherwise, as much of what is already held as cancels the opposing signs is relabeled at no cost and trades nothing, and only the remainder is bought or sold:

```
naiveFundedDelta_b = fundedYearQtyAfter_b − fundedYearQtyBeforeHonest_b
naiveExcessDelta_b = excessQtyAfter_b − excessQtyBeforeHonest_b

if naiveFundedDelta_b and naiveExcessDelta_b have opposite signs:
    realloc_b             = MIN(|naiveFundedDelta_b|, |naiveExcessDelta_b|)   // toward the portion that is buying
    fundedYearQtyBefore_b = fundedYearQtyBeforeHonest_b + realloc_b           // moves toward the buying portion
    excessQtyBefore_b     = excessQtyBeforeHonest_b − realloc_b
else:
    fundedYearQtyBefore_b = fundedYearQtyBeforeHonest_b
    excessQtyBefore_b     = excessQtyBeforeHonest_b

fundedYearQtyDelta_b = fundedYearQtyAfter_b − fundedYearQtyBefore_b
excessQtyDelta_b     = excessQtyAfter_b − excessQtyBefore_b
```

`fundedYearQtyBefore` and `excessQtyBefore`, the reconciled values, are the Quantity Before, Cost Before and Excess Amount Before columns. The number displayed as Before is the number that the delta is computed against, so that Before + Delta = After on screen. In the usual same-direction case the two coincide, and in the opposite-sign case only the displayed split changes, and the total `qtyBefore_b` does not.

Three invariants hold:

1. `fundedYearQtyDelta_b` and `excessQtyDelta_b` never have opposite signs for one CUSIP.
2. `fundedYearQtyDelta_b + excessQtyDelta_b = qtyAfter_b − qtyBefore_b`: the reallocation changes the split of the total trade and not the total.
3. `fundedYearQtyBefore_b + fundedYearQtyDelta_b = fundedYearQtyAfter_b` and `excessQtyBefore_b + excessQtyDelta_b = excessQtyAfter_b`: Before + Delta = After for each portion and not only in total.

<a id="lower-bracket-priority-rule"></a>
**Lower bracket priority rule** (`bracketWeightsN`). The formulas, the floor of the active lower bracket, and the over-allocated and under-allocated cases are in [2.0 §Retained Bracket Excess](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#retained-bracket-excess), and replace the 2-bracket formula across all lower brackets. In Rebalance:

- The retained excess cost of a maturity is `MAX(0, held qty − funded need) × cost per bond`, and the floor is the same quantity for the active lower bracket (`activeFloorWeight = activeFloorCost / totalCost`).
- The summary reports whether the match was reached (`bracketFellBack3to2`, `bracketSolveReason`).
- Each retained maturity has the target cost `totalCost × retainedWeight`, and the active lower bracket and the upper bracket have `totalCost × activeWeight` and `totalCost × upperWeight`.
- With no retained maturity (`R = 0`) the rule is the 2-bracket case: the active lower bracket is sized from `bracketWeights(dur_activeLower, dur_upper, dGap)`.
- The round-trip rounding of the partial sale is the [Round-Trip Rounding Note](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#retained-bracket-excess) of 2.0.

**Cash Delta** (`cashDelta`, `costDelta`):

```
cashDelta = −(qtyDelta × costPerTips)
  qtyDelta = qtyAfter − qtyBefore
```

Positive is cash received (a net sell) and negative is cash paid (a net buy). At a bracket-target CUSIP the Cash Deltas of the funded row and the excess row use `fundedYearQtyDelta` and `excessQtyDelta` (the reallocated split), and not a subtraction of the raw Before and After of each portion, which can produce a buy and a sell of one CUSIP. The row total `qtyAfter − qtyBefore` is the same either way, and only its split between the two rows changes.

<a id="bracket-identification-rules"></a>
## Bracket Identification Rules

The active lower bracket comes from the market data, and the retained maturities come from the holdings. The Brackets control ([6.0 §Row 2](./6.0_UI_Layout.md)) selects the mode: **2-bracket** or **Multi-bracket** (default).

### Active Lower Bracket (Canonical)

The [active lower bracket](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#active-lower-bracket) is the most recently issued outstanding 10-year TIPS in the market data that matures before `minGapYear`, the longest-dated 10-year maturity available. The holdings are not consulted. This is the search of `anchorBefore` in the gap parameter calculation. In **2-bracket** mode, older holdings that served as the lower bracket are not in `bracketYearSet`, and the rebalance rebuilds them to the funded-year quantity only and sells any excess.

<a id="retained-maturities-holdings-based"></a>
### Retained Maturities (Holdings-Based)

In **Multi-bracket** mode, `identifyBrackets` (`rebalance-lib.js`) picks one CUSIP of the holdings for the [retained lower bracket](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#retained-lower-bracket), older than the active lower bracket:

1. **CUSIP aggregation**: add up the quantity of each CUSIP across the portfolio, for the years from `firstYear`.
2. **Excess ARA**: the metric is `araByYear[y] − DARA`. Among the candidate years, the single year with the highest Excess ARA is the retained lower bracket. The Excess ARA is used here only.
3. **Tie-break**: among equal Excess ARA, the CUSIP with the highest aggregate quantity.
4. **Range**: the years in `[LOWEST_LOWER_BRACKET_YEAR, minGapYear)`, with a quantity above 0.
5. **Fallback**: with no candidate (for example `firstYear` inside the gap years), the active lower bracket alone carries the lower bracket excess, and the lower brackets reduce to the 2-bracket case.

**A second source: a holding in the maturity year of the active lower bracket.** A maturity year can hold more than one TIPS ([Bracket Year TIPS](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bracket-year-tips)), including the year of the active lower bracket: an earlier-maturing TIPS held from before the latest issue of that year became active. `rebalance-lib.js` checks the maturity year of the active lower bracket for any other held CUSIP maturing earlier, whether or not the Excess ARA pick finds a maturity (the two sources can both apply, or either alone). The funded need of that holding is calculated alone, with nothing credited forward from the active CUSIP. What it holds beyond that need is retained excess at its duration and enters the same solve as any retained maturity. An imported `excessQty` states the split for each CUSIP. The comparison with the active lower bracket is by CUSIP and never by year, because a shared maturity year does not make two TIPS the same bond.

**Implementation.** `runRebalance` resolves the canonical active lower bracket once per run, independently of `identifyBrackets`. A flag records whether it is a different bond from the pick of `identifyBrackets`, and that flag gates the cross-year source only and never the same-maturity-year check.

**One cross-year generation, plus the same-maturity-year case.** Any number of retained maturities can accumulate across different years as the active lower bracket advances: each rebalance buys the active lower bracket of its time, and when a later maturity is issued the previous one becomes retained, so the count grows by one with each issuance before the gap years. `bracketWeightsN` ([2.0 §Retained Bracket Excess](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#retained-bracket-excess)) solves for any number. The Excess ARA step of `identifyBrackets` returns one cross-year CUSIP, so only the most recent cross-year retained maturity enters the solve. Identifying and ordering several cross-year retained maturities from holdings is not implemented. The retained maturities found, the cross-year pick and the same-maturity-year one, are sorted earliest maturity first and depleted in that order when the match cannot otherwise be solved.

<a id="rebalance-algorithm-flow"></a>
## Rebalance Algorithm Flow

The rebalance runs the full ladder, gap brackets included, driven by the per-year DARA targets, which can be uneven ([2.0 §Average Gap Year Duration is Cost-Weighted](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#average-gap-year-duration-is-cost-weighted)).

### Phase 1: Derive Parameters

- Take the settlement date, the Ref CPI and the per-year DARA targets.
- Build the holdings ladder and the range ([Ladder Range](#ladder-range)).
- **DARA shape**: the per-year targets come from the panel store (derived at load, or reshaped by the holder). A derived Per-Year DARA that is not edited has the shape-preserving self-financing scale applied at Run ([Funding the rebalance](#funding-the-rebalance)), so that the executed targets net to non-negative cash. A reshaped store and the DARA block of an export of this app are used as given.
- Identify the current gap years.

### Phase 2: Gap Year and Future 30Y Parameters

- Calculate the **Gap Avg Duration** and the **Gap Total Cost** ([2.0 TIPS Ladders](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md)).
- Convert the Synthetic TIPS of the gap years at their issue dates, which gives the Excess TIPS after each conversion, the Net Cash and the interest credited to each funded year ([2.0 §Gap Year Simulated Outstanding TIPS](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#gap-year-simulated-outstanding-tips)).
- If the ladder has Future 30Y years, calculate the **Future 30Y Avg Duration** and the **Future 30Y Total Cost**, and convert their Synthetic TIPS at their issue dates ([2.0 §Future 30Y Simulated Outstanding TIPS](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#future-30y-simulated-outstanding-tips)).

### Phase 3: Bracket Resolution

- Identify the lower and upper bracket CUSIPs from the holdings ([Bracket Identification Rules](#bracket-identification-rules)).
- Calculate the modified duration of each bracket.
- Solve the weights ([2.0 TIPS Ladders](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md)), with the [lower bracket priority rule](#lower-bracket-priority-rule) when Retained Bracket Excess is present.
- If the ladder has Future 30Y years, identify the cover pair and solve the weights.

### Phase 4: Ladder Rebuild (longest to shortest)

Process every year in `[firstYear, lastYear]`, and every held, bracket and gap year, longest to shortest. Gap years and Future 30Y years have no holdings and are skipped.

- For a bracket year or a rebalance year:
  - Resolve the target CUSIP ([Target CUSIP resolution](#target-cusip-resolution)).
  - Calculate `needed = yearDara − incomingLMI − partialCredit − creditedInterest`. The incoming LMI is the running post-rebalance LMI, and for the settlement year the LMI of the remaining coupons ([2.0 §Settlement-year LMI](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#settlement-year-lmi)). The excess of a bracket year or cover year adds no interest to its maturity year, because it is sold before it matures ([Same-Maturity Excess Interest](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#same-maturity-excess-interest)).
  - For a zeroed year, the funded quantity is 0 and all non-target holdings are sold.
  - Otherwise size the non-target holdings and `targetCUSIP` as described under Target CUSIP resolution, and place the excess quantities on top of the funded quantity.
- Sell in full the holdings of the years after `lastYear` and before `firstYear`.
- Hold unchanged any other year.
- Add the post-rebalance quantity of every holding of the year, and of a CUSIP bought new, to the running LMI. Then take back the target excess of a bracket or cover year and the excess of a same-maturity-year retained maturity. The interest of the Excess TIPS and the Cover Excess, with the interest of the Simulated Outstanding Future 10Y TIPS and Future 30Y TIPS and the Net Cash, is credited to the funded years in which it is paid, worked out for each year directly and not carried in the running LMI ([2.0 §Gap Year Simulated Outstanding TIPS](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#gap-year-simulated-outstanding-tips), [§Interest credited to a funded year](./2.0_TIPS_Ladders.md#gap-interest-credited)).

## Excess Balance Check

A diagnostic of how well the current holdings funded the gap coverage in advance, and not the funding invariant. The funding invariant is net cash (`costDeltaSum`) at or above 0 and small, which the self-financing scale guarantees ([Funding the rebalance](#funding-the-rebalance)). `runRebalance` reports it in the summary as `gapCoverageSurplus`:

```
Surplus/(Shortfall) = Previous excess cost − Cost to fill rebalance years − Gap theoretical cost
```

`Previous excess cost` is `totalPreviousExcessCost`, the cost of the excess held now. `Cost to fill rebalance years` is `costForNewRungs`, the target cost of the non-bracket rungs. `Gap theoretical cost` is the Gap Total Cost.

A value near 0 says that the portfolio held the bracket excess that the gap needs. A large negative value is expected for a basic file (a broker or other CSV that this app did not build) because it did not pre-fund the gap, and the rebalance buys that excess now. The rebalance still self-finances: the shape-preserving scale sells the funded rungs to raise the cash, so `costDeltaSum` is small and non-negative even when this surplus is deeply negative. The check answers whether the earlier portfolio carried its gap coverage, and `costDeltaSum` answers whether the rebalance is self-financing.
