# 6.3 Render TIPS table

**Specifies:** [6.3 Render TIPS table](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TIPSREFERENCE)
**Implemented by:** `TipsReference/index.html` (`renderTable`, `buildHeader`, `getFilteredSorted`, `cellDisplay`, `showCpnModal`), `shared/src/date-range-filter.js`
**Input:** [TIPS Reference Rows](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-reference-rows) from [Calculate index ratios and coupons (6.2)](./6.2_Calculate_Index_Ratios_And_Coupons.md); the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) and the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) from [Load TIPS data (6.1)](./6.1_Load_TIPS_Data.md); [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) from the user
**Output:** [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables) to the user; [TIPS Reference Rows](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-reference-rows) to [Export TIPS table (6.4)](./6.4_Export_TIPS_Table.md)

Draw the table of TIPS and its header.

<a id="header"></a>
## Header

- Show the Portal link, the title "TIPS Reference" and, once loaded:
  - Price Date: the date of the quotes, the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) under Market quotes.
  - Settlement: the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of the quotes.

<a id="columns"></a>
## Columns

| # | Column | Value | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CUSIP | [CUSIP](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#cusip) | text |
| 2 | Maturity | [Maturity Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date) | mm/dd/yyyy |
| 3 | Coupon | [Coupon Rate](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#coupon-rate) | percent, 3 decimal places |
| 4 | Dated Date | [Dated Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#dated-date) | mm/dd/yyyy |
| 5 | Dated Date Ref CPI | the [Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) on the Dated Date | 5 decimal places |
| 6 | Price | [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) | 3 decimal places |
| 7 | Index Ratio | the [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio) at the Ref CPI Date ([6.2](./6.2_Calculate_Index_Ratios_And_Coupons.md#index-ratio)) | 5 decimal places |
| 8 | Adj Price | Price × Index Ratio ([Adjusted Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#adjusted-price)) | 3 decimal places |
| 9 | Yield | [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) | percent, 3 decimal places |
| 10 | Term | [Term](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#term) | 2 decimal places and "y" |
| 11 | Mac Duration | Macaulay [Duration](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#duration) | 2 decimal places |
| 12 | Mod Duration | Duration ÷ (1 + Yield / 2) | 2 decimal places |
| 13 | Adj Accrued/$1K | [Accrued Interest (Adjusted)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#accrued-interest-adjusted) at the Settlement Date, on $1,000 of par ([6.2](./6.2_Calculate_Index_Ratios_And_Coupons.md#accrued-interest)) | 4 decimal places |
| 14 | Cpn Date | the next coupon date ([Next Coupon](./6.2_Calculate_Index_Ratios_And_Coupons.md#next-coupon)) | mm/dd/yyyy |
| 15 | Cpn Date IR | the Index Ratio on the Cpn Date ([Coupon Payment](./6.2_Calculate_Index_Ratios_And_Coupons.md#coupon-payment)) | 5 decimal places |
| 16 | Adj Cpn/$1K | the coupon paid on $1,000 of par ([Coupon Payment](./6.2_Calculate_Index_Ratios_And_Coupons.md#coupon-payment)) | 4 decimal places |

- Show "—" for a value that is missing. The Cpn Date column has the tooltip "Coupon payment date on or after the selected Ref CPI Date".
- Align text columns left and number columns right.
- Show the three columns Cpn Date, Cpn Date IR and Adj Cpn/$1K only while at least one TIPS has a Adj Cpn/$1K value. When none has, show neither the columns nor their filter boxes, apply no filter or date range of theirs, and if the table is sorted by one of them, sort by Maturity, ascending. The Ref CPI Date decides which coupons have a Ref CPI, so the columns appear and disappear as the Ref CPI Date changes.
- The Adj Accrued/$1K column has the tooltip "Accrued Interest (Adjusted) per 1,000 of par at the Settlement Date".

<a id="table"></a>
## Table

- Sort by a click on a column header: the first click sorts ascending, a second click on the same header reverses, and a different header starts ascending. Mark the sorted header with ↑ or ↓. Start sorted by Maturity, ascending.
  - Sort CUSIP as text, the three date columns by their `YYYY-MM-DD` value and the other columns as numbers, with a missing value before every number.
- Place a filter box under each column header, which keeps a row whose displayed text for the column contains the typed text, ignoring case. Mark a box with text in amber. Apply the filters and the sort together on every keystroke.
- Place a calendar button in the filter cell of Maturity, Dated Date and Cpn Date, which opens a From and a To date picker (`shared/src/date-range-filter.js`). Keep the rows whose date lies within the range, comparing `YYYY-MM-DD` text.
- Show only outstanding TIPS at first: those whose Maturity Date, moved to its payment date, is today or later. A TIPS maturing on a weekend or a Bond Holiday therefore stays until the next bond trading day, and a TIPS whose payment date is today is shown. That is an exception to the rule that a TIPS is no longer outstanding from its payment date, made so that the coupon paid that day can be compared with the payment in a broker account. Today is the date in New York.
- State "N of M TIPS" above the table: N rows after the outstanding choice and every filter, and M the rows of S2.
- Switch the outstanding choice with the button beside the count, labelled "Show all" while only outstanding TIPS show and "Show outstanding" while all show.
- Alternate the row shading, with no highlight on hover, so that the shading stays while a row is selected for copying.
- Keep the two header rows in place while the table scrolls.
- Open the Coupon Calculation window from an Adj Cpn/$1K cell that has a value. It lists the CUSIP, the Coupon Date, the Coupon Rate (3 decimal places), the Semi-Annual Rate (4 decimal places), the Index Ratio, the formula `Index Ratio × (Coupon Rate / 2) × 1,000` with and without the numbers, and the Coupon Payment to 4 decimal places per 1,000. Close it with the ✕, a click outside it, or Escape.
