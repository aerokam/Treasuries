# 1.0 TIPS Reference

**Implemented by:** `TipsReference/index.html`, `shared/src/market-data.js#loadMarketData`, `shared/src/ref-cpi.js`, `shared/src/bond-math.js`, `shared/src/settlement.js`

Show one row for every TIPS in [TIPS reference data (S2)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s2), with the quote of [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7) where there is one, the [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio) at a Ref CPI Date the user selects, the Term and Duration, and the next coupon.

## Dependencies

- [TIPS Basics](../../knowledge/TIPS_Basics.md): the Index Ratio, the Inflation-Adjusted Principal and the coupon of a TIPS.
- [Data Pipeline (TipsLadderManager 3.1) §4.0 Yield Sources](../../TipsLadderManager/knowledge/3.1_Data_Pipeline.md): the sources `loadMarketData()` chooses between and the settlement date of each. This app and TipsLadderManager read the same function, so they price from the same source.

<a id="data"></a>
## Data

When the page opens, call `loadMarketData()` and build the rows:

- Take the row set from S2: every TIPS it lists, matured or not.
- Join the quote by CUSIP. Under Market quotes, Price is the ask price of [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7) and Yield is the [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) calculated from that price at the Settlement Date. A TIPS with no quote has no Price, Yield, Adj Price, Mac Duration or Mod Duration.
- Set Term to the [Term](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#term) from the Settlement Date to the Maturity Date, shown in years, or to none when the Maturity Date is not after the Settlement Date.
- Set Mac Duration to the [Duration](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#duration) from the Settlement Date, and Mod Duration to that Duration divided by `1 + Yield / 2`. Term and both durations are measured from the Settlement Date and do not change with the Ref CPI Date.
- Show the status text in the header while loading, and the error message in red when the load fails.

<a id="header"></a>
## Header

- Show the Portal link, the title "TIPS Reference" and, once loaded:
  - Price Date: the date of the quotes, the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) under Market quotes.
  - Settlement: the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of the quotes.
  - Ref CPI Date: a date picker, starting at the Settlement Date and bounded by the first and last dates of [Ref CPI (S3)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s3). A new date recalculates Index Ratio, Adj Price and the coupon columns.
  - Ref CPI: the [Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) of the Ref CPI Date, to 5 decimal places, or "—" when S3 has no value for that date.
- Show the Export CSV button, disabled until the data loads.

<a id="columns"></a>
## Columns

| # | Column | Value | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CUSIP | [CUSIP](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#cusip) | text |
| 2 | Maturity | [Maturity Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date) | `YYYY-MM-DD` |
| 3 | Coupon | [Coupon Rate](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#coupon-rate) | percent, 3 decimal places |
| 4 | Dated Date | [Dated Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#dated-date) | `YYYY-MM-DD` |
| 5 | Dated Date Ref CPI | the [Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) on the Dated Date | 5 decimal places |
| 6 | Price | [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) | 3 decimal places |
| 7 | Index Ratio | the Ref CPI of the Ref CPI Date divided by the Dated Date Ref CPI, truncated to six decimal places and then rounded to five, as Treasury rounds the ratio for the payments it calculates | 5 decimal places |
| 8 | Adj Price | Price × Index Ratio ([Adjusted Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#adjusted-price)) | 3 decimal places |
| 9 | Yield | [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) | percent, 3 decimal places |
| 10 | Term | [Term](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#term) | 2 decimal places and "y" |
| 11 | Mac Duration | Macaulay [Duration](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#duration) | 2 decimal places |
| 12 | Mod Duration | Duration ÷ (1 + Yield / 2) | 2 decimal places |
| 13 | Cpn Date | the next coupon date (see [Next Coupon](#next-coupon)) | mm/dd/yyyy |
| 14 | Cpn Date IR | the Index Ratio on the Cpn Date, rounded as column 7 | 5 decimal places |
| 15 | Cpn $ / TIPS | `(Coupon Rate / 2) × Cpn Date IR × 1,000`, the coupon paid on $1,000 of face value | 4 decimal places |

- Show "—" for a value that is missing. The Cpn Date column has the tooltip "Coupon payment date on or after the selected Ref CPI Date".
- Align text columns left and number columns right.
- Look up a Ref CPI by the exact date. A date outside the series of S3 has none, so a coupon dated beyond the last date of S3 shows "—" in Cpn Date IR and Cpn $ / TIPS.
- Set Index Ratio and Adj Price of a TIPS whose Maturity Date is before today from the Ref CPI of its Maturity Date, whatever the Ref CPI Date, because that is the principal Treasury redeemed. Today is the date in New York.

<a id="next-coupon"></a>
## Next Coupon

- Take as the coupon date the first coupon date of the TIPS on or after the Ref CPI Date, where the coupon dates are the month and day of the Maturity Date and the date six months from it. A TIPS with no coupon date on or after the Ref CPI Date has none.
- Treat a coupon whose payment date is today as the coupon, whatever the Ref CPI Date:
  - The payment date of a coupon or of the principal is the next bond trading day when the date falls on a weekend or a [Bond Holiday](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday).
  - Look for the coupon on or after the date 7 days before today, so a coupon dated before today and paid today is found.
  - For a TIPS whose Maturity Date is before today, the coupon is the one paid at maturity.
- Leave Cpn Date, Cpn Date IR and Cpn $ / TIPS empty for a TIPS matured before the Ref CPI Date unless its payment date is today.

<a id="table"></a>
## Table

- Sort by a click on a column header: the first click sorts ascending, a second click on the same header reverses, and a different header starts ascending. Mark the sorted header with ↑ or ↓. Start sorted by Maturity, ascending.
  - Sort Maturity, Dated Date, CUSIP and Cpn Date as text, and the other columns as numbers, with a missing value before every number.
- Place a filter box under each column header, which keeps a row whose displayed text for the column contains the typed text, ignoring case. Mark a box with text in amber. Apply the filters and the sort together on every keystroke.
- Place a calendar button in the filter cell of Maturity, Dated Date and Cpn Date, which opens a From and a To date picker (`shared/src/date-range-filter.js`). Keep the rows whose date lies within the range, comparing `YYYY-MM-DD` text.
- Show only outstanding TIPS at first: those whose Maturity Date, moved to its payment date, is today or later. A TIPS maturing on a weekend or a Bond Holiday therefore stays until the next bond trading day. Today is the date in New York.
- State "N of M TIPS" above the table: N rows after the outstanding choice and every filter, and M the rows of S2.
- Switch the outstanding choice with the button beside the count, labelled "Show all" while only outstanding TIPS show and "Show outstanding" while all show.
- Alternate the row shading, with no highlight on hover, so that the shading stays while a row is selected for copying.
- Keep the two header rows in place while the table scrolls.
- Open the Coupon Calculation window from a Cpn $ / TIPS cell that has a value. It lists the CUSIP, the Coupon Date, the Coupon Rate (3 decimal places), the Semi-Annual Rate (4 decimal places), the Index Ratio, the formula `Index Ratio × (Coupon Rate / 2) × $1,000` with and without the numbers, and the Coupon Payment to 4 decimal places per bond. Close it with the ✕, a click outside it, or Escape.

<a id="export"></a>
## Export

- Write the rows the table shows, in its sort order, to `tips-reference-` and the Settlement Date, as `.csv`.
- Use the column labels as the header row and the values as displayed, with these exceptions: Cpn $ / TIPS is written to 6 decimal places, so that a holder multiplying it by a quantity rounds once, to the cent, on the total. A value that contains a comma is quoted.
