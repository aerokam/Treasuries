# 6.2 Calculate index ratios and coupons

**Specifies:** [6.2 Calculate index ratios and coupons](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TIPSREFERENCE)
**Implemented by:** `shared/src/tips-reference-rows.js` (`buildTipsReferenceRows`, `applyInterestRefCpiDate`), `shared/src/tips-reference-table.js` (the Interest Ref CPI Date picker, `shared/src/date-picker.js`), `shared/src/ref-cpi.js` (`lookupRefCpi`, `indexRatio`), `shared/src/bond-math.js` (`_nextCouponOnOrAfter`, `accruedInterestAdjusted`), `shared/src/settlement.js` (`actualPaymentDate`, `todayEt`)
**Input:** [TIPS Reference Rows](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-reference-rows), the [Daily Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#daily-ref-cpi), [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday) and the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), from [Load TIPS data (6.1)](./6.1_Load_TIPS_Data.md); [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) from the user

Calculate, for every TIPS, the [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio) and the [Adjusted Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#adjusted-price) at the Settlement Date, and, for an Interest Ref CPI Date the user selects, the [Accrued Interest (Adjusted)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#accrued-interest-adjusted) and the next coupon.

<a id="settlement-ref-cpi"></a>
## Settlement Ref CPI

- Show the [Settlement Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-ref-cpi) beside the Settlement Date, to 5 decimal places, or "—" when [RefCPI (S3)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s3) has no value for that date.

<a id="index-ratio"></a>
## Index Ratio and Adj Price

- Look up a Ref CPI by the exact date. A date outside the series of S3 has none.
- Set Index Ratio to the Settlement Ref CPI divided by the Dated Date Ref CPI, truncated to six decimal places and then rounded to five, as Treasury rounds the ratio for the payments it calculates.
- Set Adj Price to Price × the Index Ratio not rounded ([Adjusted Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#adjusted-price)), because a broker does not round the Index Ratio in the adjusted prices it states ([Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio)). The Price is the quote at the Settlement Date, so the Index Ratio of Adj Price is the one of the Settlement Date.
- Set Index Ratio and Adj Price of a TIPS whose Maturity Date is before today from the Ref CPI of its Maturity Date instead of the Settlement Ref CPI, because that is the principal Treasury redeemed. Today is the date in New York.

<a id="interest-ref-cpi-date"></a>
## Interest Ref CPI Date

- Show a date picker for the [Interest Ref CPI Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#interest-ref-cpi-date), starting at the Settlement Date and bounded by the first and last dates of S3. A new date recalculates Adj Accrued/$1K and the coupon columns. It leaves Index Ratio and Adj Price as they are.
- Show the [Interest Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#interest-ref-cpi) beside the picker, to 5 decimal places, or "—" when S3 has no value for that date.

<a id="accrued-interest"></a>
## Accrued Interest

- Set Adj Accrued/$1K to [Accrued Interest (Adjusted)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#accrued-interest-adjusted), with the Interest Ref CPI Date as the date of the [Accrued Interest (Nominal)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#accrued-interest-nominal) and of the Index Ratio. A new Interest Ref CPI Date recalculates it.
- Calculate that Index Ratio from the Interest Ref CPI, truncated to six decimal places and then rounded to five as Index Ratio. Round the result to five decimal places for $1,000 par. `shared/src/bond-math.js#accruedInterestAdjusted` calculates it, from the Accrued Interest (Nominal) of `accruedInterest`.
- A TIPS whose Maturity Date is not after the Interest Ref CPI Date, whose Interest Ref CPI Date is before its [Dated Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#dated-date), or whose Interest Ref CPI Date has no Ref CPI, has none.

<a id="next-coupon"></a>
## Next Coupon

- Take as the coupon date the first coupon date of the TIPS on or after the Interest Ref CPI Date, where the coupon dates are the month and day of the Maturity Date and the date six months from it. A TIPS with no coupon date on or after the Interest Ref CPI Date has none.
- Treat a coupon whose payment date is today as the coupon, whatever the Interest Ref CPI Date:
  - The payment date of a coupon or of the principal is the next bond trading day when the date falls on a weekend or a [Bond Holiday](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday).
  - Look for the coupon on or after the date 7 days before today, so a coupon dated before today and paid today is found.
  - For a TIPS whose Maturity Date is before today, the coupon is the one paid at maturity.
- Leave Cpn Date, Cpn Date IR and Adj Cpn/$1K empty for a TIPS matured before the Interest Ref CPI Date unless its payment date is today.

<a id="coupon-payment"></a>
## Coupon Payment

- Set Cpn Date IR to the Index Ratio on the Cpn Date, rounded as Index Ratio.
- Set Adj Cpn/$1K to `(Coupon Rate / 2) × Cpn Date IR × 1,000`, the coupon paid on $1,000 of par.
- A coupon dated beyond the last date of S3 has no Ref CPI, so Cpn Date IR and Adj Cpn/$1K are empty.
