# 6.2 Calculate index ratios and coupons

**Specifies:** [6.2 Calculate index ratios and coupons](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TIPSREFERENCE)
**Implemented by:** `TipsReference/index.html` (`recomputeWithRefCpi`, `effectivePaymentDate`, `todayET`), `shared/src/ref-cpi.js` (`lookupRefCpi`, `indexRatio`), `shared/src/bond-math.js#_nextCouponOnOrAfter`, `shared/src/settlement.js`
**Input:** [TIPS Reference Rows](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-reference-rows), the [Daily Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#daily-ref-cpi), [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday) and the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), from [Load TIPS data (6.1)](./6.1_Load_TIPS_Data.md); [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) from the user
**Output:** [TIPS Reference Rows](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-reference-rows) to [Render TIPS table (6.3)](./6.3_Render_TIPS_Table.md); [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables) to the user (the Ref CPI of the Ref CPI Date)

Calculate, for every TIPS and a Ref CPI Date the user selects, the [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio), the [Adjusted Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#adjusted-price) and the next coupon, and for every TIPS the [Accrued Interest (Adjusted)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#accrued-interest-adjusted) at the Settlement Date.

<a id="ref-cpi-date"></a>
## Ref CPI Date

- Show a date picker for the Ref CPI Date, starting at the Settlement Date and bounded by the first and last dates of [Ref CPI (S3)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s3). A new date recalculates Index Ratio, Adj Price and the coupon columns.
- Show the [Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) of the Ref CPI Date, to 5 decimal places, or "—" when S3 has no value for that date.

<a id="index-ratio"></a>
## Index Ratio and Adj Price

- Look up a Ref CPI by the exact date. A date outside the series of S3 has none.
- Set Index Ratio to the Ref CPI of the Ref CPI Date divided by the Dated Date Ref CPI, truncated to six decimal places and then rounded to five, as Treasury rounds the ratio for the payments it calculates.
- Set Adj Price to Price × Index Ratio ([Adjusted Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#adjusted-price)).
- Set Index Ratio and Adj Price of a TIPS whose Maturity Date is before today from the Ref CPI of its Maturity Date, whatever the Ref CPI Date, because that is the principal Treasury redeemed. Today is the date in New York.

<a id="accrued-interest"></a>
## Accrued Interest

- Set Accrued Interest per 1,000 to [Accrued Interest (Adjusted)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#accrued-interest-adjusted), with the Settlement Date as the date of the [Accrued Interest (Nominal)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#accrued-interest-nominal) and of the Index Ratio.
- Calculate that Index Ratio from the Ref CPI of the Settlement Date, not rounded, whatever the Ref CPI Date. `shared/src/bond-math.js#accruedInterest` gives the Accrued Interest (Nominal).
- A TIPS whose Maturity Date is not after the Settlement Date, or whose Settlement Date has no Ref CPI, has none.

<a id="next-coupon"></a>
## Next Coupon

- Take as the coupon date the first coupon date of the TIPS on or after the Ref CPI Date, where the coupon dates are the month and day of the Maturity Date and the date six months from it. A TIPS with no coupon date on or after the Ref CPI Date has none.
- Treat a coupon whose payment date is today as the coupon, whatever the Ref CPI Date:
  - The payment date of a coupon or of the principal is the next bond trading day when the date falls on a weekend or a [Bond Holiday](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday).
  - Look for the coupon on or after the date 7 days before today, so a coupon dated before today and paid today is found.
  - For a TIPS whose Maturity Date is before today, the coupon is the one paid at maturity.
- Leave Cpn Date, Cpn Date IR and Cpn per 1,000 empty for a TIPS matured before the Ref CPI Date unless its payment date is today.

<a id="coupon-payment"></a>
## Coupon Payment

- Set Cpn Date IR to the Index Ratio on the Cpn Date, rounded as Index Ratio.
- Set Cpn per 1,000 to `(Coupon Rate / 2) × Cpn Date IR × 1,000`, the coupon paid on $1,000 of par.
- A coupon dated beyond the last date of S3 has no Ref CPI, so Cpn Date IR and Cpn per 1,000 are empty.
