# 6.1 Load TIPS data

**Specifies:** [6.1 Load TIPS data](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_TIPSREFERENCE)
**Implemented by:** `TipsReference/index.html` (`loadData`), `shared/src/market-data.js#loadMarketData`, `shared/src/tips-reference-rows.js#buildTipsReferenceRows`, `shared/src/bond-math.js`
**Input:** [TIPS reference data (S2)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s2), [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7), [Ref CPI (S3)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s3), [Yield curves (S13)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s13) and [Bond holidays (S16)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s16)

Read the stores and join each TIPS of S2 to its quote. The stores are the ones [Load market data §Source selection](../../TipsLadderManager/knowledge/4.1_Load_Market_Data.md#source-selection) names for `loadMarketData()`, so this app and TipsLadderManager price from the same source.

<a id="data"></a>
When the page opens, call `loadMarketData()` and build the rows:

- Take the row set from S2: every TIPS it lists, matured or not.
- Join the quote by CUSIP. Under Market quotes, Price is the ask price of [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7) and Yield is the [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) calculated from that price at the Settlement Date. A TIPS with no quote has no Price, Yield, Adj Price, Mac Duration or Mod Duration.
- Set SA Yield to the [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) of the Market TIPS security row of [Yield curves (S13)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s13) for the CUSIP, or to none where S13 has no such row.
- Set Term to the [Term](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#term) from the Settlement Date to the Maturity Date, shown in years, or to none when the Maturity Date is not after the Settlement Date.
- Set Mac Duration to the [Duration](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#duration) from the Settlement Date, and Mod Duration to that Duration divided by `1 + Yield / 2`. Term and both durations are measured from the Settlement Date and do not change with the Interest Ref CPI Date.
- Show the status text in the header while loading, and the error message in red when the load fails.
