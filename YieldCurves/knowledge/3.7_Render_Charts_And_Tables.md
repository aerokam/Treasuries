**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js`

## Scope

Everything upstream of this stage produces series. This stage decides which of them the user is currently asking for, what range of them is visible, and draws them.

It is the only process in the app that the user interacts with, so both flows to the user cross this boundary: the selections coming in, and the charts and tables going out.

Nothing here writes a data store, and nothing here calculates a yield. A view that appears to show a figure no other process produced is a defect in this stage, not a new calculation.

## Processes

<a id="select-view"></a>
### 3.7.1 Select view

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#switchTab`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#switchChartMode`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#processAndRender`

Takes the user's tab and mode selections and determines which view the other processes build.

- Select a tab: Treasuries, TIPS or breakeven.
- Select a mode: spread mode replaces the yield charts with bid and ask spread charts.
  - If the tab is breakeven: spread mode is left off and the source controls shown are the TIPS strip rather than a set of its own — breakeven is market-quoted only, so the two selections aren't independent.

**Input:** the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections), from the user.

**Output:** the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections), to [Build axis scales (3.7.2)](#build-scales) and to whichever of [Draw Treasuries view (3.7.3)](#draw-treasuries) to [Draw spread view (3.7.6)](#draw-spreads) that selection calls for.

<a id="build-scales"></a>
### 3.7.2 Build axis scales

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#buildYieldXScale`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#rescaleToVisible`, `shared/src/chart-time-axis.js`, `shared/src/chart-keys.js`

Determines what part of the series is visible, before any of it is drawn. Three things decide it, all specified in [Visual Standards](./Visual_Standards.md):

- Set the horizontal axis: calendar or term.
- Rescale the vertical axis to fit whatever is currently shown, whenever the selection changes.
  - Clip it first: [IQR Clip](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#iqr-clip) sets a floor derived from the data, which keeps a few securities at extreme negative yields from setting the lower bound for every other security. This moves the axis and never removes a point — a clipped view is still showing every security, and a figure read off the table will not agree with what the eye can see on the chart.
- Hold each tab's own zoom separately: a change of tab leaves the previous tab's zoom unchanged.
- Respond to the arrow keys, `+`/`-` and the mouse wheel for pan and zoom (`shared/src/chart-keys.js`, shared with `CpiExplorer` and `YieldsMonitor` rather than reimplemented here).

**Input:** the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections), from [Select view (3.7.1)](#select-view), and the series of the selected view.

**Output:** the [Axis Scales](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#axis-scales), to whichever of [Draw Treasuries view (3.7.3)](#draw-treasuries) to [Draw spread view (3.7.6)](#draw-spreads) is drawn.

<a id="draw-treasuries"></a>
### 3.7.3 Draw Treasuries view

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderNominalsChart`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderNominalsTable`

Draws the nominal securities as a chart and a table: each security's [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield), and the fitted nominal spot curve when Spot is shown. Both sources arrive as [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields), calculated in [Calculate Treasury yields (3.1.8)](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md#calculate-treasury-yields).

- Bills, Notes, Bonds, STRIPS and Spot can each be hidden independently, and the axis rescales to what remains, per [Build axis scales (3.7.2)](#build-scales).
  - There is no separate Ask choice: Ask is simply each type's own point, shown whenever that type is.
- Unclassified is a sixth type, with no checkbox of its own most of the time.
  - If a Market-quote CUSIP root the [Treasury CUSIP Reference](../../knowledge/Treasury_CUSIP_Reference.md) does not recognise actually occurs (per [Parse market quotes (3.1.2)](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md#parse-market-quotes)): its checkbox appears — hidden until then, not just unchecked, absent — and starts checked. A permanent, opt-in checkbox for something that (as of this writing) has never occurred would be easy to become blind to; an unexplained new row and checkbox appearing cannot be missed the same way.
- Spot's own fit always uses the Bill/Note/Bond set — STRIPS and Unclassified excluded — and is unaffected by which types are hidden or by the Maturity Range, per [Visual Standards](./Visual_Standards.md).

**Input:** the [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields) and the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date), from [Parse sources and calculate yields (3.1)](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md); the [Spot Yield Curves](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#spot-yield-curves), from [Fit spot yield curves (3.4)](./3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md); the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) and the [Axis Scales](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#axis-scales).

**Output:** the [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables), to the user.

<a id="draw-tips"></a>
### 3.7.4 Draw TIPS view

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderChart`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderTable`

Draws the TIPS as a chart and a table: the quoted yields, the [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield), the [SAO Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yield), and the fitted spot curves.

- Each series can be shown or hidden separately.
- Both sources can be drawn at once, so the same security may appear twice at different yields — that is the source comparison, not a duplicate.
- If the analysis view is opened with `?gsw`: the published curve from [GSW curve parameters (S12)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s12) is also drawn, as a reference against the app's own fit.

**Input:** the [SAO Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yields), from [Adjust for other effects (3.3)](./3.3_Adjust_For_Other_Effects.md); the [Spot Yield Curves](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#spot-yield-curves), from [Fit spot yield curves (3.4)](./3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md); the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date), from [Parse sources and calculate yields (3.1)](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md); the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) and the [Axis Scales](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#axis-scales).

**Output:** the [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables), to the user; a [Drill Request](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#drill-request), to [Answer drill request (3.7.7)](#answer-a-drill).

<a id="draw-breakeven"></a>
### 3.7.5 Draw breakeven view

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderBeiChart`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderBeiTable`

Draws breakeven inflation, both methods [Calculate breakeven inflation (3.5)](./3.5_Calculate_Breakeven_Inflation.md) produces:

- Per security: the nominal yield less each of the ask, SA and SAO yields. This pairs each TIPS with the nearest-maturity nominal, so the pairing is approximate wherever no nominal matures near that TIPS.
- Across terms: the fitted nominal curve less the fitted TIPS curve. This has no pairing to approximate — both are read at the same term on both curves.

**Input:** the [Breakeven Inflation](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#breakeven-inflation), from [Calculate breakeven inflation (3.5)](./3.5_Calculate_Breakeven_Inflation.md); the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) and the [Axis Scales](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#axis-scales).

**Output:** the [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables), to the user.

<a id="draw-spreads"></a>
### 3.7.6 Draw spread view

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderSpreadCharts`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderSpreadTable`

Draws the bid and ask spread for each security, in yield and in price, for TIPS and for nominal Treasuries.

Only the market quotes carry both sides, so this view has one source. Its outlier clipping is specified separately from the yield charts' in [Visual Standards](./Visual_Standards.md), the two distributions having different ranges.

**Input:** the [Bid and Ask Spreads](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bid-and-ask-spreads), from [Calculate bid and ask spreads (3.6)](./3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md); the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) and the [Axis Scales](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#axis-scales).

**Output:** the [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables), to the user.

<a id="answer-a-drill"></a>
### 3.7.7 Answer drill request

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#_showDrillPopup`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#_showSaDrill`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#_showSaoDrill`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#_showColHelp`

Shows, for one security the user picks, how a figure in the view above was arrived at: the seasonal adjustment for that security, the outlier adjustment applied to it, or what a column means.

It reports the intermediate values of another process rather than that process's result, which is why it is specified separately from the view the request is raised on.

**Input:** a [Drill Request](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#drill-request), from [Draw TIPS view (3.7.4)](#draw-tips).

**Output:** the [Drill Popup](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#drill-popup), to the user.

---

## What This Stage Must Not Do

A figure calculated in this stage would be a figure no spec accounts for and no other consumer of the same series could reproduce. Every figure drawn here is produced upstream and passed in.

The one thing this stage does decide for itself is what is visible: which series, which range, and where the axis is clipped. Those are choices about the view, not about the data, and [Visual Standards](./Visual_Standards.md) specifies them.
