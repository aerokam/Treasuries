# 3.7 Render charts and tables

**Specifies:** [3.7 Render charts and tables](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_YC_RENDER) and the seven processes it decomposes into  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js`  

Read the series produced upstream and the user's [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections), and produce the [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables):

- The user selects which series are shown, what range of them is visible, and where the axis is clipped. These are choices about the view, specified in [Build axis scales (3.7.2)](#build-scales) and in the processes that draw each view.
- This is the only process in Yield Curves the user interacts with. The selections arrive here and the charts and tables leave from here.

---

## Processes

<a id="select-view"></a>
### 3.7.1 Select view

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#switchTab`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#switchChartMode`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#processAndRender`

Read the user's tab and mode selections and produce the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) that determine which view the other processes build:

- Select a tab: Treasuries, TIPS or breakeven.
- Select a mode: spread mode replaces the yield charts with bid and ask spread charts.
  - If the tab is breakeven: leave spread mode off, and show the TIPS source controls. Breakeven is calculated for the market-quote source only ([Calculate breakeven inflation (3.5)](./3.5_Calculate_Breakeven_Inflation.md)).

**Input:** the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections), from the user.

**Output:** the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections), to [Build axis scales (3.7.2)](#build-scales) and to whichever of [Draw Treasuries view (3.7.3)](#draw-treasuries) to [Draw spread view (3.7.6)](#draw-spreads) that selection calls for.

<a id="build-scales"></a>
### 3.7.2 Build axis scales

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#buildYieldXScale`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#rescaleToVisible`, `shared/src/chart-time-axis.js`, `shared/src/chart-keys.js`

Read the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) and the series of the selected view, and produce the [Axis Scales](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#axis-scales) that decide what part of the series is visible:

- Set the horizontal axis: calendar or term.
  - Calendar: place the ticks with `calendarTimeAxis()` ([chart-time-axis.js (M12)](../../knowledge/SharedModules.md#m12)).
  - Term: show the time to maturity. Label a whole number of years `2y`, `5y`, never `2.0y`, and a part of a year `2y 6m`, never `2.5y`. On the Treasuries tab, place the ticks by the visible span in weeks (w):

    | Visible span | Tick every | Label |
    |---|---|---|
    | over 260w | 52w | `Xy` |
    | over 104w, up to 260w | 26w | `Xy`, `Xy 6m` |
    | over 52w, up to 104w | 13w | `Xy`, `Xy 3m`, `Xy 6m`, `Xy 9m` |
    | over 12w, up to 52w | 4w | `Xm` below one year, `Xy Zm` from one year |
    | up to 12w | 1w | `Xw` |

  - On the TIPS and spread charts, which use a time scale, Chart.js picks the unit from the visible range and the tick label is `Xw` up to 91 days, `Xm` up to 365 days and `Xy Zm` beyond, the mixed format of the table above, so a sub-yearly tick never repeats a year label.
- Round the bounds of the vertical axis (Yield %) out to the step of `snapYBounds` ([chart-keys.js (M11)](../../knowledge/SharedModules.md#m11)) when the view loads and after a reset, and round them to the step in force during a zoom or pan. Draw a horizontal gridline at every tick. Horizontal and vertical gridlines share one color (`GRID_COLOR`) on every chart, in Maturity and in Term mode, and `calendarTimeAxis()` receives it as `gridColor`.
- Rescale the vertical axis to fit what is shown, whenever the selection changes.
  - A fitted curve contributes to the fit only while it is shown, and is not clipped.
  - On the Treasuries tab, clip first: [IQR Clip](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#iqr-clip) sets a floor derived from the data, so that a few securities at extreme negative yields do not set the lower bound for every other security. The clip moves the axis and removes no point from the chart or the table.
    - The Clip Outliers checkbox is on by default. Checking or unchecking it clears the saved zoom and refits the axes.
    - The clip applies only while Bills or Notes are shown. Bonds and STRIPS are left out of it, because their yields range too widely for the fence. It does not apply to the Spot curve or to the TIPS tab.
    - Apply it on the first draw and in every rescale to the visible data (a legend click, a reset).
  - A checkbox that changes what is shown (a security type of Bills, Notes, Bonds, STRIPS or Spot, or Unclassified while it is shown) clears the saved zoom and refits both axes to the remaining data.
  - A source toggle (FedInvest, Market) rebuilds the chart with the X zoom and pan and the Y scale that it had, and changes no axis bound. A date range change, a table sort and a legend click do not clear the zoom either: a legend click refits the Y-axis to the visible data, which changes nothing when the remaining series share the range.
- Hold each tab's zoom separately: a change of tab leaves the zoom of the tab left unchanged and restores the zoom, and the date range, that the tab entered last had.
- Reset the view: clear the saved zoom, reset the zoom plugin and refit the vertical axis to the visible data, with the clip applied when it is on.
- Pan with a drag in any direction. Zoom both axes at once with the mouse wheel or a pinch. Ctrl with the wheel zooms the X-axis only and Shift with the wheel the Y-axis only. The arrow keys and `+`/`-` pan and zoom ([chart-keys.js (M11)](../../knowledge/SharedModules.md#m11)).
- Show or hide a series with a click on its legend item.

**Input:** the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections), from [Select view (3.7.1)](#select-view), and the series of the selected view.

**Output:** the [Axis Scales](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#axis-scales), to whichever of [Draw Treasuries view (3.7.3)](#draw-treasuries) to [Draw spread view (3.7.6)](#draw-spreads) is drawn.

<a id="draw-treasuries"></a>
### 3.7.3 Draw Treasuries view

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderNominalsChart`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderNominalsTable`

Read the [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields) of both sources, calculated in [Calculate Treasury yields (3.1.8)](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md#calculate-treasury-yields), and the fitted nominal spot curve, and draw them as a chart and a table: each security's [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield), and the spot curve when Spot is shown.

- Bills, Notes, Bonds, STRIPS and Spot can each be hidden independently, and the axis rescales to what remains ([Build axis scales (3.7.2)](#build-scales)).
  - Ask is each type's point, shown whenever that type is.
- There is no Ask checkbox: Ask is the point of each type and is shown whenever that type is, because the Treasuries tab has one representation of each security type, its quoted yield.
- Unclassified is a sixth type.
  - Its checkbox is absent until a Market-quote CUSIP root that the [Treasury CUSIP Reference](../../knowledge/Treasury_CUSIP_Reference.md) does not recognise occurs ([Parse market quotes (3.1.2)](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md#parse-market-quotes)). When one occurs, the checkbox appears checked. A new row and checkbox that appear unannounced are noticed, which a permanent opt-in checkbox for a type that has not occurred would not be.
- The spot curve is fitted as [Fit spot yield curves (3.4)](./3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md) states, and hiding a type or cropping the Maturity Range does not change it: the fit uses the complete Bill, Note and Bond set, with STRIPS and Unclassified left out, whichever types are checked and whatever the Maturity Range. The Maturity Range crops which part of the fitted curve is drawn or listed, the same as it crops the points of every other type.
- The Maturity Range fields (start and end date) recompute to the union of the maturities of every checked selection, the security types and Spot, whenever one of them is checked or unchecked: checking Spot with Bills widens the range to the full curve, and unchecking a selection narrows it to what remains checked. A value typed by hand stands until the next checkbox change recomputes the range. Nothing else changes it (a sort, Clip Outliers, a source toggle).

**Input:** the [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields) and the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date), from [Parse sources and calculate yields (3.1)](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md); the [Spot Yield Curves](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#spot-yield-curves), from [Fit spot yield curves (3.4)](./3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md); the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) and the [Axis Scales](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#axis-scales).

**Output:** the [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables), to the user.

<a id="draw-tips"></a>
### 3.7.4 Draw TIPS view

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderChart`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderTable`

Read the [SAO Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yields) and the fitted spot curves, and draw them as a chart and a table: the quoted yields, the [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield), the [SAO Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yield) and the spot curves.

- Each series can be shown or hidden separately. The Ask, SA, SAO, Spot and Spot SA checkboxes choose among several representations of the same TIPS, which is why this tab has an Ask checkbox.
- Draw the series in this order, from the bottom: Ask (circles), the unadjusted quote, which is the Price that [Select prices and add TIPS reference data (1.1.2)](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#select-prices-and-add-tips-reference-data) selects for the FedInvest source and the Ask Price for the Market source; the [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) (circles); the [SAO Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yield) (circles), on top. Draw the FedInvest source with dotted lines and the Market source with solid lines. A FedInvest yield is usually higher than the Market ask yield of the same security, most of all for Bills, because the Buy price it is calculated from is a midpoint of the dealer bid and ask ([FedInvest Treasury Pricing Methodology](./FedInvest_Treasury_Pricing_Methodology.md)).
- Both sources can be drawn at once. The same security then appears once per source, at that source's yield.
- If the analysis view is opened with `?gsw`: draw the published curve from [GSW curve parameters (S12)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s12) as a reference against the fitted spot curve.

**Input:** the [SAO Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yields), from [Adjust for other effects (3.3)](./3.3_Adjust_For_Other_Effects.md); the [Spot Yield Curves](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#spot-yield-curves), from [Fit spot yield curves (3.4)](./3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md); the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date), from [Parse sources and calculate yields (3.1)](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md); the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) and the [Axis Scales](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#axis-scales).

**Output:** the [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables), to the user; a [Drill Request](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#drill-request), to [Answer drill request (3.7.7)](#answer-a-drill).

<a id="draw-breakeven"></a>
### 3.7.5 Draw breakeven view

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderBeiChart`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderBeiTable`

Read the [Breakeven Inflation](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#breakeven-inflation) and draw it as a chart and a table, by both methods of [Calculate breakeven inflation (3.5)](./3.5_Calculate_Breakeven_Inflation.md):

- Per security: the nominal yield less each of the ask, SA and SAO yields.
- Across terms: the fitted nominal curve less the fitted TIPS SA curve.

**Input:** the [Breakeven Inflation](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#breakeven-inflation), from [Calculate breakeven inflation (3.5)](./3.5_Calculate_Breakeven_Inflation.md); the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) and the [Axis Scales](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#axis-scales).

**Output:** the [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables), to the user.

<a id="draw-spreads"></a>
### 3.7.6 Draw spread view

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderSpreadCharts`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderSpreadTable`

Read the [Bid and Ask Spreads](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bid-and-ask-spreads) and draw the spread of each security, in yield and in price, for TIPS and for nominal Treasuries.

- The view has the market-quote source only ([Calculate bid and ask spreads (3.6)](./3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md)).
- The TIPS table also lists the [Adjusted Bid Price and Adjusted Ask Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#adjusted-price) that the price spread of each security is calculated from.
- The Yield Spread and Price Spread charts clip both the ceiling and the floor of the vertical axis, for TIPS and for Treasuries, by a local fence and not by the whole-series fence of the yield charts. A fence over a whole maturity curve hides a gradual real trend that is high only against an unrelated dense cluster, and misses a spike that is extreme only against its own neighbors.
  - The trend line is a kernel average (`_kernelAverageTrend`) evaluated on a 200-point grid with a fixed bandwidth of 1.5 years. The y-values of each grid point's window are first limited to the IQR fence of that window (`iqrClipBounds`, no minimum fence). This reshapes the fitted line and not the plotted points.
  - A point is left out of the vertical axis minimum and maximum (`_clippedYRange`, `_localOutlierMask`) only when it falls outside the IQR fence of the points of nearby maturities, in the same window of 1.5 years and 4 points. Apply it on the first draw and in every rescale (a reset, a wheel zoom, a pan, a legend click: `_rescaleSpread`), so that a reset neither restores a clipped outlier nor clips a real trend.

**Input:** the [Bid and Ask Spreads](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bid-and-ask-spreads), from [Calculate bid and ask spreads (3.6)](./3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md); the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) and the [Axis Scales](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#axis-scales).

**Output:** the [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables), to the user.

<a id="answer-a-drill"></a>
### 3.7.7 Answer drill request

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#_showDrillPopup`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#_showSaDrill`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#_showSaoDrill`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#_showColHelp`

Read a [Drill Request](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#drill-request) and produce the [Drill Popup](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#drill-popup): for one security the user picks, the working of a figure in the view above, which is the seasonal adjustment for that security, the outlier adjustment applied to it, or what a column means.

- The popup states the intermediate values of another process, not that process's result, which is why this process is specified separately from the view the request is raised on.

**Input:** a [Drill Request](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#drill-request), from [Draw TIPS view (3.7.4)](#draw-tips).

**Output:** the [Drill Popup](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#drill-popup), to the user.
