# 3.7 Render charts and tables

**Specifies:** [3.7 Render charts and tables](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_YC_RENDER) and the seven processes it decomposes into  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js`  
**Constrained by:** [Visual Standards](./Visual_Standards.md)

## Scope

Everything upstream of this stage produces series. This stage decides which of them the user is currently asking for, what range of them is visible, and draws them.

It is the only process in the app that the user interacts with, so both flows to the user cross this boundary: the selections coming in, and the charts and tables going out.

Nothing here writes a data store, and nothing here calculates a yield. A view that appears to show a figure no other process produced is a defect in this stage, not a new calculation.

## Processes

<a id="select-view"></a>
### 3.7.1 Select view

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#switchTab`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#switchChartMode`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#processAndRender`

Takes the user's tab and mode selections and determines which view the other processes build. There are three tabs — Treasuries, TIPS and breakeven — and a spread mode that replaces the yield charts with bid and ask spread charts.

The two selections are not independent. Breakeven is market-quoted only, so selecting it leaves spread mode off, and the source controls it shows are the TIPS strip rather than a set of its own.

**Input:** the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections), from the user.

**Output:** the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections), to [3.7.2](#build-scales) and to whichever of [3.7.3](#draw-treasuries) to [3.7.6](#draw-spreads) that selection calls for.

<a id="build-scales"></a>
### 3.7.2 Build axis scales

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#buildYieldXScale`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#rescaleToVisible`, `shared/src/chart-time-axis.js`

Determines what part of the series is visible, before any of it is drawn.

Three things decide it, all specified in [Visual Standards](./Visual_Standards.md): the horizontal axis is either calendar or term; the vertical axis is rescaled to fit whatever is currently shown when the selection changes; and each tab holds its own zoom, so a change of tab leaves the previous tab's zoom unchanged.

The vertical axis may also be clipped. [IQR Clip](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#iqr-clip) sets a floor derived from the data, which keeps a few securities at extreme negative yields from setting the lower bound for every other security. **It moves the axis and never removes a point** — a clipped view is still showing every security, and a figure read off the table will not agree with what the eye can see on the chart.

**Input:** the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections), from [3.7.1](#select-view), and the series of the selected view.

**Output:** the [Axis Scales](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#axis-scales), to whichever of [3.7.3](#draw-treasuries) to [3.7.6](#draw-spreads) is drawn.

<a id="draw-treasuries"></a>
### 3.7.3 Draw Treasuries view

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderNominalsChart`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderNominalsTable`

Draws the nominal securities as a chart and a table: each security's [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield), and the fitted nominal spot curve when Spot is shown. Both sources arrive as [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields), calculated in [3.1.8](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md#calculate-treasury-yields).

Bills, Notes, Bonds, STRIPS, Unclassified and Spot can each be hidden independently, and the axis rescales to what remains, per [3.7.2](#build-scales). There is no separate Ask choice: Ask is simply each of Bills/Notes/Bonds/STRIPS/Unclassified's own point, shown whenever that type is. Unclassified defaults to shown, unlike STRIPS, which defaults to hidden — it exists so a Market-quote CUSIP root the [Treasury CUSIP Reference](../../knowledge/Treasury_CUSIP_Reference.md) does not recognise can't go unnoticed, per [3.1.2](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md#parse-market-quotes). Spot's own fit always uses the Bill/Note/Bond set — STRIPS and Unclassified excluded — and is unaffected by which types are hidden or by the Maturity Range, per [Visual Standards](./Visual_Standards.md).

**Input:** the [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields) and the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date), from 3.1; the [Spot Yield Curves](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#spot-yield-curves), from 3.4; the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) and the [Axis Scales](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#axis-scales).

**Output:** the [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables), to the user.

<a id="draw-tips"></a>
### 3.7.4 Draw TIPS view

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderChart`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderTable`

Draws the TIPS as a chart and a table: the quoted yields, the [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield), the [SAO Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yield), the fitted spot curves, and the published curve from [GSW curve parameters (S12)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s12) drawn as a reference against the app's own fit, on the analysis view opened with `?gsw` only.

Each series can be shown or hidden separately, and both sources can be drawn at once, so the same security may appear twice at different yields. That is the source comparison rather than a duplicate.

**Input:** the [SAO Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yields), from 3.3; the [Spot Yield Curves](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#spot-yield-curves), from 3.4; the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date), from 3.1; the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) and the [Axis Scales](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#axis-scales).

**Output:** the [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables), to the user; a [Drill Request](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#drill-request), to [3.7.7](#answer-a-drill).

<a id="draw-breakeven"></a>
### 3.7.5 Draw breakeven view

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderBeiChart`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderBeiTable`

Draws breakeven inflation: per security, the nominal yield less each of the ask, SA and SAO yields; and across terms, the fitted nominal curve less the fitted TIPS curve.

The per-security figures pair each TIPS with the nearest-maturity nominal, so the pairing is approximate wherever no nominal matures near that TIPS. The curve figures do not have that problem, being read at the same term on both curves.

**Input:** the [Breakeven Inflation](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#breakeven-inflation), from 3.5; the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) and the [Axis Scales](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#axis-scales).

**Output:** the [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables), to the user.

<a id="draw-spreads"></a>
### 3.7.6 Draw spread view

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderSpreadCharts`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderSpreadTable`

Draws the bid and ask spread for each security, in yield and in price, for TIPS and for nominal Treasuries.

Only the market quotes carry both sides, so this view has one source. Its outlier clipping is specified separately from the yield charts' in [Visual Standards](./Visual_Standards.md), the two distributions having different ranges.

**Input:** the [Bid and Ask Spreads](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bid-and-ask-spreads), from 3.6; the [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) and the [Axis Scales](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#axis-scales).

**Output:** the [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables), to the user.

<a id="answer-a-drill"></a>
### 3.7.7 Answer drill request

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#_showDrillPopup`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#_showSaDrill`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#_showSaoDrill`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#_showColHelp`

Shows, for one security the user picks, how a figure in the view above was arrived at: the seasonal adjustment for that security, the outlier adjustment applied to it, or what a column means.

It reports the intermediate values of another process rather than that process's result, which is why it is specified separately from the view the request is raised on.

**Input:** a [Drill Request](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#drill-request), from [3.7.4](#draw-tips).

**Output:** the [Drill Popup](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#drill-popup), to the user.

---

## What This Stage Must Not Do

A figure calculated in this stage would be a figure no spec accounts for and no other consumer of the same series could reproduce. Every figure drawn here is produced upstream and passed in.

The one thing this stage does decide for itself is what is visible: which series, which range, and where the axis is clipped. Those are choices about the view, not about the data, and [Visual Standards](./Visual_Standards.md) specifies them.
