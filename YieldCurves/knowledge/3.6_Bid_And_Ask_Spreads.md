# 3.6 Bid and Ask Spreads

**Specifies:** [3.6 Compute bid and ask spreads](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_YIELDCURVES)  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#buildProcessedTipsBonds`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#renderNominalsTable`

Subtracts the [ask](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) side from the [bid](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bid) side, in yield and in price, for each security quoted with both.

Only the market quotes carry both sides, so this process has one source. Securities priced from [S1](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) alone produce no spread.

## Dependencies

- [DATA_DICTIONARY.md](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md) — [Ask / Bid](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask), [Clean Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price), [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio)
- [3.1 Load and Parse](./3.1_Load_And_Parse.md) — supplies the bid and ask prices
- [3.7 Rendering](./3.7_Rendering.md) — draws the result

---

<a id="bid-ask-spreads"></a>
## 1.0 Yield spread

Bid yield less ask yield, in basis points.

For TIPS both sides are calculated from price in [3.1 §2.7](./3.1_Load_And_Parse.md#build-priced-bonds) rather than read from the quote, so the two sides share one convention and the difference between them is a spread. Reading the quoted bid yield and calculating the ask yield would put a method difference into the result alongside the spread.

## 2.0 Price spread

Ask price less bid price, as a percentage of the ask price.

**TIPS use the index-ratio adjusted prices and nominal Treasuries use the quoted prices.** The adjusted price is what is actually paid for a TIPS, so the spread is stated on the amount that changes hands rather than on the real price. For a nominal Treasury the two are the same figure, so no adjustment arises.

**Output:** the yield and price spreads per security, to [3.7](./3.7_Rendering.md).
