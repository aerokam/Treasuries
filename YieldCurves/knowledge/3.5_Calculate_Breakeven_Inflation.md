**Specifies:** [3.5 Calculate breakeven inflation](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_YIELDCURVES)  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#processAndRenderBei`, `shared/src/breakeven.js#findClosestNominal`, `shared/src/breakeven.js#spotBreakevenAcrossTerms`  
**Input:** [SAO Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yields), from [Adjust for other effects (3.3)](./3.3_Adjust_For_Other_Effects.md); [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields), from [Parse sources and calculate yields (3.1)](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md); [Spot Yield Curves](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#spot-yield-curves), from [Fit spot yield curves (3.4)](./3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md)

Read the [SAO Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yields), the [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields) and the [Spot Yield Curves](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#spot-yield-curves), and produce the [Breakeven Inflation](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#breakeven-inflation): subtract a real yield from a nominal yield, per security and across terms. The two methods give different results.

- The app calculates the breakeven for the market-quote source only: it needs the TIPS and the nominal Treasuries quoted the same way. The acquisition job runs the across-terms method on both sources, for the BEI rows of [Yield curves (S13)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s13), and the per-security method on the market-quote source, for [Breakeven inflation (S14)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s14).

<a id="breakeven-inflation"></a>
## Per security

For each TIPS:

- Select the nominal Treasury whose Maturity Date is nearest to it, from the Bills, Notes and Bonds.
  - A STRIPS is excluded: it is not a sensible reference for a coupon TIPS.
  - Unclassified is excluded: its cash-flow structure is not known.
  - The selection is unconditional. The smallest absolute difference in Maturity Date wins however large that difference is, so a TIPS with no nominal maturing near it is still paired and its breakeven then compares two different terms. This is why the two methods differ most where nominal maturities are sparse.
- Calculate three breakevens: the selected nominal's [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) less, in turn, the [ask](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) Yield, the [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) and the [SAO Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yield) of that TIPS.

The same implementation selects the nominal for every row of [Breakeven inflation (S14)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s14) and for the app, so a row of S14 and the figure the app shows are stated against the same security.

<a id="across-terms"></a>
## Across terms

At each Term of the grid of [Fit spot yield curves (3.4)](./3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md) (every half-year, and the longest Term of each curve) where the Treasuries curve and the TIPS SA curve both exist:

- Read the [Spot Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#spot-yield) of the Treasuries curve at that Term. [3.4](./3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md) fits it to every Bill, Note and Bond, from a minimum Term of 0.25 years.
- Read the Spot Yield of the TIPS SA curve at the same Term. It is fitted to the seasonally adjusted prices, so this breakeven is a seasonally adjusted one throughout.
- Calculate the breakeven: the Treasuries Spot Yield less the TIPS SA Spot Yield, each on a semi-annual bond-equivalent basis.
- If either Spot Yield fails its sanity check (two percentage points outside the observed yields): omit that Term. If fewer than three Terms remain: produce no breakeven across terms.

The breakeven has no minimum or maximum Term of its own: it exists at every Term where both curves do. Both yields are read at the same Term, so the pairing of the per-security method does not arise. Where the two methods differ, the per-security method is the approximation.

**Output:** the per-security breakevens and the curve breakeven, to [Render charts and tables (3.7)](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md).
