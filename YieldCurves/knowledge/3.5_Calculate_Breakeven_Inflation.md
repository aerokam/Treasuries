# 3.5 Calculate breakeven inflation

**Specifies:** [3.5 Calculate breakeven inflation](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_YIELDCURVES)  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#processAndRenderBei`, `shared/src/breakeven.js#findClosestNominal`  
**Input:** [SAO Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yields), from [Adjust for other effects (3.3)](./3.3_Adjust_For_Other_Effects.md); [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields), from [Parse sources and calculate yields (3.1)](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md); [Spot Yield Curves](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#spot-yield-curves), from [Fit spot yield curves (3.4)](./3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md)  
**Output:** [Breakeven Inflation](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#breakeven-inflation), to [Render charts and tables (3.7)](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md)

Subtracts a real yield from a nominal yield. The app produces two of them, by two different methods, and they do not agree.

Neither this process nor [Calculate bid and ask spreads (3.6)](./3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md) reads a data store or calculates a yield. Every input is a yield or a price produced upstream, and each performs one subtraction on inputs already on a common basis. A result that looks wrong is a defect in the input or in the pairing, not in the arithmetic here.

## Per security

For each TIPS:

- Select the nominal Treasury whose Maturity Date is nearest to it, from Bills, Notes and Bonds.
  - A STRIPS is excluded: not a sensible reference for a coupon TIPS.
  - Unclassified is excluded: its cash-flow structure isn't known.
  - The selection is unconditional: the smallest absolute difference in Maturity Date wins however large that difference is, so a TIPS with no nominal maturing near it is still paired, and its breakeven then compares two different terms. This is a property of the method, not a data problem, and it is why the two methods disagree most where nominal maturities are sparse.
- Calculate three breakevens: the selected nominal's [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) less, in turn, the [ask](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) Yield, the [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield), and the [SAO Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yield) of that TIPS.

One implementation states the same selected nominal in every [Breakeven inflation (S14)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s14) row, so a published breakeven and the one the app shows are stated against the same security.

## Across terms

On a half-year grid, over the range where both fits are valid:

- Read the fitted nominal curve at that term. This fit is restricted to Notes and Bonds.
- Read the fitted TIPS curve at that same term. This fit uses the seasonally adjusted prices, so this breakeven is a seasonally adjusted one throughout.
- Calculate the breakeven: the nominal curve value less the TIPS curve value.

Both curves come from [Fit spot yield curves (3.4)](./3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md). This method has no pairing to go wrong: both yields are read at the same term by construction. Where the two methods disagree, the per-security method carries the approximation.

**Output:** the per-security breakevens and the curve breakeven, to [Render charts and tables (3.7)](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md).
