## Overview
TIPS are indexed to the Non-Seasonally Adjusted CPI (CPI-NSA), so their monthly performance carries a predictable seasonal pattern (higher inflation in spring, lower in autumn) on top of the real, idiosyncratic yield. The **[Seasonal Adjustment (SA)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor)** removes that predictable pattern, so real yields can be compared across maturity months on the same basis the market already prices them on. For the intuition behind why this works, see [Intuition: Seasonal Adjustments (SA)](./SA_Intuition.md) — this spec describes only the calculation itself.

---

## The Transformation

The seasonal adjustment is Canty (2009) Equation 14, the working approximation the apps use ([Canty.md](Canty.md) §4):

\[ SACP \approx CP \times \frac{S_{Settle}}{S_{Maturity}} \]

where \( CP \), \( SACP \), and \( S_t \) map from Canty's general inflation-linked-bond notation to these TIPS quantities ([Canty.md](Canty.md) §1a has the full table):

| Canty | TIPS term | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| \( CP \) | [Clean Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price) | the quoted real price, before inflation adjustment |
| \( SACP \) | [Seasonally Adjusted Clean Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sacp) | the clean price with the predictable seasonal component removed |
| \( S_t \) | [SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) on date \( t \) | \( RefCPI_{NSA}(t) / RefCPI_{SA}(t) \) |
| \( S_{Settle} / S_{Maturity} \) | [SA Price Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-price-factor) | the multiplier applied to the clean price |
| \( S_{Maturity} \) | [Maturity SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-sa-factor) | the SA Factor for the maturity date |

In app terms: `SACP ≈ CP × SA_Price_Factor`, where `SA_Price_Factor = SA_Factor(settlement date) / Maturity_SA_Factor`. `CP` is whichever quoted price is in use; the formula does not depend on the choice (see [Data Sources](#data-sources)).

Canty assumes the seasonal pattern repeats unchanged each year, which makes \( S_t \) depend only on the calendar position of \( t \) — its month and day, not the year — so that \( S_{Maturity} \) is fixed by the month and day of the maturity date alone. On that assumption, settlement and maturity falling on the same month and day (a whole number of years apart) makes the two factors equal, the SA Price Factor 1.0, and `SACP = CP`, so there is no adjustment; the factor departs from 1.0 only by the seasonal difference over the leftover partial year ([Canty.md](Canty.md) §4, the anniversary identity).

The app carries the assumption only as far as the measurement supports. The factor for a given month and day differs between years, by an amount that grows with the distance from BLS's estimation window ([Seasonal Factor Drift §2](Seasonal_Factor_Drift.md#2-how-bls-derives-its-seasonal-factors)), so a projected maturity factor is scaled toward 1.0 ([Horizon-Dependent Maturity Factor](#horizon-dependent-maturity-factor)).

`YieldCurves/scripts/updateSaSaoYields.js` calls the same two functions when it produces [SA and SAO yields (S10)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s10), so the published SA Yield and the figure the app calculates in the browser are one number. Until 2026-09-13 the script used its own same-month/day lookup, which omitted the [Credibility Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#credibility-factor), and the published SA Yield was below the app’s: 1.056% against 1.102% for the April 2027 TIPS. The anniversary identity is therefore approximate rather than exact here: a whole number of years to maturity leaves a small adjustment rather than none.

### The SA Yield

The **[SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield)** is the ordinary yield-to-maturity of `SACP` (Actual/Actual day count, semi-annual compounding). Seasonally adjusted breakeven inflation is the nominal yield minus the SA Yield.

### Getting the two SA Factors

For each TIPS:

- Look up the Settlement Date SA Factor (`shared/src/ref-cpi.js#saFactorForDate`): a known recent date, so this is that date's exact daily value.
- Look up the [Maturity SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-sa-factor) (`shared/src/ref-cpi.js#maturitySaFactor`):
  - If the Maturity Date is inside the published series: that date's exact value.
  - Otherwise — true of nearly every outstanding TIPS, since its maturity lies beyond the published series — take the value for the same calendar month and day from the most recent completed cycle, and scale it by the [Credibility Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#credibility-factor) (see [Horizon-Dependent Maturity Factor](#horizon-dependent-maturity-factor), below).

## Horizon-Dependent Maturity Factor

Because the maturity SA Factor of nearly every outstanding TIPS is the current seasonal pattern projected onto a future date, and that projection decays with horizon ([Seasonal Factor Drift §2](Seasonal_Factor_Drift.md#2-how-bls-derives-its-seasonal-factors)), the seasonal adjustment scales the projected departure from 1.0 by the [Credibility Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#credibility-factor):

\[ S_{Maturity}^{eff} = 1 + (S_{Maturity} - 1)\, w(h) \]

- \( h \) — years from settlement to the maturity date.
- \( w(h) = A^2 / (A^2 + \sigma_{drift}(h)^2) \) — the signal-to-noise ratio of the [Seasonal Amplitude](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#seasonal-amplitude) \( A \) against the [Seasonal Factor Drift](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#seasonal-factor-drift) \( \sigma_{drift}(h) \) of the interpolated factor for that maturity's calendar month, both measured from CPI history in [Seasonal Factor Drift §3–§5](Seasonal_Factor_Drift.md), not fitted. \( \sigma_{drift} \) is per calendar month because the maturity months differ (December and the spring months drift fastest, [Seasonal Factor Drift §4](Seasonal_Factor_Drift.md)), and the long end is entirely February 15.
- The weight is applied at every horizon, with no floor. It is a smooth function of the measured drift, which has no discontinuity that a floor would follow. Near the front end \( w \) is already close to 1 (about 0.91 at 1 year, 0.85 at 3), so the adjustment there is nearly unchanged, and the [SAO](3.3_Adjust_For_Other_Effects.md) fit absorbs the small residual within its full-snap band. \( w \) falls further out: for February 15 it reaches about 0.45 at 30 years.

\( S_{Settle} \) is never scaled: it is a measured recent value, not a projection. A maturity date inside the published series (a near-dated TIPS) uses that exact day's factor, unscaled.

The long-end `SA − ask` adjustment, about −4 bp at the 2050s maturities under the unscaled projection, becomes about −3 bp; the front end barely moves ([Seasonal Factor Drift §7](Seasonal_Factor_Drift.md)). Implemented in `shared/src/ref-cpi.js#maturitySaFactor`.

## Data Sources
- **Prices**: Sourced from FedInvest (daily) or uploaded via Broker CSV (Schwab/Fidelity).
  - **Broker**: carries both an ask and a bid Price per security; the SA Yield uses the ask side.
  - **FedInvest**: one Price per security, the one [Select TIPS and Treasury prices (1.1.2)](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#select-tips-and-treasury-prices) selects — the Buy price when present (the average of dealer bid and ask), else the Sell price (the dealer bid alone), else the End of Day price. Buy is present for nearly every security, so a FedInvest yield is usually higher than the broker ask yield for the same security, since the average of dealer bid and ask is below a broker's ask price.
- **SA Factors**: `RefCpiNsaSa.csv` in R2, one row per calendar day holding `Ref CPI NSA`, `Ref CPI SA`, and their ratio (the SA Factor).
  - `Ref CPI SA` applies the 31 CFR Appendix B daily interpolation, the same interpolation used for the Ref CPI, to BLS's monthly seasonally adjusted CPI-U.
  - BLS publishes the seasonally adjusted CPI-U only monthly, and Appendix B officially interpolates the non-seasonally adjusted series alone. A daily seasonally adjusted Ref CPI has no official form: it is a constructed series, built for seasonal yield comparison, always calculated and never retrieved because there is nothing official to retrieve. See [DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi).
  - The interpolation is defined once in `shared/src/ref-cpi.js` and imported wherever it is needed.
