## Overview
TIPS are indexed to the Non-Seasonally Adjusted CPI (CPI-NSA), so their monthly performance follows a predictable seasonal pattern (higher inflation in spring, lower in autumn) on top of the real, idiosyncratic yield. The **[Seasonal Adjustment (SA)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor)** removes that predictable pattern, so real yields can be compared across maturity months on the same basis the market already prices them on. For the intuition behind why this works, see [Intuition: Seasonal Adjustments (SA)](./SA_Intuition.md) — this spec describes only the calculation itself.

## The calculation

The seasonal adjustment is Canty (2009) Equation 14, the working approximation the apps use ([Canty.md](Canty.md) §4):

\[ SACP \approx CP \times \frac{S_{Settle}}{S_{Maturity}} \]

where \( CP \), \( SACP \), and \( S_t \) map from Canty’s general inflation-linked-bond notation to these TIPS quantities ([Canty.md](Canty.md) §1a has the full table):

| Canty | TIPS term | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| \( CP \) | [Clean Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price) | the quoted real price, before inflation adjustment |
| \( SACP \) | [Seasonally Adjusted Clean Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sacp) | the clean price with the predictable seasonal component removed |
| \( S_t \) | [SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) on date \( t \) | \( RefCPI_{NSA}(t) / RefCPI_{SA}(t) \) |
| \( S_{Settle} / S_{Maturity} \) | [SA Price Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-price-factor) | the multiplier applied to the clean price |
| \( S_{Maturity} \) | [Maturity SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-sa-factor) | the SA Factor for the maturity date |

Read the [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields) and the [SA Factors](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) and produce the [SA Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yields). For each TIPS:

- Look up the Settlement Date SA Factor (`shared/src/ref-cpi.js#saFactorForDate`): a known recent date, so this is that date’s exact daily value.
- Look up the [Maturity SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-sa-factor) (`shared/src/ref-cpi.js#maturitySaFactor`):
  - If [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s4) holds the Maturity Date: that date’s exact value.
  - Otherwise — true of nearly every outstanding TIPS, since its maturity lies beyond the last date [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s4) holds — read the value for the same calendar month and day from the most recent completed cycle, and scale it toward 1.0 by the [Credibility Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#credibility-factor):
    - Calculate `h`: the years from the Settlement Date to the Maturity Date.
    - Calculate `w(h) = A² / (A² + σ_drift(h)²)` — the signal-to-noise ratio of the [Seasonal Amplitude](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#seasonal-amplitude) `A` against the [Seasonal Factor Drift](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#seasonal-factor-drift) `σ_drift(h)` of the interpolated factor for that maturity’s calendar month, both measured (not fitted) from CPI history in [Seasonal Factor Drift §3–§5](Seasonal_Factor_Drift.md).
    - Calculate the effective Maturity SA Factor: `1 + (Maturity SA Factor − 1) × w(h)`.
  - The Settlement Date SA Factor is a measured recent value, not a projection, so it is not scaled.
- Calculate the SA Price Factor: the Settlement Date SA Factor divided by the (possibly scaled) Maturity SA Factor.
- Calculate the SACP (Seasonally Adjusted Clean Price): the Price times the SA Price Factor. `CP` is whichever quoted price is in use; the formula does not depend on the choice ([Calculate TIPS yields (3.1.7)](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md#calculate-tips-yields) states which price each source supplies).
- Calculate the [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield): the ordinary yield-to-maturity of the SACP (Actual/Actual day count, semi-annual compounding, the same convention every other yield in the app uses). [Calculate breakeven inflation (3.5)](./3.5_Calculate_Breakeven_Inflation.md) subtracts it from a nominal yield.
- If the [SA Factors](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) could not be read, or the SA Yield cannot be calculated: the TIPS has no SA Yield, and keeps the yields of [Calculate TIPS yields (3.1.7)](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md#calculate-tips-yields).

One implementation (`shared/src/sa-yields.js#saYieldsFromTipsYields`, with the look-ups of `shared/src/ref-cpi.js`) calculates the SA Yield for the app and for `YieldCurves/scripts/updateSaSaoYields.js`, which produces [SA and SAO yields (S10)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s10), so the SA Yield in S10 and the figure the app calculates in the browser are one number.

### Why the scaling exists

Canty assumes the seasonal pattern repeats unchanged each year, which makes \( S_t \) depend only on the calendar position of \( t \) — its month and day, not the year — so that \( S_{Maturity} \) is fixed by the month and day of the maturity date alone. On that assumption, settlement and maturity falling on the same month and day (a whole number of years apart) makes the two factors equal, the SA Price Factor 1.0, and `SACP = CP`, so there is no adjustment; the factor departs from 1.0 only by the seasonal difference over the leftover partial year ([Canty.md](Canty.md) §4, the anniversary identity).

The app applies that assumption only as far as the measurement supports: the factor for a given month and day differs between years, by an amount that grows with the distance from BLS’s estimation window ([Seasonal Factor Drift §2](Seasonal_Factor_Drift.md#2-how-bls-derives-its-seasonal-factors)), which is why a projected Maturity SA Factor is scaled toward 1.0 rather than used unscaled. The weight `w(h)` is applied at every horizon, with no floor — it’s a smooth function of the measured drift, which has no discontinuity a floor would follow. Near the front end `w` is already close to 1 (about 0.91 at 1 year, 0.85 at 3), so the adjustment there is nearly unchanged, and the [SAO](3.3_Adjust_For_Other_Effects.md) fit smooths the small residual within its full-snap band; `w` falls further out, reaching about 0.45 at 30 years for February 15 (the long end’s only maturity month). The long-end `SA − ask` adjustment, about −4 bp at the 2050s maturities under the unscaled projection, becomes about −3 bp; the front end barely moves ([Seasonal Factor Drift §7](Seasonal_Factor_Drift.md)).

The anniversary identity above is approximate rather than exact in practice: a whole number of years to maturity leaves a small adjustment rather than none.
