# 3.6 Calculate bid and ask spreads

**Specifies:** [3.6 Calculate bid and ask spreads](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_YIELDCURVES)  
**Implemented by:** `shared/src/spreads.js#yieldSpreadBps`, `shared/src/spreads.js#priceSpreadPct`  
**Input:** [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields) and [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields), from [3.1](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md)  
**Output:** [Bid and Ask Spreads](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bid-and-ask-spreads), to [3.7](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md)

Subtracts the [ask](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) side from the [bid](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bid) side, in yield and in price, for each security quoted with both.

Only the market quotes carry both sides, so this process has one source. Securities priced from [S1](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) alone produce no spread.

- [DATA_DICTIONARY.md](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md) — [Ask / Bid](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask), [Clean Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price), [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio)

<a id="bid-ask-spreads"></a>
## Yield spread

Bid yield less ask yield, in basis points.

Both sides are calculated from price rather than read from the quote, for every security quoted with both: the TIPS in [3.1.7](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md#calculate-tips-yields), the nominal Treasuries in [3.1.8](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md#calculate-treasury-yields). The two sides therefore share one convention and the difference between them is a spread. Reading one side's quoted yield and calculating the other would put a difference of method into the result alongside the spread, and the quote states a yield on the ask side only, so the bid side is calculated in either case.

## Price spread

Ask price less bid price, as a percentage of the ask price.

**TIPS use the index-ratio adjusted prices and nominal Treasuries use the quoted prices.** The adjusted price is what is actually paid for a TIPS, so the spread is stated on the amount that changes hands rather than on the real price. For a nominal Treasury the two are the same figure, so no adjustment arises.

**Output:** the yield and price spreads per security, to [3.7](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md).
