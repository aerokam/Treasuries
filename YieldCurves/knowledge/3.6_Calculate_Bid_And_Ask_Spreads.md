# 3.6 Calculate bid and ask spreads

**Specifies:** [3.6 Calculate bid and ask spreads](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_YIELDCURVES)  
**Implemented by:** `shared/src/spreads.js#yieldSpreadBps`, `shared/src/spreads.js#priceSpreadPct`  
**Input:** [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields) and [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields), from [Parse sources and calculate yields (3.1)](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md)  
**Output:** [Bid and Ask Spreads](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bid-and-ask-spreads), to [Render charts and tables (3.7)](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md)

Only the market quotes carry both an ask and a bid side, so this process has one source. A security priced from [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) alone produces no spread.

## Dependencies

- [DATA_DICTIONARY.md](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md) — [Ask / Bid](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask), [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price), [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio)
- [Parse sources and calculate yields (3.1)](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md) — supplies the bid and ask prices
- [Render charts and tables (3.7)](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md) — draws the result

---

<a id="bid-ask-spreads"></a>
For each security quoted with both an ask and a bid [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price):

## Yield spread

- Calculate the Yield Spread: the bid [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) less the ask Yield, in basis points. Both sides are calculated from price rather than read from the quote — the TIPS in [Calculate TIPS yields (3.1.7)](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md#calculate-tips-yields), the nominal Treasuries in [Calculate Treasury yields (3.1.8)](./3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md#calculate-treasury-yields) — so the two sides share one convention and the difference between them is a spread. Reading one side's quoted yield and calculating the other would put a difference of method into the result alongside the spread, and the quote states a yield on the ask side only, so the bid side is calculated in either case.

## Price spread

- Calculate the Price Spread: the ask Price less the bid Price, as a percentage of the ask Price.
  - For a TIPS: use the [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio)-adjusted ask and bid prices — the amount that actually changes hands, so the spread is stated on that rather than on the unadjusted price.
  - For a nominal Treasury: use the quoted prices directly — the adjusted and the quoted price are the same figure, so no adjustment arises.

**Output:** the yield and price spreads per security, to [Render charts and tables (3.7)](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md).
