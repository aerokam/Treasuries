# 3.1 Parse sources and calculate yields

**Specifies:** [3.1 Parse sources and calculate yields](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_YC_LOAD) and the eight processes it decomposes into  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js`, `shared/src/fidelity-parse.js`, `shared/src/tips-yields.js`, `shared/src/treasury-yields.js`  
**Reference:** [FedInvest Pricing Logic](./FedInvest_Pricing_Logic.md), [FedInvest Treasury Pricing Methodology](./FedInvest_Treasury_Pricing_Methodology.md)

## Dependencies

- [DATA_DICTIONARY.md](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md) — [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield), [Clean Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price), [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date), [Bond Holiday](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio), [SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor), and the data flows of [§6.0](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#6.0-data-flows)
- [DataStores.md](../../knowledge/DataStores.md) — [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1), [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s4), [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7), [GSW curve parameters (S12)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s12), and the bond holidays file
- [3.2 Adjust for seasonality](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) to [3.7 Render charts and tables](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md) — the processes that receive the outputs of this one

## Scope

Reads four data stores and produces the [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields) and the [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields) that the rest of Yield Curves works from. The [SA Factors](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) and the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) also leave this process, for 3.2 and 3.7. Every process below is executed in the browser, and none of them writes a data store.

**Every yield in the app is calculated from price.** [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) carries a [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) per security, calculated from price when the file was written by ingestion process 1.1, and [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7) carries the [ask](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) yield its source quotes. The app uses neither. Each yield is calculated from price at the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of its own source: every TIPS in [3.1.7](#calculate-tips-yields), and every nominal Treasury in [3.1.8](#calculate-treasury-yields). One method across both sources, both security types and both sides of a quote is what makes the four sets comparable with each other, and a difference between two of them a difference in the market rather than in the arithmetic.

## Processes

<a id="parse-fedinvest-prices"></a>
### 3.1.1 Parse FedInvest prices

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseFedInvestPrices`

Reads [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) and produces the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) on its first line, and the [TIPS Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-prices) and the [Treasury Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices), separated by the `type` column. That split is what lets one store supply both the real curve and the nominal curve.

The settlement date is the one [1.1.1 Determine settlement date](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#determine-settlement-date) assigned to the prices, T+0.

**Output:** [TIPS Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-prices), to [3.1.7](#calculate-tips-yields); [Treasury Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices), to [3.1.8](#calculate-treasury-yields); the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), to [3.1.7](#calculate-tips-yields) and [3.1.8](#calculate-treasury-yields).

<a id="parse-market-quotes"></a>
### 3.1.2 Parse market quotes

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseMarketQuotes`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseFidelityNominals`, `shared/src/fidelity-parse.js#parseFidelityTipsRows`, `shared/src/fidelity-parse.js#parseFidelityNominalRows`, `shared/src/fidelity-parse.js#parseFidelityDownloadDate`

Reads [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7) and produces the [TIPS Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-quotes), the [Treasury Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-quotes) and the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date):

- It separates the TIPS rows from the Treasury rows by the `Product` column, and keeps the first row for each CUSIP.
- It keeps a TIPS row only for a CUSIP that [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) also has, and only when the row has an ask price. From each it takes the coupon rate, the maturity date, the [ask and bid](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) [Clean Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price), the ask and bid adjusted prices, and the [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio) the quote states, which the export calls `Inflation factor`.
- It drops a Treasury row that has no maturity date it can read, no quoted ask yield, a CUSIP that [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) has as a TIPS or a description naming it TIPS, or a CUSIP root the [Treasury CUSIP Reference](../../knowledge/Treasury_CUSIP_Reference.md) does not recognise. Each Treasury row kept is given its security type, Bill, Note, Bond or STRIPS, from its CUSIP root, in the type vocabulary of [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1). From each it takes the coupon rate, the maturity date and the ask and bid clean prices.
- It reads the Download Date from the trailing line of the file.

The column names are the export's own rather than the Data Dictionary's. They are recorded as synonyms in the [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7) entry, which gives the defined term each one carries.

[Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7) states its [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) on a trailing line rather than in a column. The date is read here and passed to [3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date), and to [3.7](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md), which shows it against the market-quote views.

**STRIPS are Treasury rows.** [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7) separates only Treasury from TIPS in its `Product` column, so the zero-coupon STRIPS arrive among the nominal Treasuries and are told apart by their CUSIP root.

**Output:** [TIPS Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-quotes), to [3.1.7](#calculate-tips-yields); [Treasury Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-quotes), to [3.1.8](#calculate-treasury-yields); the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date), to [3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date) and to [3.7](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md).

<a id="parse-ref-cpi-and-sa-factors"></a>
### 3.1.3 Parse Ref CPI and SA factors

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseRefCpiAndSaFactors`, `shared/src/csv.js#parseCsv`

Reads [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s4), giving one [Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) and one [SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) per calendar day. [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s4) carries a row for every calendar day it covers. Finding the factor for a given date, including a maturity date beyond the last row, is part of [3.2 Adjust for seasonality](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md).

**Output:** the [SA Factors](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor), to [3.2](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md).

<a id="parse-bond-holidays"></a>
### 3.1.4 Parse bond holidays

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseBondHolidays`, `shared/src/settlement.js#parseHolidaySet`

Reads the bond holidays file and produces the [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday). [3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date) is the only process that uses them, and the only reason this store is read.

**Output:** the [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), to [3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date).

<a id="parse-gsw-parameters"></a>
### 3.1.5 Parse GSW parameters

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseGswParameters`

Reads [GSW curve parameters (S12)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s12), the Federal Reserve’s published curve parameters and the date they were fitted for, and produces nothing when the store cannot be read.

The parameters are drawn only on an analysis view opened with `?gsw`. No view of the app uses them otherwise, so this process is specified but not drawn on the diagram.

**Output:** the [GSW Curve Parameters](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#gsw-curve-parameters), to the `?gsw` analysis view only.

<a id="determine-settlement-date"></a>
### 3.1.6 Determine settlement date

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#marketSettleIso`, `shared/src/settlement.js#nextBusinessDay`

Produces the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) the market quotes are stated at: the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) from [3.1.2](#parse-market-quotes), advanced by one bond trading day past any [Bond Holiday](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday) from [3.1.4](#parse-bond-holidays) (T+1).

A security from [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) has its own settlement date, read in [3.1.1](#parse-fedinvest-prices). The two sources are downloaded on separate schedules, so neither file's date says anything about the other's.

**Output:** the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), to [3.1.7](#calculate-tips-yields) and [3.1.8](#calculate-treasury-yields).

<a id="calculate-tips-yields"></a>
### 3.1.7 Calculate TIPS yields

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/tips-yields.js#tipsYieldsFromPrices`

Calculates the yield of each TIPS from its [Clean Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price) at the settlement date of its source: from the [TIPS Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-prices) at the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) from [3.1.1](#parse-fedinvest-prices), and from the [TIPS Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-quotes) at the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) from [3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date). A quoted TIPS has two yields, from its [ask](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) price and from its [bid](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) price, calculated for the reasons [3.1.8](#calculate-treasury-yields) gives for the nominal Treasuries in the same file. Its [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio) is the one the quote states. A TIPS from [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) has none.

The [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) of [3.2](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) is calculated in the same function, so a TIPS whose [SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) is unavailable for either of its dates is dropped here. A quoted TIPS is also dropped when it has no ask price, and any TIPS when its ask yield cannot be calculated.

One implementation serves the app and the acquisition job that writes [Yield curves (S13)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s13), [Breakeven inflation (S14)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s14) and [Bid and ask spreads (S15)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s15), so the published figures and the ones the app shows are one calculation.

**This process is called once per render, not once per load.** It is called once for each source shown, each time a view is rendered.

**Output:** the [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields), to [3.2](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) and [3.6](./3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md).

<a id="calculate-treasury-yields"></a>
### 3.1.8 Calculate Treasury yields

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/treasury-yields.js#treasuryYieldsFromPrices`, `shared/src/fidelity-parse.js#parseFidelityNominalRows`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseFidelityNominals`

Calculates the yield of each nominal Treasury from its [Clean Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price): from the [Treasury Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices) at the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) from [3.1.1](#parse-fedinvest-prices), once when the page loads; and from the [Treasury Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-quotes) at the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) from [3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date), both the [ask](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) and the [bid](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) yield. For the quotes the calculation is made in the same pass as the parse of [3.1.2](#parse-market-quotes).

Three properties of a quote decide calculating its yields rather than reading the yield it states:

- The quoted price states more decimal places than the quoted yield.
- A yield is quoted on the ask side only, so the bid side is calculated in either case.
- Calculating both sides puts them on one convention, which is what makes their difference the spread of [3.6](./3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md).

The quoted ask yield is still read, as a presence test: a row without one has no live offer. A row is dropped when its ask yield cannot be calculated, for want of an ask price or for a maturity date on or before the settlement date. A row with no quoted bid price keeps its ask yield and has no bid yield.

A STRIP is calculated like every other row. At a coupon of zero the yield calculation builds no coupon cash flows, so a STRIP has the semi-annual bond-equivalent yield of its own price, and one maturing inside half a year has the yield of Treasury's bill investment-rate formula. The processes that fit a coupon-bond curve leave STRIPS out of the fitting set.

One implementation of each calculation serves the app and the acquisition job that writes [Yield curves (S13)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s13) and [Bid and ask spreads (S15)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s15). What the app supplies is its own gating and the [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) type vocabulary the rest of it filters on.

**Output:** the [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields), to [3.4](./3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md), [3.5](./3.5_Calculate_Breakeven_Inflation.md), [3.6](./3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md) and [3.7](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md).
