# 3.1 Parse sources and calculate yields

**Specifies:** [3.1 Parse sources and calculate yields](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_YC_LOAD) and the eight processes it decomposes into  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js`, `shared/src/fidelity-parse.js`, `shared/src/tips-yields.js`, `shared/src/treasury-yields.js`  
**Reference:** [FedInvest Pricing Logic](./FedInvest_Pricing_Logic.md), [FedInvest Treasury Pricing Methodology](./FedInvest_Treasury_Pricing_Methodology.md)

## Dependencies

- [DATA_DICTIONARY.md](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md) — [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield), [Clean Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price), [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date), [Bond Holiday](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio), [SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor), and the data flows of [§6.0](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#6.0-data-flows)
- [DataStores.md](../../knowledge/DataStores.md) — [S1](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1), [S4](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s4), [S7](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7), [S12](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s12), and the bond holidays file
- [3.2 Adjust for seasonality](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) to [3.7 Render charts and tables](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md) — the processes that receive the outputs of this one

## Scope

Reads five data stores and produces the [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields) and the [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields) that the rest of Yield Curves works from. The [SA Factors](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor), the [GSW Curve Parameters](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#gsw-curve-parameters) and the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) also leave this process, for 3.2, 3.4 and 3.7. Every process below is executed in the browser, and none of them writes a data store.

**Every yield in the app is calculated from price.** [S1](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) carries a [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) per security, calculated from price when the file was written by ingestion process 1.1, and [S7](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7) carries the [ask](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) yield its source quotes. The app uses neither. Each yield is calculated from price at the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of its own source: every TIPS in [3.1.7](#calculate-tips-yields), and every nominal Treasury in [3.1.8](#calculate-treasury-yields). One method across both sources, both security types and both sides of a quote is what makes the four sets comparable with each other, and a difference between two of them a difference in the market rather than in the arithmetic.

## Processes

<a id="parse-fedinvest-prices"></a>
### 3.1.1 Parse FedInvest prices

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseFedInvestPrices`

Reads [S1](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) and splits it into the TIPS and the nominal Treasuries, by its `Type` column. That split is what lets one store supply both the real curve and the nominal curve.

[S1](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) states one [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) for the whole file rather than one per security, so that date is read once and attached to every security taken from it. Yields calculated at that date, rather than at a date offset from it, are the ones that match the source's own published yields, so it is taken as the settlement date directly. This is an empirical finding about the source, not a convention applied to it.

**Output:** [TIPS Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-prices), to [3.1.7](#calculate-tips-yields); [Treasury Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices), to [3.1.8](#calculate-treasury-yields).

<a id="parse-market-quotes"></a>
### 3.1.2 Parse market quotes

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseMarketQuotes`, `shared/src/fidelity-parse.js#parseFidelityTipsRows`, `shared/src/fidelity-parse.js#parseFidelityDownloadDate`

Reads [S7](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7) and separates its Treasury rows from its TIPS rows, by its `Product` column, taking the [bid](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) and [ask](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) [Clean Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price) for each.

The column names are the export's own rather than the Data Dictionary's. They are recorded as synonyms in the [S7](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7) entry, which gives the defined term each one carries.

[S7](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7) states its [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) on a trailing line rather than in a column. The date is read here and passed to [3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date), and to [3.7](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md), which shows it against the market-quote views.

**STRIPS are Treasury rows.** [S7](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7) separates only Treasury from TIPS in its `Product` column, so the zero-coupon STRIPS arrive among the nominal Treasuries and are told apart by their CUSIP root.

**Output:** [TIPS Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-quotes), to [3.1.7](#calculate-tips-yields); [Treasury Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-quotes), to [3.1.8](#calculate-treasury-yields); the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date), to [3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date) and to [3.7](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md).

<a id="parse-ref-cpi-and-sa-factors"></a>
### 3.1.3 Parse Ref CPI and SA factors

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseRefCpiAndSaFactors`, `shared/src/csv.js#parseCsv`

Reads [S4](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s4), giving one [Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) and one [SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) per calendar day. [S4](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s4) carries a row for every calendar day it covers. Finding the factor for a given date, including a maturity date beyond the last row, is part of [3.2 Adjust for seasonality](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md).

**Output:** the [SA Factors](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor), to [3.2](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md).

<a id="parse-bond-holidays"></a>
### 3.1.4 Parse bond holidays

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseBondHolidays`, `shared/src/settlement.js#parseHolidaySet`

Reads the bond holidays file and produces the [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday). [3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date) is the only process that uses them, and the only reason this store is read.

**Output:** the [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), to [3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date).

<a id="parse-gsw-parameters"></a>
### 3.1.5 Parse GSW parameters

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseGswParameters`

Reads [S12](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s12), which holds the Federal Reserve's own published curve parameters and the date they were fitted for.

Nothing is evaluated here. [3.4 Fit spot yield curves](./3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md) evaluates them, to draw the published curve against the app's own fit.

**Output:** the [GSW Curve Parameters](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#gsw-curve-parameters), to [3.4](./3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md).

<a id="determine-settlement-date"></a>
### 3.1.6 Determine the settlement date

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#marketSettleIso`, `shared/src/settlement.js#nextBusinessDay`

Produces the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) the market quotes are stated at: the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) from [3.1.2](#parse-market-quotes), advanced by one bond trading day past any [Bond Holiday](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday) from [3.1.4](#parse-bond-holidays) (T+1).

A security from [S1](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) has its own settlement date, read in [3.1.1](#parse-fedinvest-prices). The two sources are downloaded on separate schedules, so neither file's date says anything about the other's.

**Output:** the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), to [3.1.7](#calculate-tips-yields) and [3.1.8](#calculate-treasury-yields).

<a id="calculate-tips-yields"></a>
### 3.1.7 Calculate the TIPS yields

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/tips-yields.js#tipsYieldsFromPrices`

Calculates the yield of each TIPS from its [Clean Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price) at the settlement date of its source: from the [TIPS Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-prices) at the date [S1](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) states, and from the [TIPS Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-quotes) at the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) from [3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date). A quoted TIPS has two yields, from its [ask](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) price and from its [bid](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) price, calculated for the reasons [3.1.8](#calculate-treasury-yields) gives for the nominal Treasuries in the same file. Its [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio) is the one the quote states. A TIPS from [S1](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) has none.

The [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) of [3.2](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) is calculated in the same function, so a TIPS whose [SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) is unavailable for either of its dates is dropped here. A quoted TIPS is also dropped when it has no ask price, and any TIPS when its ask yield cannot be calculated.

One implementation serves the app and the acquisition job that writes [S13](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s13), [S14](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s14) and [S15](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s15), so the published figures and the ones the app shows are one calculation.

**This process is called once per render, not once per load.** It is called once for each source shown, each time a view is rendered.

**Output:** the [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields), to [3.2](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) and [3.6](./3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md).

<a id="calculate-treasury-yields"></a>
### 3.1.8 Calculate the Treasury yields

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/treasury-yields.js#treasuryYieldsFromPrices`, `shared/src/fidelity-parse.js#parseFidelityNominalRows`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseFidelityNominals`

Calculates the yield of each nominal Treasury from its [Clean Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price): from the [Treasury Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices) at the date [S1](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) states, once when the page loads; and from the [Treasury Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-quotes) at the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) from [3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date), both the [ask](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) and the [bid](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) yield. For the quotes the calculation is made in the same pass as the parse of [3.1.2](#parse-market-quotes).

Three properties of a quote decide calculating its yields rather than reading the yield it states:

- The quoted price states more decimal places than the quoted yield.
- A yield is quoted on the ask side only, so the bid side is calculated in either case.
- Calculating both sides puts them on one convention, which is what makes their difference the spread of [3.6](./3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md).

The quoted ask yield is still read, as a presence test: a row without one has no live offer. A row is dropped when its ask yield cannot be calculated, for want of an ask price or for a maturity date on or before the settlement date. A row with no quoted bid price keeps its ask yield and has no bid yield.

A STRIP is calculated like every other row. At a coupon of zero the yield calculation builds no coupon cash flows, so a STRIP has the semi-annual bond-equivalent yield of its own price, and one maturing inside half a year has the yield of Treasury's bill investment-rate formula. The processes that fit a coupon-bond curve leave STRIPS out of the fitting set.

One implementation of each calculation serves the app and the acquisition job that writes [S13](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s13) and [S15](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s15). What the app supplies is its own gating and the [S1](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) type vocabulary the rest of it filters on.

**Output:** the [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields), to [3.4](./3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md), [3.5](./3.5_Calculate_Breakeven_Inflation.md), [3.6](./3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md) and [3.7](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md).
