# 3.1 Parse sources and calculate yields

**Specifies:** [3.1 Parse sources and calculate yields](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_YC_LOAD) and the eight processes it decomposes into  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js`, `shared/src/fidelity-parse.js`, `shared/src/tips-yields.js`, `shared/src/treasury-yields.js`  
**Reference:** [FedInvest Pricing Logic](./FedInvest_Pricing_Logic.md), [FedInvest Treasury Pricing Methodology](./FedInvest_Treasury_Pricing_Methodology.md)

## Dependencies

- [DATA_DICTIONARY.md](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md) — [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield), [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price), [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date), [Bond Holiday](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio), [SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor), and the data flows of [§6.0](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#6.0-data-flows)
- [DataStores.md](../../knowledge/DataStores.md) — [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1), [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s4), [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7), [GSW curve parameters (S12)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s12), and the bond holidays file
- [3.2 Adjust for seasonality](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) to [3.7 Render charts and tables](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md) — the processes that receive the outputs of this one

---

## Scope

Reads four data stores and produces the [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields) and the [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields) that the rest of Yield Curves works from. The [SA Factors](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) and the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) also leave this process, for 3.2 and 3.7. Every process below is executed in the browser, and none of them writes a data store.

**Every yield in the app is calculated from price.** [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) carries a [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) per security, calculated from price when the file was written by ingestion process 1.1, and [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7) carries the [ask](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) yield its source quotes. The app uses neither. Each yield is calculated from price at the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of its own source: every TIPS in [3.1.7](#calculate-tips-yields), and every nominal Treasury in [3.1.8](#calculate-treasury-yields). One method across both sources, both security types and both sides of a quote is what makes the four sets comparable with each other, and a difference between two of them a difference in the market rather than in the arithmetic.

---

## Processes

<a id="parse-fedinvest-prices"></a>
### 3.1.1 Parse FedInvest prices

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseFedInvestPrices`

Reads [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) and produces the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) on its first line, and the [TIPS Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-prices) and the [Treasury Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices), separated by the `type` column. That split is what lets one store supply both the real curve and the nominal curve.

The settlement date is the one [1.1.1 Determine settlement date](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#determine-settlement-date) assigned to the prices, T+0.

**Output:** [TIPS Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-prices), to [3.1.7](#calculate-tips-yields); [Treasury Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices), to [3.1.8](#calculate-treasury-yields); the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), to [3.1.7](#calculate-tips-yields) and [3.1.8](#calculate-treasury-yields).

<a id="parse-market-quotes"></a>
### 3.1.2 Parse market quotes

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseMarketQuotes`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseFidelityNominals`, `shared/src/fidelity-parse.js#parseFidelityTipsRows`, `shared/src/fidelity-parse.js#parseFidelityNominalRows`, `shared/src/fidelity-parse.js#parseFidelityDownloadDate`

Reads [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7) and produces the [TIPS Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-quotes), the [Treasury Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-quotes) and the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date):

- It separates the TIPS rows from the Treasury rows by the `Product` column, and keeps the first row for each CUSIP.
- It keeps every TIPS row that has an ask price. From each it takes the coupon rate, the maturity date, the [ask and bid](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) and the ask and bid adjusted prices. The quote's own `Inflation factor` column is parsed but not carried into the [TIPS Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-quotes) flow — `shared/src/ref-cpi.js#tipsIndexRatios` calculates the [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio) instead of reading it.
- It drops a Treasury row that has no maturity date it can read or no ask price. Each Treasury row kept is given its security type, Bill, Note, Bond or STRIPS, from its CUSIP root, or Unclassified when the [Treasury CUSIP Reference](../../knowledge/Treasury_CUSIP_Reference.md) does not recognise the root. From each it takes the coupon rate, the maturity date and the ask and bid prices. Nothing here reads [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1): every check is answered from Market quotes (S7) alone, so a CUSIP FedInvest does not carry is still kept. TIPS and Treasury rows are told apart by the `Product` column alone — a row's description is never checked for the word "TIPS", since that word appears in some TIPS descriptions and not others and would be an unreliable signal even where `Product` were absent.
- It reads the Download Date from the trailing line of the file.

The column names are the export's own rather than the Data Dictionary's. They are recorded as synonyms in the [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7) entry, which gives the defined term each one carries.

[Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7) states its [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) on a trailing line rather than in a column. The date is read here and passed to [3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date), and to [3.7](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md), which shows it against the market-quote views.

**STRIPS are Treasury rows.** [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7) separates only Treasury from TIPS in its `Product` column, so the zero-coupon STRIPS arrive among the nominal Treasuries and are told apart by their CUSIP root.

**Output:** [TIPS Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-quotes), to [3.1.7](#calculate-tips-yields); [Treasury Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-quotes), to [3.1.8](#calculate-treasury-yields); the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date), to [3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date) and to [3.7](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md).

<a id="parse-ref-cpi-and-sa-factors"></a>
### 3.1.3 Parse Ref CPI and SA factors

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseRefCpiAndSaFactors`, `shared/src/csv.js#parseCsv`

Reads [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s4), giving one [Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) and one [SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) per calendar day. [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s4) carries a row for every calendar day it covers. Finding the factor for a given date, including a maturity date beyond the last row, is part of [3.2 Adjust for seasonality](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md).

**Output:** the [SA Factors](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor), to [3.2](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md).

<a id="parse-bond-holidays"></a>
### 3.1.4 Parse bond holidays

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseBondHolidays`, `shared/src/settlement.js#parseHolidaySet`

Reads the bond holidays file and produces the [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday). [3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date) is the only process that uses them, and the only reason this store is read.

**Output:** the [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), to [3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date).

<a id="parse-gsw-parameters"></a>
### 3.1.5 Parse GSW parameters

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseGswParameters`

Reads [GSW curve parameters (S12)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s12), the Federal Reserve’s published curve parameters and the date they were fitted for, and produces nothing when the store cannot be read.

The parameters are drawn only on an analysis view opened with `?gsw`. No view of the app uses them otherwise, so this process is specified but not drawn on the diagram.

**Output:** the [GSW Curve Parameters](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#gsw-curve-parameters), to the `?gsw` analysis view only.

<a id="determine-settlement-date"></a>
### 3.1.6 Determine settlement date

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#marketSettleIso`, `shared/src/settlement.js#nextBusinessDay`

Produces the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) the market quotes are stated at: the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) from [3.1.2](#parse-market-quotes), advanced by one bond trading day past any [Bond Holiday](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday) from [3.1.4](#parse-bond-holidays) (T+1).

A security from [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) has its own settlement date, read in [3.1.1](#parse-fedinvest-prices). The two sources are downloaded on separate schedules, so neither file's date says anything about the other's.

**Output:** the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), to [3.1.7](#calculate-tips-yields) and [3.1.8](#calculate-treasury-yields).

<a id="calculate-tips-yields"></a>
### 3.1.7 Calculate TIPS yields

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/tips-yields.js#tipsYieldsFromPrices`, `shared/src/bond-math.js#yieldFromPrice`

For each TIPS, from either source — one TIPS per [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7) row that Parse market quotes ([3.1.2](#parse-market-quotes)) kept, or one TIPS per [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) row:

- Take its [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) and [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date).
  - For a market quote: the ask price, and the Settlement Date that Determine settlement date ([3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date)) produces.
  - For FedInvest: the row's own price, and the Settlement Date that Parse FedInvest prices ([3.1.1](#parse-fedinvest-prices)) reads from the file.
- Look up the [SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) at the Settlement Date and at the [Maturity Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date).
  - If either is unavailable: drop the TIPS.
- Calculate an [Ask Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) from the Price:
  - If there's no usable Price, or the Settlement Date is on or after the Maturity Date: there is no yield.
  - Otherwise: find the yield that makes the coupon and principal payments, discounted twice a year, worth exactly the Price plus accrued interest.
- Calculate an [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield): the same calculation, from the Price adjusted by the ratio of the two SA Factors. This is not a second yield calculation — it is the same one, given a different price.
  - If the Ask Yield or the SA Yield can't be calculated: drop the TIPS.
- If this is a market quote:
  - Calculate a Bid Yield from the bid Price, the same way as the Ask Yield.
  - Calculate the Yield Spread and Price Spread between ask and bid.
  - If there is no bid Price: skip these two — the TIPS keeps its Ask Yield, with no Bid Yield and no bid-ask spread (Calculate bid and ask spreads, [3.6](./3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md)).

**This process does not calculate Index Ratio.** [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio) has no dependency on Price or on either yield, so it is not part of this calculation — `shared/src/ref-cpi.js#tipsIndexRatios` calculates it separately (Ref CPI at the Settlement Date, divided by the Ref CPI at the Dated Date, rounded once, half up, to nine decimal places), and `YieldCurves/src/app.js` attaches it onto a security's record afterward, only for the market-quote TIPS table that displays it. (This process bundled the two together until 2026-09-17; see [DFD_Worklist.md §3.0](../../knowledge/DFD_Worklist.md).)

One implementation runs the yield calculation above for the app and for the acquisition job that writes [Yield curves (S13)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s13), [Breakeven inflation (S14)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s14) and [Bid and ask spreads (S15)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s15), so the published figures and the ones the app shows are one calculation. The app runs this process once per render, not once per load — once for each source shown, each time a view is rendered.

**Output:** the [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields), to Adjust for seasonality ([3.2](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md)), Calculate bid and ask spreads ([3.6](./3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md)) and Render charts and tables ([3.7](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md)). Index Ratio reaches [3.7](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md) separately, from `ref-cpi.js#tipsIndexRatios`, not from this process.

<a id="calculate-treasury-yields"></a>
### 3.1.8 Calculate Treasury yields

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/fidelity-parse.js#parseFidelityNominalRows` (market quotes), `shared/src/treasury-yields.js#treasuryYieldsFromPrices` (FedInvest), `shared/src/bond-math.js#yieldFromPrice`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseFidelityNominals`

For each Treasury, from either source, calculate its yield from its [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) at the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of its source:

- If there's no usable Price, or the Settlement Date is on or after the [Maturity Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date): there is no yield.
- If the coupon is zero and less than six months remain to maturity: calculate the yield with Treasury's bill investment-rate formula instead.
  - This rule looks only at the coupon rate and the time remaining — it cannot tell a Treasury bill from a zero-coupon STRIPS maturing in the same window; both get the bill formula (see Common, below).
- Otherwise: find the yield that makes the security's coupon and principal payments, discounted twice a year, worth exactly the Price plus accrued interest.

For a market quote, one [Treasury Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-quotes) row per [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s7) row, at the Settlement Date from Determine settlement date ([3.1.6](#determine-settlement-date)), calculated in the same pass as the parse of Parse market quotes ([3.1.2](#parse-market-quotes)):

- Calculate the Ask Yield from the ask price, as above.
  - If it can't be calculated: drop the row.
- Calculate the Bid Yield from the bid price the same way.
  - If there is no bid price, or the Bid Yield can't be calculated: keep the row with its Ask Yield only, and no bid-ask spread (Calculate bid and ask spreads, [3.6](./3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md)).
- Ignore Fidelity's own stated yields for the row — an explicit ask yield (Ask Yield to Maturity) and, unlabeled, a bid yield (the "Yield" column). The yield calculated from the quoted price is more accurate, so both sides are always calculated from price.
- Calculate the Yield Spread and Price Spread between ask and bid separately, after this calculation, wherever this process's caller renders or publishes the result.

For FedInvest, one [Treasury Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices) row per [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) row, at the Settlement Date from Parse FedInvest prices ([3.1.1](#parse-fedinvest-prices)), calculated once when the page loads:

- Calculate the yield from the price that Select TIPS and Treasury prices ([1.1.2](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#select-tips-and-treasury-prices)) selects, as above — one value, not a true ask or a true bid.
  - If it can't be calculated: drop the row.
- Drop the row also when it has no Price, or its Maturity Date is on or before its Settlement Date.

**Common** to both sources:

- A STRIP is calculated like every other row. At a coupon of zero, no coupon cash flows are built, so a STRIP maturing six months or more out gets the semi-annual bond-equivalent yield of its own price.
- **A STRIP maturing in under six months gets the bill formula instead** (see the bill-formula rule above) — the calculation has no way to tell it apart from an actual Treasury bill in that window (not yet resolved, [DFD_Worklist.md §3.0](../../knowledge/DFD_Worklist.md)).
- The processes that fit a coupon-bond curve leave STRIPS out of the fitting set regardless.
- One implementation of each calculation serves the app and the acquisition job that writes [Yield curves (S13)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s13) and [Bid and ask spreads (S15)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s15). What the app supplies is its own gating and the [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s1) type vocabulary the rest of it filters on.

**Output:** the [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields), to Fit spot yield curves ([3.4](./3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md)), Calculate breakeven inflation ([3.5](./3.5_Calculate_Breakeven_Inflation.md)), Calculate bid and ask spreads ([3.6](./3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md)) and Render charts and tables ([3.7](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md)).
