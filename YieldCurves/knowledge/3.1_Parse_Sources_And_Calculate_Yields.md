# 3.1 Parse sources and calculate yields

**Specifies:** [3.1 Parse sources and calculate yields](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_YC_LOAD) and the eight processes it decomposes into  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js`, `shared/src/market-quotes.js`, `shared/src/tips-yields.js`, `shared/src/treasury-yields.js`  
**Reference:** [FedInvest Treasury Pricing Methodology](./FedInvest_Treasury_Pricing_Methodology.md)

Read [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1), [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7), [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s4) and [Bond holidays (S16)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s16), and produce the [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields) and the [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields) that the rest of Yield Curves works from. The [SA Factors](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) are produced with them, for [Adjust for seasonality (3.2)](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md), and the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date), for [Render charts and tables (3.7)](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md):

- Every process below executes in the browser and writes no data store.

**Every yield in the app is calculated from price.** [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1) states a [Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) per security, calculated from price by [Download FedInvest prices (1.1)](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md), and [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7) states prices only. The app does not read the yield of S1. It calculates each yield from price at the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of that source: every TIPS in [Calculate TIPS yields (3.1.7)](#calculate-tips-yields), and every nominal Treasury in [Calculate Treasury yields (3.1.8)](#calculate-treasury-yields). One method across both sources, both security types and both sides of a quote makes the four sets comparable with each other, so a difference between two of them is a difference in the market and not in the arithmetic.

---

## Processes

<a id="parse-fedinvest-prices"></a>
### 3.1.1 Parse FedInvest prices

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseFedInvestPrices`

Read [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1) and produce the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), the [TIPS Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-prices) and the [Treasury Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices):

- Read the Settlement Date from the first line of the store.
  - It is the one [Determine settlement date (1.1.1)](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#determine-settlement-date) assigned to the prices, T+0.
- Separate the TIPS Prices from the Treasury Prices by the `type` column.

**Output:** [TIPS Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-prices), to [Calculate TIPS yields (3.1.7)](#calculate-tips-yields); [Treasury Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices), to [Calculate Treasury yields (3.1.8)](#calculate-treasury-yields); the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), to [Calculate TIPS yields (3.1.7)](#calculate-tips-yields) and [Calculate Treasury yields (3.1.8)](#calculate-treasury-yields).

<a id="parse-market-quotes"></a>
### 3.1.2 Parse market quotes

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseMarketQuotes`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseMarketNominals`, `shared/src/market-quotes.js#parseTipsQuotes`, `shared/src/market-quotes.js#parseTreasuryQuotes`, `shared/src/market-quotes.js#parseDownloadDate`

Read [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7) and produce the [TIPS Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-quotes), the [Treasury Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-quotes) and the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date):

- Separate the TIPS rows from the Treasury rows by the `type` column, and keep the first row for each CUSIP.
- For each TIPS row that has an ask price:
  - Read the coupon rate, the maturity date, the [ask and bid](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) and the [Adjusted Ask Price and Adjusted Bid Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#adjusted-price).
- For each Treasury row:
  - Drop it when it has no maturity date that can be read.
  - Set its security type, Bill, Note, Bond or STRIPS, from its CUSIP root, or Unclassified when the [Treasury CUSIP Reference](../../knowledge/Treasury_CUSIP_Reference.md) does not recognise the root. The `type` column does not separate STRIPS from the other Treasuries, so the CUSIP root does.
  - Read the coupon rate, the maturity date and the ask and bid prices.
- Read the Download Date from the first line of the file.

**Output:** [TIPS Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-quotes), to [Calculate TIPS yields (3.1.7)](#calculate-tips-yields); [Treasury Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-quotes), to [Calculate Treasury yields (3.1.8)](#calculate-treasury-yields); the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date), to [Determine settlement date (3.1.6)](#determine-settlement-date) and to [Render charts and tables (3.7)](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md), which shows it against the market-quote views.

<a id="parse-ref-cpi-and-sa-factors"></a>
### 3.1.3 Parse Ref CPI and SA factors

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseRefCpiAndSaFactors`, `shared/src/csv.js#parseCsv`

Read [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s4) and produce the [SA Factors](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor):

- Produce one [SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) for each calendar day the store covers.
- The look-up of the factor for a date, including a maturity date beyond the last row, is part of [Adjust for seasonality (3.2)](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md).

**Output:** the [SA Factors](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor), to [Adjust for seasonality (3.2)](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md).

<a id="parse-bond-holidays"></a>
### 3.1.4 Parse bond holidays

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseBondHolidays`, `shared/src/settlement.js#parseHolidaySet`

Read [Bond holidays (S16)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s16) and produce the [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday). [Determine settlement date (3.1.6)](#determine-settlement-date) is the only process that uses them.

**Output:** the [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), to [Determine settlement date (3.1.6)](#determine-settlement-date).

<a id="parse-gsw-parameters"></a>
### 3.1.5 Parse GSW parameters

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseGswParameters`

Read [GSW curve parameters (S12)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s12), the Federal Reserve’s published curve parameters and the date they were fitted for, and produce the [GSW Curve Parameters](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#gsw-curve-parameters):

- Produce nothing when the store cannot be read.

Only an analysis view opened with `?gsw` draws the parameters, so this process is specified and not drawn on the diagram.

**Output:** the [GSW Curve Parameters](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#gsw-curve-parameters), to the `?gsw` analysis view only.

<a id="determine-settlement-date"></a>
### 3.1.6 Determine settlement date

**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#marketSettleIso`, `shared/src/settlement.js#nextBusinessDay`

Read the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) from [Parse market quotes (3.1.2)](#parse-market-quotes) and the [Bond Holidays](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday) from [Parse bond holidays (3.1.4)](#parse-bond-holidays), and produce the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) the market quotes are stated at:

- Advance the Download Date by one bond trading day (T+1), past any Bond Holiday.
- The Settlement Date of a security from [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1) is the one read in [Parse FedInvest prices (3.1.1)](#parse-fedinvest-prices). The two sources are downloaded on separate schedules, so the two Settlement Dates can differ.

**Output:** the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), to [Calculate TIPS yields (3.1.7)](#calculate-tips-yields) and [Calculate Treasury yields (3.1.8)](#calculate-treasury-yields).

<a id="calculate-tips-yields"></a>
### 3.1.7 Calculate TIPS yields

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/tips-yields.js#tipsYieldsFromPrices`, `shared/src/bond-math.js#yieldFromPrice`

Read the [TIPS Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-prices) or the [TIPS Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-quotes) with their [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) and produce the [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields). For each TIPS, from either source — one TIPS per [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7) row that [Parse market quotes (3.1.2)](#parse-market-quotes) kept, or one TIPS per [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1) row:

- Read its [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) and [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date).
  - For a market quote: the ask price, and the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) that [Determine settlement date (3.1.6)](#determine-settlement-date) produces.
  - For FedInvest: the price that [Select prices and add TIPS reference data (1.1.2)](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#select-prices-and-add-tips-reference-data) selects from the three FedInvest publishes (Buy, Sell, End of Day), and the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) that [Parse FedInvest prices (3.1.1)](#parse-fedinvest-prices) reads from the store.
- Calculate an [Ask Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) from the [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price):
  - If there is no usable [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price), or the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) is on or after the [Maturity Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date): there is no yield, and the TIPS is dropped.
  - Otherwise: find the yield that makes the coupon and principal payments, discounted twice a year, worth exactly the [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) plus accrued interest.
- If this is a market quote and has a bid [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price): calculate a [Bid Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) from it, the same way as the [Ask Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield).
  - If there is no bid [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price): the TIPS keeps its [Ask Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield), with no [Bid Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield).

The same implementation calculates these yields for the app and for the acquisition jobs that write [SA and SAO yields (S10)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s10), [Yield curves (S13)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s13), [Breakeven inflation (S14)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s14) and [Bid and ask spreads (S15)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s15), so the figures in those stores and the ones the app shows are one calculation. The app runs this process once per render, for each source shown, and not once per load.

**Output:** the [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields), to [Adjust for seasonality (3.2)](./3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) and [Calculate bid and ask spreads (3.6)](./3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md).

<a id="calculate-treasury-yields"></a>
### 3.1.8 Calculate Treasury yields

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/market-quotes.js#parseTreasuryQuotes` (market quotes), `shared/src/treasury-yields.js#treasuryYieldsFromPrices` (FedInvest), `shared/src/bond-math.js#yieldFromPrice`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseMarketNominals`

Read the [Treasury Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices) or the [Treasury Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-quotes) with their [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) and produce the [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields). For each Treasury, from either source, calculate its yield from its [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) at the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of its source:

- If there is no usable [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price), or the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) is on or after the [Maturity Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date): there is no yield.
- If the coupon is zero and the days to maturity are fewer than half the days in the year: calculate the yield with Treasury’s bill investment-rate formula.
  - The condition looks only at the coupon rate and the days to maturity, so a zero-coupon STRIPS in the same window gets the bill formula as well.
- Otherwise: find the yield that makes the coupon and principal payments, discounted twice a year, worth exactly the [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) plus accrued interest.

For a market quote, one [Treasury Quotes](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-quotes) row per [Market quotes (S7)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7) row, at the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) from [Determine settlement date (3.1.6)](#determine-settlement-date), calculated in the same pass as [Parse market quotes (3.1.2)](#parse-market-quotes):

- Calculate the [Ask Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) from the ask price, as above.
  - If it cannot be calculated: drop the row.
- Calculate the [Bid Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) from the bid price the same way.
  - If there is no bid price, or the [Bid Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) cannot be calculated: keep the row with its [Ask Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) only, and no bid-ask spread ([Calculate bid and ask spreads (3.6)](./3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md)).

For FedInvest, one [Treasury Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices) row per [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1) row, at the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) from [Parse FedInvest prices (3.1.1)](#parse-fedinvest-prices), calculated once when the page loads:

- Calculate the yield from the price that [Select prices and add TIPS reference data (1.1.2)](../../knowledge/1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#select-prices-and-add-tips-reference-data) selects, as above: one value, not a true ask or a true bid.
  - If it cannot be calculated: drop the row.

**Common** to both sources:

- A STRIPS is calculated like every other row. At a coupon of zero, no coupon cash flows are built, so a STRIPS maturing in half a year or more gets the semi-annual bond-equivalent yield of its price.
- The processes that fit a coupon-bond curve leave STRIPS out of the fitting set.
- The same implementation calculates each yield for the app and for the acquisition job that writes [Yield curves (S13)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s13) and [Bid and ask spreads (S15)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s15).

**Output:** the [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields), to [Fit spot yield curves (3.4)](./3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md), [Calculate breakeven inflation (3.5)](./3.5_Calculate_Breakeven_Inflation.md), [Calculate bid and ask spreads (3.6)](./3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md) and [Render charts and tables (3.7)](./3.7_Render_Charts_And_Tables.md).
