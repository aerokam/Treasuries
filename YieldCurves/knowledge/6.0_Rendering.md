# 6.0 Rendering

Specifies process 7.7 of the Yield Curves data flow diagram and the seven processes it decomposes into.

This is the process spec: what the rendering stage does, and with what inputs. [3.0 Visual Standards & Interaction](./3.0_Visual_Standards.md) is the separate question of what the output must look like once drawn, and this spec links to it rather than restating it.

## Dependencies

- [3.0 Visual Standards & Interaction](./3.0_Visual_Standards.md) — the rules every view drawn here obeys
- [5.0 Load and Parse](./5.0_Load_And_Parse.md) — supplies the source dates the views label themselves with
- [1.0 Seasonal Adjustments](./1.0_Seasonal_Adjustments.md), [2.0 SAO Adjustment](./2.0_SAO_Adjustment.md), [4.0 Spot Yield Curves](./4.0_Spot_Yield_Curves.md) — supply the series drawn
- [DATA_DICTIONARY.md](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md) — [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield), [SAO Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yield), [IQR Clip](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#iqr-clip), [Yield Curve](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield-curve)

## 1.0 Scope

Everything upstream of this stage produces series. This stage decides which of them the user is currently asking for, what range of them is visible, and draws them.

It is the only process in the app that the user interacts with, so both flows to the user cross this boundary: the selections coming in, and the charts and tables going out.

Nothing here writes a data store, and nothing here calculates a yield. A view that appears to show a figure no other process produced is a defect in this stage, not a new calculation.

## 2.0 Processes

<a id="select-view"></a>
### 2.1 Select the view (7.7.1)

Takes the user's tab and mode selections and determines which view the other processes build. There are three tabs — Treasuries, TIPS and breakeven — and a spread mode that replaces the yield charts with bid and ask spread charts.

The two selections are not independent. Breakeven is market-quoted only, so selecting it leaves spread mode off, and the source controls it shows are the TIPS strip rather than a set of its own.

**Output:** the current tab and mode, to [2.2](#build-scales) and to whichever of [2.3](#draw-treasuries) to [2.6](#draw-spreads) that selection calls for.

<a id="build-scales"></a>
### 2.2 Build the axis scales (7.7.2)

Determines what part of the series is visible, before any of it is drawn.

Three things decide it, all specified in [3.0](./3.0_Visual_Standards.md): the horizontal axis is either calendar or term; the vertical axis is rescaled to fit whatever is currently shown when the selection changes; and each tab holds its own zoom, so a change of tab leaves the previous tab's zoom unchanged.

The vertical axis may also be clipped. [IQR Clip](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#iqr-clip) sets a floor derived from the data, which keeps a few securities at extreme negative yields from setting the lower bound for every other security. **It moves the axis and never removes a point** — a clipped view is still showing every security, and a figure read off the table will not agree with what the eye can see on the chart.

**Output:** the axis scales, to whichever view is being drawn.

<a id="draw-treasuries"></a>
### 2.3 Draw the Treasuries view (7.7.3)

Draws the nominal securities as a chart and a table: the quoted yields, and the fitted nominal spot curve when it is shown.

Bills, Notes and Bonds can each be hidden independently, and the axis rescales to what remains, per [2.2](#build-scales).

**Output:** the Treasuries chart and table.

<a id="draw-tips"></a>
### 2.4 Draw the TIPS view (7.7.4)

Draws the TIPS as a chart and a table: the quoted yields, the [SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield), the [SAO Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yield), the fitted spot curves, and the published curve from [S12](../../knowledge/DataStores.md#s12) drawn as a reference against the app's own fit.

Each series can be shown or hidden separately, and both sources can be drawn at once, so the same security may appear twice at different yields. That is the source comparison rather than a duplicate.

**Output:** the TIPS chart and table.

<a id="draw-breakeven"></a>
### 2.5 Draw the breakeven view (7.7.5)

Draws breakeven inflation: per security, the nominal yield less each of the ask, SA and SAO yields; and across terms, the fitted nominal curve less the fitted TIPS curve.

The per-security figures pair each TIPS with the nearest-maturity nominal, so the pairing is approximate wherever no nominal matures near that TIPS. The curve figures do not have that problem, being read at the same term on both curves.

**Output:** the breakeven chart and table.

<a id="draw-spreads"></a>
### 2.6 Draw the spread view (7.7.6)

Draws the bid and ask spread for each security, in yield and in price, for TIPS and for nominal Treasuries.

Only the market quotes carry both sides, so this view has one source. Its outlier clipping is specified separately from the yield charts' in [3.0](./3.0_Visual_Standards.md), the two distributions having different ranges.

**Output:** the spread charts and table.

<a id="answer-a-drill"></a>
### 2.7 Answer a drill request (7.7.7)

Shows, for one security the user picks, how a figure in the view above was arrived at: the seasonal adjustment for that security, the outlier adjustment applied to it, or what a column means.

It reports the intermediate values of another process rather than that process's result, which is why it is specified separately from the view the request is raised on.

**Output:** the drill popup.

## 3.0 What This Stage Must Not Do

A figure calculated in this stage would be a figure no spec accounts for and no other consumer of the same series could reproduce. Every figure drawn here is produced upstream and passed in.

The one thing this stage does decide for itself is what is visible: which series, which range, and where the axis is clipped. Those are choices about the view, not about the data, and [3.0](./3.0_Visual_Standards.md) specifies them.
