# 3.1 Read yield curve data sets

**Specifies:** [3.1 Read yield curve data sets](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_YIELDCURVES)  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/src/app.js#loadYieldCurveDataSets`, `YieldCurves/src/app.js#parseGswParameters`, `shared/src/yield-curve-stores.js#parseYieldCurvesCsv`, `shared/src/yield-curve-stores.js#parseBreakevenCsv`, `shared/src/yield-curve-stores.js#parseSpreadsCsv`  

Read [Yield curves (S13)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s13), [Breakeven inflation (S14)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s14), [Bid and ask spreads (S15)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s15), [CMT rates (S24)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s24), [R-CMT rates (S25)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s25) and [GSW curve parameters (S12)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s12), and produce the [TIPS Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields), the [Treasury Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields), the [SAO Yields](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yields), the [Spot Yield Curves](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#spot-yield-curves), the [CMT](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#cmt), the [R-CMT](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#r-cmt), the [Breakeven Inflation](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#breakeven-inflation), the [Bid and Ask Spreads](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bid-and-ask-spreads), the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of each source and the [Download Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date):

- Every value the views draw is a value [Calculate yield curve data sets (1.3)](../../knowledge/1.3_Calculate_Yield_Curve_Data_Sets.md) calculated and stored, except the [Breakeven Inflation](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#breakeven-inflation) of the CMT, which this process calculates.
- Find each column of a store by its header name, never by its position.
- Read [Yield curves (S13)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s13):
  - Read the [Trade Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#trade-date) and the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of `FedInvest` and of `Market` from the four date lines before the header. The Trade Date of `Market` is the Download Date.
  - Read each row with a `CUSIP` other than `Spot` as a security of its `Source`: a row of Type `TIPS` as a TIPS with its `Ask`, `SA`, `SAO` and its SA working values, and every other row as a nominal Treasury with its `Ask`.
  - Read each row with `CUSIP` `Spot` as a point of the spot curve of its `Type` and `Source`: `Treasury`, `TIPS` or `BEI`, with `Spot` and `Spot SA`.
- Read [CMT rates (S24)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s24) and [R-CMT rates (S25)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s25):
  - For each store, read the row with the latest `Date` as the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of its rates (T+0), and each rate of the row that is not blank.
  - Set the [Maturity Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date) of each rate to the Settlement Date plus the maturity of its column. A maturity of 1.5 months is 6 weeks.
  - Calculate the [Breakeven Inflation](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#breakeven-inflation) of the CMT at the latest `Date` that both stores hold, which is its Settlement Date: at each maturity of 5, 7, 10, 20 and 30 years where both rates are not blank, the [CMT](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#cmt) less the [R-CMT](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#r-cmt).
- Read [Breakeven inflation (S14)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s14) and [Bid and ask spreads (S15)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s15): the dates on the first two lines, then one row per security.
- Read [GSW curve parameters (S12)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s12), the Federal Reserve’s published curve parameters and the date they were fitted for, only when the analysis view is opened with `?gsw`. Produce nothing when the store cannot be read.

**Input:** [Yield curves (S13)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s13), [Breakeven inflation (S14)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s14), [Bid and ask spreads (S15)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s15), [CMT rates (S24)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s24), [R-CMT rates (S25)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s25) and [GSW curve parameters (S12)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s12).
