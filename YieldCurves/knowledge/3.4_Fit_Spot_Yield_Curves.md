## Why
Real Treasury and TIPS instruments carry different coupons, so two bonds of the same maturity have different modified duration and different yield-to-maturity. A plot of quoted yields against maturity shows that scatter, and a plot against duration does not remove it: the long end folds back on itself because low-coupon vintages have longer duration than newer high-coupon issues.

A **spot (zero-coupon) yield curve** removes the coupon effect by construction. It is the set of discount rates `z(t)` for which every bond's observed price equals the present value of its cash flows discounted at `z`. Fitted to the nominal Treasuries and to the TIPS, each point of a curve is a function of horizon `t` alone, and the difference between the two curves is a same-horizon breakeven at every `t` ([Spot BEI](#spot-bei)).

## The calculation

For each bond in the fit:

- Include the bond when it has a usable [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) and its [Term](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#term) is at least the curve's minimum: 0.25 years for the Treasuries curve, 1 year for the nominal curve of the Breakeven tab, `SAO_NOISE_YRS` (0.5 years) for the TIPS curves.
  - The Term is `shared/src/bond-math.js#termYears` from the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of the bond's source, not from the clock at run time. One measure serves the fit, the table, the chart and the [Yield curves (S13)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s13) grid, so refitting the same prices later in the day reproduces the same grid.
- Enumerate its cash flows (`cashflowSchedule`) and its accrued interest.

Then, for each source and each curve:

- If fewer than five bonds are included: produce no curve.
- Fit the curve in **price space**, not yield space. Find the zero yields `z(t)`, in the Svensson form of [The smooth curve (3.3)](./3.3_Adjust_For_Other_Effects.md#the-smooth-curve), for which the cash flows of every included bond, discounted at `z(t)`, price closest to that bond's dirty price (its [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) plus accrued interest). The squared price error of a bond is weighted by `1 / max(1, Term)`.
  - Grid-search the two decay parameters over 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 7, 10, 15, 20 and 30 years, the second above the first.
  - For each pair, start the four `β` coefficients from the ordinary-least-squares fit to the bond yields, refine them with up to eight Gauss-Newton iterations, and keep the pair with the lowest weighted sum of squared price errors.
- Calculate the [Spot Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#spot-yield) on a half-year grid of Terms, from the shortest included Term (rounded up to a half-year) to the longest, and at the longest included Term itself as the final point.
  - The curve ends at the longest included bond's exact maturity and is not extrapolated past it. The TIPS curve ends at the longest outstanding TIPS, which is not a round 30 years.
  - `z(t)` is continuously compounded in the fit and shown on a semi-annual bond-equivalent basis (`zToSA`).
- If any grid point lies more than two percentage points outside the lowest and highest yield of the included bonds: drop the whole curve. A fit that blows up on a large maturity-to-maturity discontinuity is not drawn.
- If fewer than three grid points remain: produce no curve.

## Which bonds each curve is fitted to

- **TIPS tab:** two curves per source. "Spot" is fitted to the ask prices. "Spot SA" is fitted to the ask prices multiplied by the [SA Price Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-price-factor).
- **Treasuries tab:** one "Spot" curve per source, fitted to every Bill, Note and Bond.
  - Bills are zero-coupon and anchor the short end.
  - A STRIPS is excluded: its price and yield already state a pure zero-coupon discount, so it cannot be fitted as a plain coupon bond.
  - An Unclassified CUSIP (a root the [Treasury CUSIP Reference](../../knowledge/Treasury_CUSIP_Reference.md) does not recognise) is excluded: its cash-flow structure is not known.
  - The fit does not depend on the Bills, Notes and Bonds checkboxes, which select the per-bond series drawn, or on the Maturity Range, which crops the terms listed and drawn.
- **Breakeven tab:** the Spot BEI uses a nominal curve fitted to the Notes and Bonds and the TIPS SA curve ([Spot BEI](#spot-bei)).

<a id="spot-bei"></a>
## Spot BEI

Spot BEI is a nominal curve fitted to the Notes and Bonds less the TIPS SA curve at the same Term, calculated in [Across terms (3.5)](./3.5_Calculate_Breakeven_Inflation.md#across-terms).

## Known limitation: free decay parameters

The two decay parameters are fitted and not fixed, and the Svensson parameters are not uniquely identified, so a small change in the bond set moves the curve by 5 to 8 bp at some maturities ([Spot Curve Verification](./Spot_Curve_Verification.md)).
