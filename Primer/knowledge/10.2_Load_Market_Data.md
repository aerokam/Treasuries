# 10.2 Load market data

**Specifies:** [10.2 Load market data](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_PRIMER)
**Implemented by:** `Primer/src/primer-data.js` (`loadPrimerData`, `pickFeaturedTips`, `pickFeaturedNote`, `datedDateRefCpiFor`), `Primer/index.html`, `shared/src/csv.js`, `shared/src/ref-cpi.js#lookupRefCpi`
**Input:** [FedInvest prices (S1)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1), [Ref CPI (S3)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s3) and [TIPS reference data (S2)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s2)
**Output:** [TIPS Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-prices), [Treasury Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices), the [Daily Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#daily-ref-cpi), [TIPS Reference Data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-reference-data) and the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) to [Run calculators (10.3)](./10.3_Run_Calculators.md); [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables) to the user (the source line)

Read the three stores when the page opens, so that a calculator opens on a real security.

<a id="load-market-data"></a>
- Fetch the three stores in parallel, each with the browser cache bypassed.
- Read S1: the first line states the Settlement Date, and the table under its header row holds the rows. Keep a row that has a Maturity Date and a Coupon that is a number. Read its Dated Date Ref CPI, Price and Yield as numbers, and as none when the value is not a number or is 0.
- Read S3 as the date and the Ref CPI of each row.
- Read S2 as the CUSIP, Maturity Date, Dated Date, Coupon, Dated Date Ref CPI and Term of each row.
- Pick the featured TIPS: of the S1 rows whose type is TIPS and that have a Dated Date Ref CPI, the one whose Maturity Date is closest to 10 years after the Settlement Date.
- Pick the featured note: of the S1 rows whose type is MARKET BASED NOTE or MARKET BASED BOND, the one whose Maturity Date is closest to 10 years after the Settlement Date. A year is 365.25 days.
- Take the Dated Date Ref CPI of a TIPS from S2, and from its S1 row when S2 has none.
- When the load completes, state in the source line "Live data: FedInvest prices/yields, TreasuryDirect Reference CPI — settlement " and the Settlement Date.
- When a fetch fails, state in the source line "Live data unavailable: " and the reason. There is no offline fallback: a page that needs the data states "Live data unavailable (" and the reason "). This page needs it — try reloading."
