# 10.3 Run calculators

**Specifies:** [10.3 Run calculators](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_PRIMER)
**Implemented by:** `Primer/src/primer-calcs.js` (`wireIndexRatioCalc`, `wireInflAdjPrincipal`, `wirePriceVsYieldCalc`, `CALCS`), `shared/src/bond-math.js#priceFromYield`, `shared/src/ref-cpi.js` (`indexRatio`, `TREASURY_ROUNDING`, `NO_ROUNDING`)
**Input:** [TIPS Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-prices), [Treasury Prices](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices), the [Daily Ref CPI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#daily-ref-cpi), [TIPS Reference Data](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-reference-data) and the [Settlement Date](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), from [Load market data (10.2)](./10.2_Load_Market_Data.md); [View Selections](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#view-selections) from the user (the entries of a calculator)
**Output:** [Charts and Tables](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#charts-and-tables) to the user

Mount a calculator in each page of the deck that has one, and recalculate its outputs at each entry. `CALCS` maps the id of the page to its calculator, and [Render deck pages (10.4)](./10.4_Render_Deck_Pages.md) mounts it after the blocks of the page.

<a id="index-ratio-calculator"></a>
## Index Ratio calculator

- List each TIPS of S2 that has a Dated Date Ref CPI, by Maturity Date, as its CUSIP and its Maturity Date, starting on the featured TIPS.
- Take a date from a date field, bounded by the first and last dates of S3 and not earlier than the Dated Date of the selected TIPS, starting on the last date of S3.
- Show the Dated Date Ref CPI with the Dated Date, the Ref CPI of the date, the [Index Ratio](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#index-ratio) to 5 decimal places, and $1,000 of par as inflation-adjusted by the Index Ratio rounded as Treasury rounds it.
- When S3 has no Ref CPI for the date, or the TIPS has no Dated Date Ref CPI, state "No Ref CPI data for that date."

<a id="inflation-adjusted-principal-calculator"></a>
## Inflation-adjusted principal calculator

- Use the featured TIPS, with its Dated Date Ref CPI and the Ref CPI of the Settlement Date, or the Ref CPI of the last date of S3 when S3 has none for the Settlement Date.
- Take a par amount, starting at 1,000, minimum 100, in steps of 100.
- Show the Index Ratio to 5 decimal places, the par amount, the [Inflation-Adjusted Principal](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#inflation-adjusted-principal), which is the par amount times the Index Ratio rounded as Treasury rounds it, and the next semiannual coupon, which is that principal times the Coupon Rate divided by 2.
- When no Index Ratio can be calculated, state "Live Ref CPI unavailable for this date."

<a id="price-vs-yield-calculator"></a>
## Price vs. Yield calculator

- Use the featured note, with its Coupon Rate.
- Take a yield from a slider, from 0% to 8% in steps of 0.05%, starting on the yield of the note.
- Show the Coupon Rate, the Yield and the [Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) from `priceFromYield` at the Settlement Date to 4 decimal places, and "premium" when the Price is 100 or more and "discount" otherwise.
- When no Price can be calculated, state "Can't price this combination."
