Level 2 — Yields Monitor

No process here writes a data store. Every flow ends at 2.5, 2.6 or 2.7 and is gone when the page closes. CNBC (E5) is drawn as a box, in the store column, since this is the one place a Level 2 diagram in this portal shows an app reading an entity directly, live — no acquisition job stands between it and the app (see DFD_Worklist.md §4.0). Its two flows are independently named: market yields, its chart-bar feed, to 2.1; live quotes, its quote service, to 2.2. 2.1 explodes at Level 3. 2.3 and 2.4 each read Bond holidays (S16) directly, to exclude a weekend or holiday bar from their own calculation, rather than through 2.1. Each flow is named by the one structure it holds, defined in Data Dictionary §6.4.
