Level 3 — Yields Monitor 2.1 Assemble range data

One process per family of UI ranges — 2D/10D, 1Y/2Y/3Y, 10Y/ALL and Custom — each independent: only one is active at a time, so nothing here combines their output. 2.1.5 runs beside the active one when Show CMTs is on. Every process here drills to its own section of 2.1 Assemble range data. Every process except 2.1.5 also reads market yields live, from CNBC (E5)'s chart-bar feed, for its own primary feed or its own live tip — only Intraday archive (S18) and Yield history (S6) are the fallbacks drawn on the left.
