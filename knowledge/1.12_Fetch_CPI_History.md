## Scope

Read the [Monthly CPI-U](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#monthly-cpi-u) that [BLS (E4)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e4) states, for the not-seasonally-adjusted series (`CUUR0000SA0`) and the seasonally adjusted series (`CUSR0000SA0`) alike, from January 1913 to the current year, and write [CPI history (S8)](./DataStores.md#s8): one row per year and month, replacing the store's previous contents in full — this is the same two series [Fetch monthly CPI (1.9)](./1.9_Fetch_Monthly_CPI.md) also reads, fetched separately (see 1.9 for why).

- Fetch BLS in ten-year windows, its free-tier limit per request, from 1913 up through the current year.
  - Drop the annual-average row BLS reports for each year (period code `M13`) and any period code that is not a plain two-digit month, keeping one row per calendar month only.
  - A month with no value for a series (BLS states `-`, or omits it) is written blank rather than dropped, so the row still holds whichever of the two series does have a value.
- Sort the rows oldest first, by year and then by month — the reverse of [Fetch monthly CPI (1.9)](./1.9_Fetch_Monthly_CPI.md)'s newest-first order, since the two stores serve different readers.

**Output:** [CPI history (S8)](./DataStores.md#s8), read directly by CPI Explorer.
