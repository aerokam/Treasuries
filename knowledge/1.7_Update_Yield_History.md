# 1.7 Update yield history

**Specifies:** [1.7 Update yield history](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_INGESTION)  
**Implemented by:** `YieldsMonitor/scripts/updateYieldsHistory.js`, `YieldsMonitor/scripts/cnbcBarDates.js#mergeHistory`  
**Reference:** [Close Price Investigation](../YieldsMonitor/knowledge/Close_Price_Investigation.md)

## Dependencies

- [DATA_DICTIONARY.md](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md) — [CNBC (E5)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e5), and the data flow [Market Yields](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#market-yields)
- [DataStores.md](./DataStores.md) — [Yield history (S6)](./DataStores.md#s6)
- `scripts/setup-windows-tasks.ps1` — the schedule of the Windows task that runs this process

---

Read the [Market Yields](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#market-yields) that [CNBC (E5)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e5)'s chart-bar feed states, for each of the fourteen symbols [Yield history (S6)](./DataStores.md#s6) tracks, and write [Yield history (S6)](./DataStores.md#s6):

- For each symbol:
  - Read each of CNBC's `ALL`, `5Y`, `6M`, `3M` and `1M` chart-bar feeds.
  - Merge the `6M`, `3M` and `1M` bars (one per trading day) into the symbol's history already accumulated in [Yield history (S6)](./DataStores.md#s6), each bar dated at its own TradeTime, so a fresher feed's Close for a shared date overrides an earlier one.
    - Drop a bar dated on or after the current Eastern-time day: that day is still live and has not closed.
  - Merge the `5Y` bars (one per week) and the `ALL` bars (one per quarter). CNBC sets a bar's TradeTime at the start of its week (Sunday 00:00 Eastern) or quarter (the first day), and its Close is the Close of the last bond trading day in that week or quarter. Each such Close is dated as follows:
    - A weekly Close is dated that week's Friday.
    - A quarterly Close is dated the quarter's last weekday.
    - A week or quarter that has not ended is dropped.
    - A week that already holds a date at daily resolution is dropped, and a quarter that already holds a date at daily or weekly resolution is dropped, so a finer date is never coarsened.
  - Re-date any Close already accumulated in [Yield history (S6)](./DataStores.md#s6) at a weekly or quarterly bar's TradeTime by the same rule: a Sunday-dated Close is a weekly bar's, and a Close dated the first day of January, April, July or October is a quarterly bar's where it equals the `ALL` feed's Close for that date or predates the earliest `6M`, `3M` or `1M` bar.
  - Stamp every retained date 15:00 Eastern, the time CNBC's daily-feed close tracks — the ~3 PM benchmark close, not the 17:05 session print (see [Close Price Investigation](../YieldsMonitor/knowledge/Close_Price_Investigation.md)).
- Write one closing value per completed trading day per symbol at daily resolution, per week before that, and per quarter before that, rewriting the whole file each run.

**Output:** [Yield history (S6)](./DataStores.md#s6), to [Assemble range data (2.1)](../YieldsMonitor/knowledge/2.1_Assemble_Range_Data.md).
