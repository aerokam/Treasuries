# 1.9 Fetch monthly CPI

**Specifies:** [1.9 Fetch monthly CPI](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_INGEST_MONTHLYCPI)  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/scripts/fetchCpiBls.js`  
**Scheduled by:** `scripts/setup-windows-tasks.ps1`, with no standalone trigger of its own: it chains from the `SaFactors` task

Read the [Monthly CPI-U](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#monthly-cpi-u) that [BLS (E4)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e4) states, for the not-seasonally-adjusted series (`CUUR0000SA0`) and the seasonally adjusted series (`CUSR0000SA0`) alike, and write [Monthly CPI (S17)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s17):

- Fetch from 2019 to the current year.
- Set each series' value at each year and month to BLS's stated value.
  - October 2025 is the one exception: BLS collected no data that month, and the two series are hardcoded to 325.604 (NSA) and 325.551 (SA) whenever BLS's own response leaves either blank.
- Sort the rows newest first, by year and then by month.
- Write one row per year and month, replacing the store's previous contents in full.

This is the same two series [Fetch CPI history (1.12)](./1.12_Fetch_CPI_History.md) also reads, fetched separately so that the refresh of [Monthly CPI (S17)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s17) does not depend on the release-date-triggered refresh of [CPI history (S8)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s8).
