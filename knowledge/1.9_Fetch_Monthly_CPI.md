## Scope

Reads the [Monthly CPI-U](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#monthly-cpi-u) that [BLS (E4)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e4) states, for the not-seasonally-adjusted series (`CUUR0000SA0`) and the seasonally adjusted series (`CUSR0000SA0`) alike, from 2019 to the current year, and writes [Monthly CPI (S17)](./DataStores.md#s17): one row per year and month, replacing the store's previous contents in full — this is not a merge.

- Take BLS's stated value for each series at each year and month.
  - October 2025 is the one exception: BLS collected no data that month, and the two series are hardcoded to 325.604 (NSA) and 325.551 (SA) whenever BLS's own response leaves either blank.
- Sort the rows newest first, by year and then by month.

This is the same two series [Fetch CPI history (1.12)](./1.12_Fetch_CPI_History.md) also reads, fetched separately so the daily App. B interpolation that produces [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DataStores.md#s4) does not depend on CPI history's own release-date-triggered refresh.

**Output:** [Monthly CPI (S17)](./DataStores.md#s17), to [1.10](./1.10_Interpolate_Daily_Ref_CPI_NSA_And_SA.md), in the same scheduled run.
