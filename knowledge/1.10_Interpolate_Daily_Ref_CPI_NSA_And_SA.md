# 1.10 Interpolate daily Ref CPI NSA and SA

**Specifies:** [1.10 Interpolate daily Ref CPI NSA and SA](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_INGESTION)  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/scripts/calcRefCpi.js`, `shared/src/ref-cpi.js#refCpiFromMonthly`

## Dependencies

- [DATA_DICTIONARY.md](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md) — [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi), [SA Factor](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor)
- [DataStores.md](./DataStores.md) — [Monthly CPI (S17)](./DataStores.md#s17), [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DataStores.md#s4)
- `scripts/setup-windows-tasks.ps1` — this process has no standalone trigger of its own; it chains from the `SaFactors` task

---

Read [Monthly CPI (S17)](./DataStores.md#s17), produced by [Fetch monthly CPI (1.9)](./1.9_Fetch_Monthly_CPI.md) in the same scheduled run; interpolate a daily [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) for both the not-seasonally-adjusted and the seasonally adjusted series; and write [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DataStores.md#s4):

- For each of the two monthly series (NSA, SA), for each calendar day from three months after the first month [Monthly CPI (S17)](./DataStores.md#s17) has to three months after the last:
  - On the first of the month: the day's value is the value of the month three months earlier, per the 31 CFR § 356 Appendix B rule that a [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) always looks back three months.
  - On every other day: linearly interpolate between the value three months earlier and the value two months earlier, by the day's position in the month — day 2 of a 30-day month is `1/30` of the way from the three-months-back value to the two-months-back value, day 30 is `29/30` of the way.
  - Truncate the result to six decimal places, then round the truncated value to five — Treasury's two-step convention for a [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi).
  - A day whose required month (three months, or for days after the first, also two months, earlier) is not yet in [Monthly CPI (S17)](./DataStores.md#s17) produces no value, and neither does any later day in that same output range, since the same missing month is needed again.
- For each calendar day that has a [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) in both series: divide the NSA value by the SA value to get that day's [SA Factor](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor).
- Write one row per calendar day, each with a [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) value for the not-seasonally-adjusted series and the seasonally adjusted series, and their [SA Factor](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) — newest first.

The SA series has no other source. The NSA series is the calculated fallback for [Ref CPI (S3)](./DataStores.md#s3), which [Fetch daily Ref CPI (1.13)](./1.13_Fetch_Daily_Ref_CPI.md) retrieves from [TreasuryDirect SecIndex (E2)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e2) (see [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi)).

**Output:** [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DataStores.md#s4), to [Calculate SA and SAO yields (1.11)](./1.11_Calculate_SA_And_SAO_Yields.md) and to every process across the portal that reads an [SA Factor](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) or a [Maturity SA Factor](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-sa-factor).
