# 1.10 Interpolate daily Ref CPI NSA and SA

## Scope

Reads [Monthly CPI (S17)](./DataStores.md#s17), produced by [Fetch monthly CPI (1.9)](./1.9_Fetch_Monthly_CPI.md) in the same scheduled run, and writes [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DataStores.md#s4): one row per calendar day, each with a [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) value calculated independently for the not-seasonally-adjusted series and the seasonally adjusted series, and their [SA Factor](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor).

- For each of the two monthly series (NSA, SA), for each calendar day from three months after the first month [Monthly CPI (S17)](./DataStores.md#s17) has to three months after the last:
  - On the first of the month: the day's value is that month's own value, three months earlier, per the 31 CFR § 356 Appendix B rule that a [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) always looks back three months.
  - On every other day: linearly interpolate between the value three months earlier and the value two months earlier, by the day's position in the month — day 2 of a 30-day month is `1/30` of the way from the three-months-back value to the two-months-back value, day 30 is `29/30` of the way.
  - Truncate the result to six decimal places, then round the truncated value to five — Treasury's own two-step convention for a [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi).
  - A day whose required month (three months, or for days after the first, also two months, earlier) is not yet in [Monthly CPI (S17)](./DataStores.md#s17) produces no value, and neither does any later day in that same output range, since the same missing month is needed again.
- Divide the day's NSA value by its SA value to get that day's [SA Factor](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor).
- Write the rows newest first.

This is the sole source of a daily seasonally adjusted [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi): BLS publishes CPI-SA only monthly, and Appendix B's daily interpolation officially applies to the NSA series alone, so the SA half of this calculation has no official or retrieved counterpart to fall back to. The NSA half is a fallback only — [Ref CPI (S3)](./DataStores.md#s3), retrieved directly from [TreasuryDirect SecIndex (E2)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e2) by [Fetch daily Ref CPI (1.13)](./1.13_Fetch_Daily_Ref_CPI.md), is the one every app uses for an NSA [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi).

**Output:** [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DataStores.md#s4), to [Calculate SA and SAO yields (1.11)](./1.11_Calculate_SA_And_SAO_Yields.md) — which reads whatever is currently in the store, not necessarily what this same run just wrote, since [Calculate SA and SAO yields (1.11)](./1.11_Calculate_SA_And_SAO_Yields.md) also runs on its own schedule, three times a day — and to every process across the portal that reads an [SA Factor](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) or a [Maturity SA Factor](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-sa-factor).
