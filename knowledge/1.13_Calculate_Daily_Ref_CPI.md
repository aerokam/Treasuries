# 1.13 Calculate daily Ref CPI

**Specifies:** [1.13 Calculate daily Ref CPI](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_INGEST_DAILYREFCPI)  
**Implemented by:** `scripts/calcDailyRefCpi.js`, `shared/src/ref-cpi.js#refCpiFromMonthly`, `shared/src/ref-cpi.js#fillUnreportedMonths`  
**Scheduled by:** `scripts/syncCpiTasks.ps1`, with no standalone trigger: it chains from the `FetchCpi` task, after [1.12 Fetch CPI](./1.12_Fetch_CPI.md) writes [CPI (S8)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s8)

Read [CPI (S8)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s8) and [RefCPI (S3)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s3), calculate the daily [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) for both the not-seasonally-adjusted and the seasonally adjusted series, and write S3:

- Read the `NSA` and the `SA` value of each month of [CPI (S8)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s8).
  - A month with a blank value, which BLS did not report (October 2025), takes the number [31 CFR 356 Appendix B §I.B.4(iv)](./31_CFR_356_Definitions_And_Formulas.md) derives from the last available twelve-month change of that series: `fillUnreportedMonths`, applied to the NSA series and to the SA series alike. Treasury states 325.604 for the NSA series of October 2025, and Ref CPI from 2026-01-01 to 2026-01-31 rests on it.
- For each calendar day from 1997-01-15, the dated date of the first TIPS issued, calculate the Ref CPI of each series with `refCpiFromMonthly`:
  - On the first of the month: the value of the month three months earlier, per the 31 CFR § 356 Appendix B rule that a [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) always looks back three months.
  - On every other day: linearly interpolate between the value three months earlier and the value two months earlier, by the day's position in the month. Day 2 of a 30-day month is `1/30` of the way from the three-months-back value to the two-months-back value, and day 30 is `29/30` of the way.
  - Truncate the result to six decimal places, then round the truncated value to five, Treasury's two-step convention for a [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi).
  - A day whose required month is not yet in [CPI (S8)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s8) has no value, and neither has any later day. The series therefore ends on the first of the month three months after the latest month of [CPI (S8)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s8), the day whose value is that latest month as it stands. With October 2026 the latest month, the series ends on 2027-01-01.
- Keep the `Ref CPI NSA` of every date [RefCPI (S3)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s3) already holds. Treasury continues to use the CPI as first reported when BLS later revises it ([31 CFR 356 Appendix B §I.B.4(i)](./31_CFR_356_Definitions_And_Formulas.md)), and [CPI (S8)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s8) states the revised value for 426 dates of March to November 2000 and July to November 2016 where the two differ. Only the days after the last date S3 holds take a newly calculated `Ref CPI NSA`.
- Recalculate the `Ref CPI SA` of every date in full from the SA series of [CPI (S8)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s8): BLS revises that series each February for the five years before, and no official daily series exists to keep.
- For each date, divide the `Ref CPI NSA` by the `Ref CPI SA` to get the [SA Factor](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor), to five decimal places.
- Write one row per calendar day, ascending by date.
- Write nothing and exit with a failure code when [CPI (S8)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s8) cannot be read, or when the series has a gap of more than one calendar day: [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) is defined for every calendar day with no gap allowed.

[RefCPI (S3)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s3) is written at the time [CPI (S8)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s8) is, so it states each Ref CPI as soon as BLS has published the month it rests on, before [TreasuryDirect SecIndex (E2)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e2) states it. [Check daily Ref CPI (1.17)](./1.17_Check_Daily_Ref_CPI.md) compares its `Ref CPI NSA` with [TreasuryDirect SecIndex (E2)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e2) once that source has published.

A new [RefCPI (S3)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s3) changes the [SA Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) of every TIPS, so the run ends by refreshing the yield curve data sets with [1.3 Calculate yield curve data sets](./1.3_Calculate_Yield_Curve_Data_Sets.md).
