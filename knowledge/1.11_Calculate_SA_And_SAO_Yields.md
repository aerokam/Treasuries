## Scope

Reads every TIPS row [Market quotes (S7)](./DataStores.md#s7) holds, reads [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DataStores.md#s4) and [Bond holidays (S16)](./DataStores.md#s16), and writes [SA and SAO yields (S10)](./DataStores.md#s10): the ask, SA and SAO Yield of every TIPS quoted, one row per CUSIP, sorted by maturity date. This process runs on two independent triggers: three times a day whenever [Market quotes (S7)](./DataStores.md#s7) is refreshed, and once more daily as the last step chained after [Interpolate daily Ref CPI NSA and SA (1.10)](./1.10_Interpolate_Daily_Ref_CPI_NSA_And_SA.md) — either run reads whatever [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DataStores.md#s4) currently holds, not necessarily a value either trigger just wrote.

- Determine the settlement date: the next business day, per [Bond holidays (S16)](./DataStores.md#s16), after the date [Market quotes (S7)](./DataStores.md#s7)'s own footer states it was downloaded.
- For each TIPS row [Market quotes (S7)](./DataStores.md#s7) holds with a usable price, coupon and maturity date:
  - Calculate its ask Yield from its ask price, the same yield-from-price calculation every other source and security type in the portal uses.
  - Look up the [SA Factor](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) at the settlement date and the [Maturity SA Factor](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-sa-factor) at the maturity date, both from [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DataStores.md#s4) — see [3.2 Adjust for seasonality](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) for how a Maturity SA Factor beyond the measured series is itself derived.
    - Drop the TIPS if either factor is unavailable.
  - Calculate its [SA Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) the same way [3.2](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) does: the yield of the ask price scaled by the ratio of the two factors.
    - Drop the TIPS if the ask Yield or the SA Yield cannot be calculated.
- Smooth the SA yields of every TIPS that survived, together, into an [SAO Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yield) each, the same curve-smoothing calculation [3.3 Adjust for other effects](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.3_Adjust_For_Other_Effects.md) applies.

**Output:** [SA and SAO yields (S10)](./DataStores.md#s10), read directly by TipsLadderManager, [Enrich fund holdings (1.14)](./1.14_Enrich_Fund_Holdings.md), and TIPS Reference.
