# 1.11 Calculate SA and SAO yields

**Specifies:** [1.11 Calculate SA and SAO yields](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_INGESTION)  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/scripts/updateSaSaoYields.js`

## Dependencies

- [DATA_DICTIONARY.md](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md) — [SA Factor](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor), [Maturity SA Factor](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-sa-factor), [SA Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield), [SAO Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yield), [Bond Holiday](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday)
- [DataStores.md](./DataStores.md) — [Market quotes (S7)](./DataStores.md#s7), [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DataStores.md#s4), [Bond holidays (S16)](./DataStores.md#s16), [SA and SAO yields (S10)](./DataStores.md#s10)
- `scripts/setup-windows-tasks.ps1` — this process has no standalone trigger of its own; it chains from the Fidelity Quotes and SA Factor Update tasks

---

Read every TIPS row [Market quotes (S7)](./DataStores.md#s7) holds, read [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DataStores.md#s4) and [Bond holidays (S16)](./DataStores.md#s16); calculate the ask, SA and SAO Yield of each TIPS; and write [SA and SAO yields (S10)](./DataStores.md#s10):

- Determine the settlement date: the next business day, per [Bond holidays (S16)](./DataStores.md#s16), after the date [Market quotes (S7)](./DataStores.md#s7)'s own footer states it was downloaded.
- For each TIPS row [Market quotes (S7)](./DataStores.md#s7) holds with a usable price, coupon and maturity date:
  - Calculate its ask Yield from its ask price, the same yield-from-price calculation every other source and security type in the portal uses.
  - Look up the [SA Factor](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) at the settlement date and the [Maturity SA Factor](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-sa-factor) at the maturity date, both from [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DataStores.md#s4) — see [Adjust for seasonality (3.2)](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) for how a [Maturity SA Factor](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-sa-factor) beyond the measured series is itself derived.
    - Drop the TIPS if either factor is unavailable.
  - Calculate its [SA Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) the same way [Adjust for seasonality (3.2)](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md) does: the yield of the ask price scaled by the ratio of the two factors.
    - Drop the TIPS if the ask Yield or the [SA Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) cannot be calculated.
- Smooth the [SA Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) of every TIPS that survived, together, into an [SAO Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yield) each, the same curve-smoothing calculation [Adjust for other effects (3.3)](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.3_Adjust_For_Other_Effects.md) applies.
- Write one row per CUSIP, sorted by maturity date: the ask, SA and SAO Yield of every TIPS quoted.

**This process chains from two independent places.** Three times a day after [Download market quotes (1.2)](./1.2_Download_Market_Quotes.md) refreshes [Market quotes (S7)](./DataStores.md#s7), and once more daily as the last step after [Interpolate daily Ref CPI NSA and SA (1.10)](./1.10_Interpolate_Daily_Ref_CPI_NSA_And_SA.md) — either run reads whatever [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DataStores.md#s4) currently holds, not necessarily a value either trigger just wrote.

**Output:** [SA and SAO yields (S10)](./DataStores.md#s10), read directly by TipsLadderManager, [Enrich fund holdings (1.14)](./1.14_Enrich_Fund_Holdings.md), and TIPS Reference.
