## Scope

Reads the [Fund Holdings](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#fund-holdings) that [Vanguard Advisors (E7)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e7) through [BlackRock iShares (E12)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e12) state, for the eight tracked fund tickers across the six providers, and writes [Fund holdings (S11)](./DataStores.md#s11): a raw and an enriched CSV per ticker, the enriched file adding [Market quotes (S7)](./DataStores.md#s7)/[SA and SAO yields (S10)](./DataStores.md#s10)-derived yield, term and duration to every holding.

- For each of the six providers: fetch that provider's own holdings export for its ticker or tickers, independently of every other provider — a fetch failure here is caught and logged, and does not stop any other provider's fetch or any ticker's enrichment from proceeding.
- For each of the eight tickers, regardless of which providers just succeeded or failed: enrich that ticker's raw holdings CSV — see [FundHoldings 1.0 §Enrichment Logic](../FundHoldings/knowledge/1.0_FundHoldings.md#enrichment-logic) for the calculation itself — and write the result to [Fund holdings (S11)](./DataStores.md#s11).
- End the run as a failure, after every ticker has still been enriched and uploaded, if any provider's fetch failed — so the schedule's own retry/alert behavior sees the failure, without withholding the tickers that did fetch successfully.

**Output:** [Fund holdings (S11)](./DataStores.md#s11), read directly by the Fund Holdings app.
