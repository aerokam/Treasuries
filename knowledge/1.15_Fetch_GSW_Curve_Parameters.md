## Scope

Reads the [GSW Curve Parameters](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#gsw-curve-parameters) that [Federal Reserve FEDS 2008-05 (E13)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e13) states and writes [GSW curve parameters (S12)](./DataStores.md#s12): the most recent row of the published table, unevaluated — the six Svensson coefficients and their observation date, not a curve of evaluated yields.

- Read the Federal Reserve's own published table of recent Svensson fits, newest row first.
- Take the first row's own observation date and its `BETA0`–`BETA3`, `TAU1` and `TAU2` values, as the source states them.

**Output:** [GSW curve parameters (S12)](./DataStores.md#s12), read directly by YieldCurves as a reference overlay against its own spot fit, drawn only on the analysis view opened with `?gsw`.
