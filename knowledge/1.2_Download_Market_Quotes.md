## Scope

Reads [Fidelity Fixed Income (E6)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e6) from the local downloads folder and writes [Market quotes (S7)](./DataStores.md#s7): the export's own rows, unchanged, except for one transformation — every `="value"` Excel literal-string wrapper the export applies to a field is stripped to `value`. No row is added, dropped, reordered or restructured; [Market quotes (S7)](./DataStores.md#s7) states the export's own columns in the export's own order.

The run is skipped on a weekend or a [Bond Holiday](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), checked against [Bond holidays (S16)](./DataStores.md#s16) before the browser session that fetches [Fidelity Fixed Income (E6)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e6) opens. How [Fidelity Fixed Income (E6)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e6) is obtained — the browser automation, sign-in and MFA steps — is out of scope for this spec; it is documented in the gitignored `knowledge/Data_Pipeline_Local.md`, which this spec links to and does not restate.

A successful run chains into [Calculate SA and SAO yields (1.11)](./1.11_Calculate_SA_And_SAO_Yields.md), so a fresh [Market quotes (S7)](./DataStores.md#s7) is followed immediately by a fresh [SA and SAO yields (S10)](./DataStores.md#s10) built from it.

**Output:** [Market quotes (S7)](./DataStores.md#s7), to [Parse market quotes (3.1.2)](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md#parse-market-quotes).
