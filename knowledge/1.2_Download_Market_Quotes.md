# 1.2 Download market quotes

**Specifies:** [1.2 Download market quotes](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_INGESTION)  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/scripts/marketQuotesDownload.js`, `YieldCurves/scripts/marketQuotesFromExport.js`, `YieldCurves/scripts/uploadMarketQuotes.js`, `YieldCurves/scripts/marketQuotesCatchupIfStale.js`  
**Scheduled by:** `scripts/setup-windows-tasks.ps1`  
**Reference:** the steps specific to the source of the download are in a local file that is not in the repository

Read [Market quotes source (E6)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e6) from the local downloads folder, and write [Market quotes (S7)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7):

- Skip the run on a weekend or a [Bond Holiday](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), checked against [Bond holidays (S16)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s16) before the browser session that fetches [Market quotes source (E6)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e6) opens.
- Remove the `="value"` Excel literal-string wrapper from every field that has one, then keep from each row only the fields [Market quotes (S7)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7) states:
  - `type` is the `Product` field, `TIPS` or `Treasury`.
  - `cusip` is the `Cusip` field.
  - `maturity` is the `Maturity date` field, `YYYY-MM-DD`.
  - `coupon` is the `Coupon` field divided by 100.
  - `askPrice` and `bidPrice` are the `Ask price` and `Bid price` fields without the quantity quoted beside each price.
  - `adjAskPrice` and `adjBidPrice` are the `Adjusted ask price` and `Adjusted bid price` fields, for a TIPS row only.
- Write the [Download Date](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) of [Market quotes source (E6)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e6) as the first line of [Market quotes (S7)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7), `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM ZONE` on a 24-hour clock, with the time zone the date line of [Market quotes source (E6)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e6) states.
  - A date line that states no time zone fails the run, leaving [Market quotes (S7)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7) unchanged.
- Before writing [Market quotes (S7)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7), parse its TIPS rows ([`parseTipsQuotes`](../shared/src/market-quotes.js)) and require at least 10, against 50 or more in a complete day's capture.
  - Below 10: leave [Market quotes (S7)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7) unchanged and exit with a failure code. A capture cut short by an incomplete page load, an unapplied product-type filter or a dropped session is a valid CSV holding only a header and boilerplate, and writing it would replace a complete capture with no TIPS rows.
- The download stays in the local downloads folder, where the next download overwrites it. The download is not published.
- If the capture is rejected, or the upload fails for any reason, repeat the capture of [Market quotes source (E6)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e6) on the same signed-in page, up to 3 attempts in all, before the run reports failure.
  - No fresh sign-in or MFA between attempts.

**How [Market quotes source (E6)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e6) is obtained is out of scope for this spec.** The page-specific steps (sign-in, MFA, filtering and the download itself) and the raw download's column names are in a local file that is not in the repository.

**A successful run chains into [Calculate SA and SAO yields (1.11)](./1.11_Calculate_SA_And_SAO_Yields.md).** A fresh [Market quotes (S7)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7) is followed immediately by a fresh [SA and SAO yields (S10)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s10) built from it.

**Output:** [Market quotes (S7)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7), to [Parse market quotes (3.1.2)](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md#parse-market-quotes).
