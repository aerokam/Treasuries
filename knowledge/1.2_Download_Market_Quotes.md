## Scope

Reads [Fidelity Fixed Income (E6)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e6) from the local downloads folder and writes [Market quotes (S7)](./DataStores.md#s7): the export's own rows, unchanged, except for one transformation — every `="value"` Excel literal-string wrapper the export applies to a field is stripped to `value`. No row is added, dropped, reordered or restructured; S7 states the export's own columns in the export's own order.

The run is skipped on a weekend or a [Bond Holiday](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), checked against the bond holidays store before the browser session that fetches E6 opens. How E6 is obtained — the browser automation, sign-in and MFA steps — is out of scope for this spec; it is documented in the gitignored `knowledge/Data_Pipeline_Local.md`, which this spec links to and does not restate.

A successful run chains into the SA and SAO yields job (1.11), so a fresh Market quotes (S7) is followed immediately by a fresh SA and SAO yields (S10) built from it.

**Output:** [Market quotes (S7)](./DataStores.md#s7), to [3.1.2](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md#parse-market-quotes).
