# 1.3.1 Parse sources and calculate yields

**Specifies:** [1.3.1 Parse sources and calculate yields](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL4_INGEST_YIELDCURVEDATA_PARSE) and the seven processes it decomposes into  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/scripts/updateYieldCurveDataSets.js`, through `shared/src/yield-curve-stores.js#buildYieldCurveStores`, which calls `shared/src/fedinvest-prices.js`, `shared/src/market-quotes.js`, `shared/src/tips-yields.js` and `shared/src/treasury-yields.js`  
**Reference:** [FedInvest Treasury Pricing Methodology](../YieldCurves/knowledge/FedInvest_Treasury_Pricing_Methodology.md)

Read [FedInvest prices (S1)](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1), [Market quotes (S7)](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7), [Ref CPI (S3)](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s3) and [Bond holidays (S16)](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s16), and produce the [TIPS Yields](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields) and the [Treasury Yields](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields). The [SA Factors](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) and the [Download Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) are produced with them:

- Every process below is a step of [Calculate yield curve data sets (1.3)](./1.3_Calculate_Yield_Curve_Data_Sets.md) and writes no data store itself; that job writes the three stores from what they produce.

**Every yield is calculated from price.** [FedInvest prices (S1)](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1) states a [Yield](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) per security, calculated from price by [Download FedInvest prices (1.1)](1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md), and [Market quotes (S7)](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7) states prices only. The job does not read the yield of S1. It calculates each yield from price at the [Settlement Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of that source: every TIPS in [Calculate TIPS yields (1.3.1.6)](#calculate-tips-yields), and every nominal Treasury in [Calculate Treasury yields (1.3.1.7)](#calculate-treasury-yields). One method across both sources, both security types and both sides of a quote makes the four sets comparable with each other, so a difference between two of them is a difference in the market and not in the arithmetic.

---

## Processes

<a id="parse-fedinvest-prices"></a>
### 1.3.1.1 Parse FedInvest prices

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/fedinvest-prices.js#parseFedInvestPrices`

Read [FedInvest prices (S1)](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1) and produce the [Settlement Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), the [TIPS Prices](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-prices) and the [Treasury Prices](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices):

- Read the Settlement Date from the first line of the store.
  - It is the one [Determine settlement date (1.1.1)](1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#determine-settlement-date) assigned to the prices, T+0.
- Separate the TIPS Prices from the Treasury Prices by the `type` column.

<a id="parse-market-quotes"></a>
### 1.3.1.2 Parse market quotes

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/market-quotes.js#parseTipsQuotes`, `shared/src/market-quotes.js#parseTreasuryQuotes`, `shared/src/market-quotes.js#parseDownloadDate`

Read [Market quotes (S7)](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7) and produce the [TIPS Quotes](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-quotes), the [Treasury Quotes](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-quotes) and the [Download Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date):

- Separate the TIPS rows from the Treasury rows by the `type` column, and keep the first row for each CUSIP.
- For each TIPS row that has an ask price:
  - Read the coupon rate, the maturity date, the [ask and bid](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ask) [Price](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) and the [Adjusted Ask Price and Adjusted Bid Price](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#adjusted-price).
- For each Treasury row:
  - Drop it when it has no maturity date that can be read.
  - Set its security type, Bill, Note, Bond or STRIPS, from its CUSIP root, or Unclassified when the [Treasury CUSIP Reference](Treasury_CUSIP_Reference.md) does not recognise the root. The `type` column does not separate STRIPS from the other Treasuries, so the CUSIP root does.
  - Read the coupon rate, the maturity date and the ask and bid prices.
- Read the Download Date from the first line of the file.

<a id="parse-ref-cpi-and-sa-factors"></a>
### 1.3.1.3 Parse Ref CPI and SA factors

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/csv.js#parseCsv`

Read [Ref CPI (S3)](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s3) and produce the [SA Factors](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor):

- Produce one [SA Factor](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) for each calendar day the store covers.
- The look-up of the factor for a date, including a maturity date beyond the last row, is part of [Adjust for seasonality (1.3.2)](./1.3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md).

<a id="parse-bond-holidays"></a>
### 1.3.1.4 Parse bond holidays

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/settlement.js#parseHolidaySet`, `shared/src/csv.js#parseCsv`

Read [Bond holidays (S16)](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s16) and produce the [Bond Holidays](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday).

<a id="determine-settlement-date"></a>
### 1.3.1.5 Determine settlement date

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/settlement.js#marketSettleIso`, `shared/src/settlement.js#nextBusinessDay`

Read the [Download Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) from [Parse market quotes (1.3.1.2)](#parse-market-quotes) and the [Bond Holidays](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday) from [Parse bond holidays (1.3.1.4)](#parse-bond-holidays), and produce the [Settlement Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) the market quotes are stated at:

- Advance the Download Date by one bond trading day (T+1), past any Bond Holiday.
- The Settlement Date of a security from [FedInvest prices (S1)](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1) is the one read in [Parse FedInvest prices (1.3.1.1)](#parse-fedinvest-prices). The two sources are downloaded on separate schedules, so the two Settlement Dates can differ.

<a id="calculate-tips-yields"></a>
### 1.3.1.6 Calculate TIPS yields

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/tips-yields.js#tipsYieldsFromPrices`, `shared/src/bond-math.js#yieldFromPrice`

Read the [TIPS Prices](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-prices) or the [TIPS Quotes](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-quotes) with their [Settlement Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) and produce the [TIPS Yields](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-yields). For each TIPS, from either source — one TIPS per [Market quotes (S7)](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7) row that [Parse market quotes (1.3.1.2)](#parse-market-quotes) kept, or one TIPS per [FedInvest prices (S1)](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1) row:

- Read its [Price](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) and [Settlement Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date).
  - For a market quote: the ask price, and the [Settlement Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) that [Determine settlement date (1.3.1.5)](#determine-settlement-date) produces.
  - For FedInvest: the price that [Select prices and add TIPS reference data (1.1.2)](1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#select-prices-and-add-tips-reference-data) selects from the three FedInvest publishes (Buy, Sell, End of Day), and the [Settlement Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) that [Parse FedInvest prices (1.3.1.1)](#parse-fedinvest-prices) reads from the store.
- Calculate an [Ask Yield](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) from the [Price](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price):
  - If there is no usable [Price](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price), or the [Settlement Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) is on or after the [Maturity Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date): there is no yield, and the TIPS is dropped.
  - Otherwise: find the yield that makes the coupon and principal payments, discounted twice a year, worth exactly the [Price](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) plus accrued interest.
- If this is a market quote and has a bid [Price](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price): calculate a [Bid Yield](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) from it, the same way as the [Ask Yield](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield).
  - If there is no bid [Price](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price): the TIPS keeps its [Ask Yield](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield), with no [Bid Yield](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield).

The job runs this process once for each source.

<a id="calculate-treasury-yields"></a>
### 1.3.1.7 Calculate Treasury yields

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/market-quotes.js#parseTreasuryQuotes` (market quotes), `shared/src/treasury-yields.js#treasuryYieldsFromPrices` (FedInvest), `shared/src/bond-math.js#yieldFromPrice`

Read the [Treasury Prices](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices) or the [Treasury Quotes](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-quotes) with their [Settlement Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) and produce the [Treasury Yields](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields). For each Treasury, from either source, calculate its yield from its [Price](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) at the [Settlement Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of its source:

- If there is no usable [Price](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price), or the [Settlement Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) is on or after the [Maturity Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date): there is no yield.
- If the coupon is zero and the days to maturity are fewer than half the days in the year: calculate the yield with Treasury’s bill investment-rate formula.
  - The condition looks only at the coupon rate and the days to maturity, so a zero-coupon STRIPS in the same window gets the bill formula as well.
- Otherwise: find the yield that makes the coupon and principal payments, discounted twice a year, worth exactly the [Price](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#price) plus accrued interest.

For a market quote, one [Treasury Quotes](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-quotes) row per [Market quotes (S7)](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7) row, at the [Settlement Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) from [Determine settlement date (1.3.1.5)](#determine-settlement-date), calculated in the same pass as [Parse market quotes (1.3.1.2)](#parse-market-quotes):

- Calculate the [Ask Yield](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) from the ask price, as above.
  - If it cannot be calculated: drop the row.
- Calculate the [Bid Yield](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) from the bid price the same way.
  - If there is no bid price, or the [Bid Yield](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) cannot be calculated: keep the row with its [Ask Yield](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) only.

For FedInvest, one [Treasury Prices](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices) row per [FedInvest prices (S1)](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1) row, at the [Settlement Date](DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) from [Parse FedInvest prices (1.3.1.1)](#parse-fedinvest-prices):

- Calculate the yield from the price that [Select prices and add TIPS reference data (1.1.2)](1.1_Download_FedInvest_Prices.md#select-prices-and-add-tips-reference-data) selects, as above: one value, not a true ask or a true bid.
  - If it cannot be calculated: drop the row.

**Common** to both sources:

- A STRIPS is calculated like every other row. At a coupon of zero, no coupon cash flows are built, so a STRIPS maturing in half a year or more gets the semi-annual bond-equivalent yield of its price.
