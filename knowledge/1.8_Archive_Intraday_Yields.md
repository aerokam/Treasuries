# 1.8 Archive intraday yields

**Specifies:** [1.8 Archive intraday yields](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL2_INGESTION)  
**Implemented by:** `YieldsMonitor/scripts/archiveIntraday.js`

## Scope

Read the [Market Yields](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#market-yields) that [CNBC (E5)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e5)'s chart-bar feed states, and write [Intraday archive (S18)](./DataStores.md#s18): one immutable snapshot per symbol for the current Eastern-time day, run after the 17:00 ET cash close so the close-window bars are already settled in the feed. Unlike [Update yield history (1.7)](./1.7_Update_Yield_History.md), this is a raw audit copy of what the feed served, not a merged daily-close series — nothing here is combined with a previous day's snapshot.

- For each of the fourteen symbols [Yield history (S6)](./DataStores.md#s6) tracks:
  - Fetch CNBC's `1D` and `5D` chart-bar feeds.
  - Record, for each feed, its bar count and the Eastern time of its first and last bar, alongside every bar's own trade time and value.
  - Write the two feeds, keyed by their own time range, plus the wall-clock Eastern time the fetch itself ran, to one snapshot file for that symbol and date.

**Output:** [Intraday archive (S18)](./DataStores.md#s18), the last-resort fallback for [Assemble range data (2.1)](../YieldsMonitor/knowledge/2.1_Assemble_Range_Data.md) when CNBC's chart-bar feed itself returns nothing for a symbol.
