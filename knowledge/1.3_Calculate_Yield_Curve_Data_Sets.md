# 1.3 Calculate yield curve data sets

**Specifies:** [1.3 Calculate yield curve data sets](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_INGEST_YIELDCURVEDATA)  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/scripts/updateSpotYieldCurves.js`, through the modules the Yield Curves app also imports: `shared/src/market-quotes.js#parseTreasuryQuotes`, `shared/src/tips-yields.js#tipsYieldsFromPrices`, `shared/src/sa-yields.js#saYieldsFromTipsYields`, `shared/src/treasury-yields.js#treasuryYieldsFromPrices`, `shared/src/spot-curve.js#spotCurveFit`, `shared/src/breakeven.js#findClosestNominal`, `shared/src/breakeven.js#spotBreakevenAcrossTerms`, `shared/src/spreads.js#addTipsSpreads`, `shared/src/spreads.js#addTreasurySpreads`, `shared/src/spreads.js#yieldSpreadBps`, `shared/src/spreads.js#priceSpreadPct`, `shared/src/bond-math.js#termYears`  
**Scheduled by:** `scripts/setup-windows-tasks.ps1`, with no standalone trigger of its own: it chains on success from the FedInvest and market quotes ingestion tasks and from the `SaFactors` task, which writes [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s4)

Read [FedInvest prices (S1)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1), [Market quotes (S7)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7), [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s4) and [Bond holidays (S16)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s16); calculate the yields, spot curves, breakeven inflation and bid and ask spreads of each source; and write [Yield curves (S13)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s13), [Breakeven inflation (S14)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s14) and [Bid and ask spreads (S15)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s15):

- For each of the two sources, FedInvest and Market:
  - Calculate the ask, [SA Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) and [SAO Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yield) of every TIPS, and the yield of every nominal Treasury, per [Parse sources and calculate yields (3.1)](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md).
  - Fit three spot curves — nominal, TIPS to its quoted price, TIPS to its seasonally adjusted price — per [Fit spot yield curves (3.4)](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md), excluding STRIPS and an Unclassified security from the fitting inputs and not from the security rows below.
  - Write [Yield curves (S13)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s13):
    - The [Trade Date](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#trade-date) and [Settlement Date](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of each source, on four lines before the header, with each date in the Maturity column.
    - One row per actual security, its Coupon and Price, and its Ask/SA/SAO Yield populated where they exist.
    - On a TIPS row, the working values of its SA Yield: the Settlement Date SA Factor, the Naive Maturity SA Factor, the Maturity SA Factor, the SA Price Factor and the SA Price.
    - One row per Type (`Treasury`, `TIPS`, `BEI`) per term on a half-year grid spanning the three fits' combined valid range, each fit's `z(t)` evaluated, not refit — see [Yield curves (S13)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s13) for the grid and column detail in full.
- Match every Market TIPS to its nearest-maturity Market nominal and write [Breakeven inflation (S14)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s14), with the Trade Date and Settlement Date of the Market source on its first two lines, per [Calculate breakeven inflation (3.5)](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.5_Calculate_Breakeven_Inflation.md) — Market only, since a breakeven needs its nominal and its TIPS quoted the same way and FedInvest states only one price.
  - The `BEI` rows hold the spot breakeven inflation of [Calculate breakeven inflation (3.5)](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.5_Calculate_Breakeven_Inflation.md#across-terms): the Treasury value less the TIPS value, and less the TIPS SA value, at each term where both exist.
- Calculate the bid/ask yield and price spread of every Market security, STRIPS and an Unclassified security included, and write [Bid and ask spreads (S15)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s15), with the Trade Date and Settlement Date of the Market source on its first two lines, per [Calculate bid and ask spreads (3.6)](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md) — Market only, for the same reason: FedInvest states one price, not a separate bid and ask, so it has nothing to spread.
