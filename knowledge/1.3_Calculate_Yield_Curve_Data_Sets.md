# 1.3 Calculate yield curve data sets

**Specifies:** [1.3 Calculate yield curve data sets](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_INGEST_YIELDCURVEDATA)  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/scripts/updateYieldCurveDataSets.js`, through `shared/src/yield-curve-stores.js#buildYieldCurveStores`  
**Scheduled by:** `scripts/setup-windows-tasks.ps1`, with no standalone trigger: it chains on success from the FedInvest and market quotes ingestion tasks and from the `SaFactors` task, which writes [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s4)

Read [FedInvest prices (S1)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1), [Market quotes (S7)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s7), [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s4) and [Bond holidays (S16)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s16), and write [Yield curves (S13)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s13), [Breakeven inflation (S14)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s14) and [Bid and ask spreads (S15)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s15), in seven processes:

- [Parse sources and calculate yields (1.3.1)](./1.3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md): for each of the two sources, FedInvest and Market, calculate the ask, bid and nominal Treasury yields from price.
- [Adjust for seasonality (1.3.2)](./1.3.2_Adjust_For_Seasonality.md): calculate the [SA Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) of every TIPS.
- [Adjust for other effects (1.3.3)](./1.3.3_Adjust_For_Other_Effects.md): calculate the [SAO Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yield) of every TIPS.
- [Fit spot yield curves (1.3.4)](./1.3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md): fit three spot curves per source: nominal, TIPS to its quoted price, TIPS to its seasonally adjusted price. A STRIPS and an Unclassified security are not fitting inputs.
- [Calculate breakeven inflation (1.3.5)](./1.3.5_Calculate_Breakeven_Inflation.md): match every Market TIPS to its nearest-maturity Market nominal, and calculate the spot breakeven inflation across terms.
- [Calculate bid and ask spreads (1.3.6)](./1.3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md): calculate the yield and price spread of every Market security, STRIPS and an Unclassified security included.
- [Write yield curve data sets (1.3.7)](./1.3.7_Write_Yield_Curve_Data_Sets.md): write the three stores.
