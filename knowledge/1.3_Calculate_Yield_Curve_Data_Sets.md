---

Read [FedInvest prices (S1)](./DataStores.md#s1), [Market quotes (S7)](./DataStores.md#s7), [Ref CPI NSA and SA (S4)](./DataStores.md#s4) and [Bond holidays (S16)](./DataStores.md#s16), and write [Yield curves (S13)](./DataStores.md#s13), [Breakeven inflation (S14)](./DataStores.md#s14) and [Bid and ask spreads (S15)](./DataStores.md#s15): the same yields, spot curves, breakeven and spread figures YieldCurves computes live, from the same four stores, through the same shared calculation code.

**No portal app reads these three files.** They exist to publish that same calculation as a standalone, spreadsheet-ready resource for use outside the app entirely.

- For each of the two sources, FedInvest and Market:
  - Calculate the ask, [SA Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield) and [SAO Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yield) of every TIPS, and the yield of every nominal Treasury, per [Parse sources and calculate yields (3.1)](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md).
  - Fit three spot curves — nominal, TIPS to its quoted price, TIPS to its seasonally adjusted price — per [Fit spot yield curves (3.4)](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md), excluding STRIPS and an Unclassified security from the fitting inputs (not from the security rows below) the same way the app does.
  - Write [Yield curves (S13)](./DataStores.md#s13):
    - One row per actual security, its Ask/SA/SAO Yield populated where they exist.
    - One row per Type (`Treasury`, `TIPS`, `BEI`) per term on a half-year grid spanning the three fits' combined valid range, each fit's own `z(t)` evaluated rather than refit — see [Yield curves (S13)](./DataStores.md#s13) for the grid and column detail in full.
- Match every Market TIPS to its nearest-maturity Market nominal and write [Breakeven inflation (S14)](./DataStores.md#s14), per [Calculate breakeven inflation (3.5)](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.5_Calculate_Breakeven_Inflation.md) — Market only, since a breakeven needs its nominal and its TIPS quoted the same way and FedInvest states only one price.
  - Spot breakeven inflation is not separately persisted: it is fully re-derivable from [Yield curves (S13)](./DataStores.md#s13)'s own Treasury and TIPS grid rows, so storing it again would be a duplicate, not a cross-check.
- Calculate the bid/ask yield and price spread of every Market TIPS and nominal Treasury and write [Bid and ask spreads (S15)](./DataStores.md#s15), per [Calculate bid and ask spreads (3.6)](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md) — Market only, for the same reason: FedInvest states one price, not a separate bid and ask, so it has nothing to spread.

**Output:** [Yield curves (S13)](./DataStores.md#s13), [Breakeven inflation (S14)](./DataStores.md#s14) and [Bid and ask spreads (S15)](./DataStores.md#s15) — not read by any portal process; a standalone resource published for spreadsheet and other external use.
