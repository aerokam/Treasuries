# 1.1 Download FedInvest prices

**Specifies:** [1.1 Download FedInvest prices](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_INGEST_FEDINVEST) and the three processes it decomposes into  
**Implemented by:** `scripts/getYieldsFedInvest.js`  
**Reference:** [FedInvest Pricing Logic](../YieldCurves/knowledge/FedInvest_Pricing_Logic.md), [FedInvest Treasury Pricing Methodology](../YieldCurves/knowledge/FedInvest_Treasury_Pricing_Methodology.md)

- [DATA_DICTIONARY.md](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md) — [E1](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e1), [S1](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1), [Settlement Date](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), [Download Date](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date), [Bond Holiday](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), [Clean Price](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price), [Coupon Rate](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#coupon-rate), [Maturity Date](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date), [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi), [Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield), and the data flows [Daily Mid-Market Prices](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#daily-mid-market-prices), [TIPS Prices](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-prices) and [Treasury Prices](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices)
- [DataStores.md](./DataStores.md) — [S1](./DataStores.md#s1), [S2](./DataStores.md#s2), and the bond holidays file
- [Data_Pipeline.md](./Data_Pipeline.md) — the schedule and retries of the task that executes this process

## Scope

Reads the FedInvest daily price list ([E1](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e1)), [S2](./DataStores.md#s2) and the bond holidays, and writes [S1](./DataStores.md#s1): the [Settlement Date](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of the prices, then one line for each TIPS and each market-based bill, note and bond, with one [Clean Price](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price) and the [Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) of that price at the settlement date. A TIPS takes its coupon rate, maturity date and dated date Ref CPI from S2, and a TIPS that S2 does not hold is dropped. Every other security takes its coupon rate and maturity date from E1. S1 is not written on a [Bond Holiday](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday), or when the price list does not state prices for the run date.

**A past trading day.** `scripts/getFedInvestPricesForDate.js` executes 1.1.2 and 1.1.3 for the TIPS alone, on the price list FedInvest publishes for a past trading day, and writes a local file in the S1 composition rather than S1. Its settlement date is the date asked for, and the file is written only when the page states that date and its price table has rows.

## Processes

<a id="determine-settlement-date"></a>
### 1.1.1 Determine settlement date

**Implemented by:** `scripts/getYieldsFedInvest.js#determineSettlementDate`

Produces the [Settlement Date](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of the prices in [S1](./DataStores.md#s1): the date on the `Prices For:` line of the [E1](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e1) page, taken as the settlement date itself (T+0). Market quotes settle one bond trading day after their [Download Date](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date) (T+1, [3.1.6](../YieldCurves/knowledge/3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md#determine-settlement-date)).

**T+0 was determined empirically.** Yields calculated from FedInvest prices at T+0 come close to the yields of broker quotes settling at T+1. The developer established this in a spreadsheet comparison. No record of that measurement is held in this repository.

No settlement date is produced, and S1 is not written, in three cases:

- The run date, in Eastern time, is a [Bond Holiday](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-holiday). The run ends without a retry.
- The page has no `Prices For:` line. The run ends without a retry.
- The page states a date other than the run date. The run ends as a failure, and the task retries ([Data_Pipeline.md](./Data_Pipeline.md) §2.0).

**Output:** the [Settlement Date](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date), to [1.1.3](#calculate-yields).

<a id="select-tips-and-treasury-prices"></a>
### 1.1.2 Select TIPS and Treasury prices

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/fedinvest-prices.js#parseFedInvestPriceRows`, `shared/src/fedinvest-prices.js#selectPricedSecurity`

Reads the rows of the [E1](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#e1) price table and [S2](./DataStores.md#s2), and produces the [TIPS Prices](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-prices) and the [Treasury Prices](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices):

- It keeps a row whose `SECURITY TYPE` is `TIPS`, `MARKET BASED BILL`, `MARKET BASED NOTE` or `MARKET BASED BOND`, and drops every other.
- It takes the `BUY` price as the [Clean Price](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price), the `SELL` price where `BUY` is empty or zero, and the `END OF DAY` price where both are. A row with none of the three has no price.
- A TIPS takes the [Coupon Rate](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#coupon-rate), the [Maturity Date](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#maturity-date) and the dated date [Ref CPI](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi) that S2 holds for its CUSIP. A TIPS that S2 does not hold is dropped.
- Every other security takes the `RATE` and the `MATURITY DATE` that E1 states.

The three prices, and how Treasury derives them, are described in [FedInvest Pricing Logic](../YieldCurves/knowledge/FedInvest_Pricing_Logic.md).

**Output:** the [TIPS Prices](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#tips-prices) and the [Treasury Prices](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-prices), to [1.1.3](#calculate-yields).

<a id="calculate-yields"></a>
### 1.1.3 Calculate yields

**Implemented by:** `shared/src/fedinvest-prices.js#yieldForSecurity`, `shared/src/bond-math.js#yieldFromPrice`, `shared/src/fedinvest-prices.js#serializeS1`

Calculates the [Yield](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#yield) of each TIPS and each Treasury from its [Clean Price](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price) at the [Settlement Date](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) from [1.1.1](#determine-settlement-date), and writes [S1](./DataStores.md#s1) in its [composition](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s1): the settlement date on the first line, then a header, then one line per security. A security with no price is written with no yield.

**Output:** [S1](./DataStores.md#s1).
