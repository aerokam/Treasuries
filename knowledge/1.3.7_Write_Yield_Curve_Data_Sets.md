# 1.3.7 Write yield curve data sets

**Specifies:** [1.3.7 Write yield curve data sets](/knowledge/DFD_LEVEL3_INGEST_YIELDCURVEDATA)  
**Implemented by:** `YieldCurves/scripts/updateYieldCurveDataSets.js`, `shared/src/yield-curve-stores.js#buildYieldCurvesCsv`, `shared/src/yield-curve-stores.js#buildBreakevenCsv`, `shared/src/yield-curve-stores.js#buildSpreadsCsv`, `shared/src/yield-curve-stores.js#spotRowsFromFits`  

Read the [Treasury Yields](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields), the [SAO Yields](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yields), the [Spot Yield Curves](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#spot-yield-curves), the [Breakeven Inflation](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#breakeven-inflation), the [Bid and Ask Spreads](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bid-and-ask-spreads), the [Settlement Date](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of each source and the [Download Date](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date), and write [Yield curves (S13)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s13), [Breakeven inflation (S14)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s14) and [Bid and ask spreads (S15)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s15):

- Write [Yield curves (S13)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s13):
  - The [Trade Date](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#trade-date) and [Settlement Date](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#settlement-date) of each source, on four lines before the header, with each date in the Maturity column.
  - One row per actual security, its Coupon and Price, and its Ask/SA/SAO Yield populated where they exist.
  - On a TIPS row, the working values of its SA Yield: the Settlement Date SA Factor, the Naive Maturity SA Factor, the Maturity SA Factor, the SA Price Factor and the SA Price.
  - One spot row per Type (`Treasury`, `TIPS`, `BEI`) per half-year term across the three fits' combined valid range, each fit's `z(t)` evaluated, not refit. See [Yield curves (S13)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s13) for the spot rows and columns in full.
  - The `BEI` rows hold the spot breakeven inflation of [Calculate breakeven inflation (1.3.5)](./1.3.5_Calculate_Breakeven_Inflation.md#across-terms): the Treasury value less the TIPS value, and less the TIPS SA value, at each term where both exist.
- Write [Breakeven inflation (S14)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s14), with the Trade Date and Settlement Date of the Market source on its first two lines. It is Market only, since a breakeven needs its nominal and its TIPS quoted the same way and FedInvest states only one price.
- Write [Bid and ask spreads (S15)](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#s15), with the Trade Date and Settlement Date of the Market source on its first two lines. It is Market only: FedInvest states one price, not a separate bid and ask, so it has nothing to spread.

**Input:** the [Treasury Yields](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#treasury-yields) and the [Download Date](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#download-date), from [Parse sources and calculate yields (1.3.1)](./1.3.1_Parse_Sources_And_Calculate_Yields.md); the [SAO Yields](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sao-yields), from [Adjust for other effects (1.3.3)](./1.3.3_Adjust_For_Other_Effects.md); the [Spot Yield Curves](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#spot-yield-curves), from [Fit spot yield curves (1.3.4)](./1.3.4_Fit_Spot_Yield_Curves.md); the [Breakeven Inflation](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#breakeven-inflation), from [Calculate breakeven inflation (1.3.5)](./1.3.5_Calculate_Breakeven_Inflation.md); the [Bid and Ask Spreads](./DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bid-and-ask-spreads), from [Calculate bid and ask spreads (1.3.6)](./1.3.6_Calculate_Bid_And_Ask_Spreads.md).
